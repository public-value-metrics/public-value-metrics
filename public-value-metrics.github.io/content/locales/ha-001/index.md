# Ma'aunin Ƙimar Jama'a

Cikakken gabatarwa ga lissafi, misalai da hujjoji kan ƙimar jama'a, wanda aka rubuta ga injiniyoyin manhaja da ke gina wa ƙungiyoyin gwamnati da na ɓangaren zamantakewa a faɗin duniya. Kowane fayil yana magana kan ma'auni ɗaya ko ra'ayi ɗaya: ma'ana, dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci, lissafi, misali da aka yi aiki da shi, alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja, kura-kurai da za a guje wa, da majiyoyi.

Sabo ne a nan? Fara da [ƙimar jama'a](locales/ha-001/topics/ƙimar-jamaa/), [ƙimar da aka samu daidai da kuɗi](locales/ha-001/topics/ƙimar-da-aka-samu-daidai-da-kuɗi/), da [ka'idar sauyi](locales/ha-001/topics/kaidar-sauyi/) — ra'ayoyi uku da duk sauran ke tsaye a kansu.

## Tushen ƙimar jama'a

- [Ƙimar Jama'a (Public Value)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/public-value/) — Triangle na dabara na Mark Moore: halacci, manufa, da ƙarfin aiki a matsayin gwajin ƙimar jama'a
- [Ƙimar da Aka Samu Daidai da Kuɗi (VFM)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/value-for-money/) — Tattali, inganci, tasiri — da E na huɗu da ake jayayya a kai, adalci
- [Kuɗin Damar Da Aka Rasa a Kashe Kuɗin Jama'a (Opportunity Cost)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/opportunity-cost-in-public-spending/) — Kowane fam da ma'aikata ta kashe fam ne da ba a kashe a kan shiri na gaba mafi kyau
- [Kason Rangwame na Zamantakewa (Social Discount Rate)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/social-discount-rate/) — Kason rangwame mai raguwa na Green Book, da dalilin da ya sa 3.5% ya zama tambayar siyasa mai zafi
- [Auna Rabon Fa'ida bisa Matsayin Mutane](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/distributional-weighting/) — Dalilin da ya sa fam yake da ƙima fiye da ga gida mai talauci — da yadda Green Book ke ba shi farashi
- [Ƙarin Tasiri da Deadweight](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/additionality-and-deadweight/) — Shin da ma zai faru? Tambayar da ke rage girman yawancin iƙirarin tasiri
- [Ƙauratarwa da Danganta Tasiri](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/displacement-and-attribution/) — Lokacin da nasararka asarar wani ce, da lokacin da ba duk naka ba ne ka yi iƙirari
- [Nazarin Counterfactual (Yanayin Rashin Shiga Tsakani)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/counterfactual-analysis/) — Ba tare da counterfactual ba, babu shaida — gina duniyar da ba ta da shisshigi

## Tantance tattalin arziki a gwamnati

- [Kimanta Shawarwari ta Green Book (Tsarin Shari'o'i Biyar)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/green-book-appraisal/) — Tsarin shari'o'i biyar na HM Treasury: shari'ar dabara, ta tattalin arziki, ta kasuwanci, ta kuɗi, da ta gudanarwa
- [Nazarin Kuɗi da Amfani na Zamantakewa (SCBA)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/social-cost-benefit-analysis/) — Ƙimanta komai da kuɗi, har da abin da kasuwanni ba su taɓa ba da farashi ba
- [Nazarin Ingancin Kuɗi a Gwamnati](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cost-effectiveness-analysis-in-government/) — Kuɗin kowace raka'ar sakamako, lokacin da ba za a iya mayar da sakamako zuwa kuɗi da gaskiya ba
- [Nazarin Yanke Shawara Bisa Ma'auni Da Yawa (MCDA)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/multi-criteria-decision-analysis/) — Ba da maki ga zaɓuɓɓuka bisa ma'auni masu nauyi lokacin da ma'auni ɗaya zai ɓoye musayar
- [Ƙimantawar Jin Daɗin Rayuwa (WELLBY)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/wellbeing-valuation/) — WELLBYs da gyaran jin daɗi: ba da farashin manufa a raka'un gamsuwa da rayuwa
- [Ƙimantawa ta Fifikon da Aka Bayyana (Stated Preference)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/stated-preference-valuation/) — Tambayar mutane nawa za su biya — contingent valuation da matsalar sahihancinsa
- [Ƙididdige Ƙima Ta Fifikon da Aka Bayyana a Aikace (Revealed Preference Valuation)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/revealed-preference-valuation/) — Fahimtar ƙima daga abin da mutane suke yi da gaske, daga hedonic pricing zuwa hanyoyin kuɗin tafiya
- [Farashin Inuwa (Shadow Pricing)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/shadow-pricing/) — Ba da farashi ga abin da ba shi da farashin kasuwa: carbon, lokaci, da kayan da ba a ciniki da su

## Ƙimar zamantakewa da auna tasiri

- [Ribar Zamantakewa daga Zuba Jari (SROI)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/social-return-on-investment/) — Rabon SROI, da horon da ke hana shi zama lambar tallace-tallace
- [Ka'idar Sauyi (Theory of Change)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/theory-of-change/) — Zana sarkar dalili da sakamako daga aiki zuwa sakamakon dogon lokaci kafin ka auna komai
- [Samfurin Dabaru na Shiri (Logic Model)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/logic-model/) — Shigarwa, ayyuka, abin da aka samar, sakamako, tasiri — ƙashin bayan alhakin shiri
- [Sakamako da Fitarwa: Bambancin Outcomes da Outputs](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/outcomes-vs-outputs/) — Dalilin da ya sa "mutane 500 sun halarta" ba iƙirari ɗaya ba ne da "rayuwar mutane 500 ta inganta"
- [Dokar Ƙimar Zamantakewa (Social Value Act)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/social-value-act/) — Wajibin Birtaniya na 2012 na yin la'akari da ƙimar zamantakewa a sayen gwamnati
- [Rumbun Bayanan Kuɗin Raka'a (Unit Cost Databases)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/unit-cost-databases/) — Social Value Bank na HACT da Global Value Exchange: wakilai da aka riga aka ba su farashi domin kowa kada ya fara daga sifili
- [Hanyoyin Kimanta Tasiri](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/impact-evaluation-methods/) — RCTs, difference-in-differences, da propensity score matching ga manufofin da ba za ka iya gwadawa a dakin gwaji ba
- [Kimanta Tasiri da Kimanta Tsari](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/impact-evaluation-vs-process-evaluation/) — Shin ya yi aiki, ko ya faru yadda aka tsara — tambayoyi biyu daban, hanyoyi biyu daban

## Ma'aunin aiki da isarwa a gwamnati

- [Manyan Alamomin Aiki na Ɓangaren Jama'a (Public Sector KPIs)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/public-sector-kpis/) — Zaɓar alamomin da ke tsira daga dokar Goodhart
- [Katin Kimanta Ƙimar Jama'a (Public Value Scorecard)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/public-value-scorecard/) — Daidaita balanced scorecard na Kaplan da Norton ga manufa da ba riba ba ce
- [Alhaki Bisa Sakamako (Outcomes-Based Accountability)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/outcomes-based-accountability/) — "Results-Based Accountability" na Mark Friedman: sakamakon jama'a da alhakin aiki
- [Biyan Kuɗi Bisa Sakamako da Bondin Tasirin Zamantakewa (Payment by Results and Social Impact Bonds)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/payment-by-results-and-social-impact-bonds/) — Biyan kuɗi don sakamako, ba aiki ba — da dalilin da ya sa tsarin ƙarfafawa shi ne ainihin wasan
- [Ƙarfin Aikin Hidimomin Jama'a (Public Service Productivity)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/public-service-productivity/) — Auna ƙaruwar abin da aka samar a hidimomi marasa farashin kasuwa, daga hanyar ONS zuwa gaba
- [Ma'aunin Gamsuwar Ɗan Ƙasa](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/citizen-satisfaction-metrics/) — Net satisfaction, binciken amana, da gibin da ke tsakanin ingancin sabis da ƙimar da ake ganin ta
- [Ƙa'idodin Hidima da Ma'aunan Mu'amala (Service Standards and Transaction Metrics)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/service-standards-and-transaction-metrics/) — Ƙa'idodin hidima irin na GOV.UK: yawan kammalawa, kuɗin kowace ma'amala, karɓar dijital
- [Ma'aunin Amana da Halaccin Mulki](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/trust-and-legitimacy-metrics/) — Dalilin da ya sa halacci ɗaya ne daga gwaje-gwajen Moore uku, da yadda gwamnatoci ke ƙoƙarin auna shi

## Ma'aunin ɓangaren zamantakewa da bayar da agaji

- [Kuɗin Kowane Sakamako](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cost-per-outcome/) — Tattalin arzikin raka'a na ƙungiyar agaji: kuɗin kowane mutum da aka taimaka masa da gaske, ba wanda aka taɓa kawai ba
- [Kuɗin Kowane Mai Cin Gajiya](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cost-per-beneficiary/) — Ɗan'uwan kuɗi-a-kowane-sakamako mafi sauri kuma mafi ɓarkatsa, da dalilin da ya sa masu ba da tallafi har yanzu suke nema
- [Ƙimar Haɗaka (Blended Value)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/blended-value/) — Riba ta kuɗi, zamantakewa, da muhalli a matsayin layin ƙarshe ɗaya wanda ba a iya rarrabawa
- [Ingancin Kuɗi na Bayarwa Mai Tasiri (Effective Altruism)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/effective-altruism-cost-effectiveness/) — Tunanin kuɗi-a-kowane-rai-da-aka-ceto irin na GiveWell, da asalinsa mai siffar QALY
- [Rabon Kuɗin Gudanarwa na Ƙungiyar Agaji](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/charity-overhead-ratio/) — Dalilin da ya sa tambayar "nawa ke zuwa gudanarwa" tambaya ce marar kyau, kuma masu ba da tallafi har yanzu suna yin ta
- [Dawowar Jari ga Mai Ba da Gudummawa](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/donor-return-on-investment/) — Abin da mai ba da gudummawa ke samu da gaske, da dalilin da ya sa ba ROI na ƙungiyar agaji ba ne
- [Rahoton Sakamakon Tallafi (IRIS+)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/grant-outcomes-reporting/) — Ma'aunin IRIS+ da daidaita abin da masu ba da tallafi ke nema daga masu karɓa su bayar da rahoto
- [Ƙimar Lokacin Masu Aikin Sa Kai](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/volunteer-time-value/) — Ba da farashin aikin da ba a biya ba ba tare da shafe shi ko ƙara masa girma ba

## Ƙimar jama'a a gwamnatin dijital

- [Ma'aunin Hidimar Dijital](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/digital-service-standard/) — Ƙa'idodin ƙira na GOV.UK da tantance sabis a matsayin ƙofar ƙimar jama'a
- [Kuɗin Kowace Ma'amala](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cost-per-transaction/) — Babban tattalin arzikin hanyar dijital, da dabarar lissafi mafi yawa game da shi
- [Tanadin Sauya Hanyar Hidima (Channel Shift)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/channel-shift-savings/) — Abin da ke faruwa da gaske ga layin waya da kantin hidima lokacin da sabis ya koma yanar gizo
- [Haɗa Kowa da Dijital](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/digital-inclusion/) — Taimakon dijital, gibin dijital, da ƙimar da ake rasawa lokacin da "dijital ta asali" ke ware mutane
- [Gwamnati a Matsayin Dandamali (GaaP)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/government-as-a-platform/) — Abubuwan da ake rabawa a matsayin kayayyakin more rayuwa na jama'a: GOV.UK Notify, Pay, da tattalin arzikin sake amfani
- [Ƙimar Buɗaɗɗen Bayanai (Open Data Value)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/open-data-value/) — Ƙimanta bayanan da ba su da farashi, daga ƙiyasin McKinsey zuwa nazarin shari'o'i na kowane saitin bayanai
- [Ƙimar Tsaron Yanar Gizo a Ɓangaren Jama'a (Public Sector Cybersecurity Value)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/public-sector-cybersecurity-value/) — Ba da farashin rage haɗari ga bayanan 'yan ƙasa da keta tsaro zai fallasa
- [Ƙimar AI a Gwamnati](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/ai-in-government-value/) — Tantance karɓar AI a hidimomin jama'a bisa ma'aunin ƙimar da aka samu daidai da kuɗi ɗaya da sauran komai

## Jin daɗi, adalci, da alamomin zamantakewa

- [Madadin GDP](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/gdp-alternatives/) — Genuine Progress Indicator, Gross National Happiness, da sanannun makafin GDP
- [Ma'aunin Ci Gaban Ɗan Adam (HDI)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/human-development-index/) — Tsawon rayuwa, ilimi, da kuɗin shiga a haɗe a lamba ɗaya — da abin da yake lallaɓawa
- [Ma'aunin Talauci Mai Fuskoki Da Yawa (Multidimensional Poverty Index)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/multidimensional-poverty-index/) — Ƙirga rashi, ba kuɗin shiga kaɗai ba, a lafiya, ilimi, da matsayin rayuwa
- [Shekarun Rayuwa da Aka Daidaita da Jin Daɗi (WELLBY)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/wellbeing-adjusted-life-years/) — WELLBY: raka'a mai siffar QALY don manufofin da kuɗinsu gamsuwa da rayuwa ne, ba lafiya ba
- [Ma'aunin Talauci Mai Fuskoki Da Dama na Ingila (IMD)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/index-of-multiple-deprivation/) — Jerin talauci na ƙananan yankuna na Ingila, da yadda yake tura kuɗi da hankali
- [Ma'aunin Jarin Zamantakewa (Social Capital)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/social-capital-metrics/) — Amana, hanyoyin sadarwa, da shiga harkokin jama'a a matsayin kadarar tattalin arziki da ba ta da shigarwa a ledger
- [Lissafin Jarin Halitta (Natural Capital Accounting)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/natural-capital-accounting/) — Sanya muhalli a takardar ma'auni, daga 25 Year Environment Plan na Birtaniya zuwa gaba
- [Adalci tsakanin Tsararraki da Rangwamen Dorewa](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/intergenerational-equity-and-sustainability-discounting/) — Dalilin da ya sa rage ƙimar nesa nan gaba a kowane kason tabbatacce zai iya share ta daga nazarin

## Injiniyancin manhaja da isarwa ta dijital

- [Kuɗin Jinkiri a Shirye-shiryen Jama'a (CoD)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cost-of-delay-in-public-programmes/) — £/mako ko WELLBYs/mako na rashin isarwa — ma'aunin gada na ƙoli, da aka shigo da shi daga tattalin arzikin lafiya
- [Ma'aunin DORA don Ƙimar Jama'a](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/dora-metrics-for-public-value/) — Ma'aunin aikin isarwa, an fassara su zuwa sharuɗɗan alhakin ɓangaren jama'a
- [Ma'aunin Kwarara a Isar da Aikin Gwamnati](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/flow-metrics-in-government-delivery/) — Little's Law, WIP, da ingancin kwarara da aka yi amfani da su a jerin shari'o'i maimakon allon sprint
- [Bashin Fasaha a Matsayin Zaizayar Ƙimar Jama'a](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/technical-debt-as-public-value-erosion/) — Ainihin bashi da ruwa, da tattalin arzikin cuta mai daɗewa na tsohon tsarin jama'a
- [Jimillar Kuɗin Mallaka (TCO) a IT na Gwamnati](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/total-cost-of-ownership-in-government-it/) — Kulawa tana ɗaukar 50–80% na lissafin; kuskuren farashin saye na ba-tunani a IT na jama'a
- [Gina ko Saya a Gwamnati](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/build-vs-buy-in-government/) — Kwatancen da aka daidaita da haɗari tare da farashin jinkiri, ga ɓangaren da galibi yake saye
- [Cimma Fa'idodi (Benefits Realization)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/benefits-realization/) — Duba ko amfanin da aka yi hasashe a shari'ar kasuwanci ya samu da gaske, bayan fara aiki
- [Ƙaruwar Aiki ta AI a Fannin Gwamnati](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/ai-productivity-in-the-public-sector/) — Abin da tushen shaidar ƙaruwar aikin masu haɓaka da AI yake nufi ga ƙungiyoyin injiniya na ɓangaren jama'a
