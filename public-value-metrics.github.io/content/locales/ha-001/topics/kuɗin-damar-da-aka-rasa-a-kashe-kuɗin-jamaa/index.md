# Kuɗin Damar Da Aka Rasa a Kashe Kuɗin Jama'a (Opportunity Cost)

Kuɗin damar da aka rasa (opportunity cost) shi ne ƙimar mafi kyawun madadin da aka bari lokacin da hukumar jama'a ta ɗauki alƙawarin kuɗi, lokacin ma'aikata, ko ƙarfin siyasa ga zaɓi ɗaya maimakon wani. A ma'aikata mai kasafin kuɗi ƙayyadadde, kowane fam da aka kashe a shiri ɗaya fam ne da ba za a iya kashewa a shiri na gaba mafi kyau ba — ainihin kuɗin shawara ba abin da take kashewa ba ne, sai abin da take kora.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Kasafin kuɗin jama'a an iyakance shi da kuɗi a cikin lokacin nazarin kashe kuɗi (spending review), don haka — ba kamar kamfani mai zaman kansa da ke girma ba — ma'aikatar gwamnati ba za ta iya kawai "samo ƙarin kuɗi" don kyakkyawan tunani ba; ba da kuɗi gare shi yana nufin cire wa wani abu. Green Book na HM Treasury yana ɗaukar wannan a matsayin ginshiƙi: ana buƙatar kowane kimantawa ya kwatanta wani mataki da tushen "mafi ƙaranci" (do minimum) *da kuma* da madadin amfani na gaske na albarkatu iri ɗaya, daidai saboda ainihin tambayar da ƙungiyar kashe kuɗi ta Treasury ke yi ba ta taɓa kasancewa "shin wannan yana da kyau?" ba, sai "shin wannan ya fi abin da wannan kuɗin zai iya saya?" Babban ƙa'idar kimantawa ta Green Book — cewa a tura albarkatun jama'a ga matakin da ke da mafi girman ƙimar zamantakewa ta net ga kowane fam — kuɗin damar da aka rasa ne da aka bayyana a matsayin manufa.

Yana da sauƙin faɗa amma wuyar aiwatarwa domin "madadin na gaba mafi kyau" da wuya a ganshi a cikin takardar shaidar kasuwanci ɗaya. Ana kwatanta shirin tallafi na £2 miliyan don aikin matasa, a takardar shaidar kasuwanci, da rashin yin komai — amma abin kwatance na gaskiya shi ne matakin aikin matasa na gaba mafi kyau, ko ma mafi kyawun amfani da £2 miliyan a ko'ina cikin jerin shirye-shirye, har da kashe kuɗin da ba na aiki ba. Littafin Magenta (HM Treasury, 2020) ya yi gargaɗi a fili cewa kimantawar da ke kwatanta "tare da shiga tsakani" da "ba tare da shiga tsakani ba" suna rage matakin da dole ne shiga tsakani ya cimma, saboda "ba tare da wannan shiga tsakani ba" ba daidai yake da "babu komai kwata-kwata" ba — kuɗin da aka saki yana ba da kuɗin wani abu dabam.

## Lissafi

```
Opportunity cost of choosing A = value of the best forgone alternative B

Net public value of A = value(A) − value(B), not value(A) − 0
```

Babu dabara ta duniya baki ɗaya domin madadin da aka bari ya dogara da yanayi, amma tsarin yana yaɗuwa: a gano madadin amfani na gaske na gaba mafi kyau na layin kasafin kuɗi ɗaya (ba "babu komai" na kyakkyawan zato ba), a ƙididdige shi a kan tushe ɗaya (a mayar da shi kuɗi inda zai yiwu, bisa [nazarin kuɗi da alfanu na zamantakewa (social cost-benefit analysis)](../nazarin-kuɗi-da-amfani-na-zamantakewa/)), sannan a rage.

## Misali aiki

**Layin kasafin kuɗin ma'aikata**: asusun sauya fasaha ta dijital na £5 miliyan zai iya ba da kuɗin shawara ɗaya daga cikin biyu a wannan shekarar kuɗi.

- *Zaɓi A*: sabon dandamalin sarrafa shari'o'i, alfanun da aka mayar kuɗi £7.2 miliyan a shekaru 5 (tanadin inganci da saurin warware shari'o'i).
- *Zaɓi B*: hidimar tabbatar da shaida da ma'aikatu uku za su raba, alfanun da aka mayar kuɗi £6.4 miliyan a shekaru 5.

Takardar shaidar kasuwanci mara zurfi ta A tana kwatanta £7.2 miliyan na alfanu da £5 miliyan na kuɗi kuma tana ba da rahoton ma'aunin alfanu-da-kuɗi 1.44:1 — da alama mai ƙarfi. Amma saboda A da B suna gogayya a kan £5 miliyan ɗaya, kuɗin damar da aka rasa na zaɓar A shi ne alfanun £6.4 miliyan na B da aka bari. Ƙarar *net* ta A a kan madadin na gaske £7.2m − £6.4m = £0.8 miliyan ne kawai, ba cikakken £7.2 miliyan na kanun labarai ba. Idan zaɓi na uku, C, ya bayar da alfanun £7.5 miliyan don £5 miliyan ɗaya, ba da kuɗi ga A maimakon C zai lalata £0.3 miliyan na ƙimar jama'a ko da takardar shaidar kasuwanci ta A ita kaɗai tana da cikakken dalili.

**Lokacin ma'aikatan ƙaramar hukuma**: ƙungiyar bayanai ta mutane uku ta majalisa za ta iya gina ko dai allon bayanai na jerin jiran gidaje (an ƙiyasta zai tanadi sa'o'in jami'i 400 a shekara, a £28 a sa'a = £11,200 a shekara) ko kayan aikin tantance zamba a tallafi (an ƙiyasta zai hana £85,000 a shekara na biyan kuɗi marasa daidai). Gina allon bayanai yana da kuɗin damar da aka rasa na £85,000 a shekara da aka bari, ba kawai kuɗin albashin ƙungiyar bayanai ba — ainihin kuɗin ginin cikin gida "kyauta" shi ne alfanu mafi girma sosai da ƙungiyar za ta iya samarwa a wani wuri.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Ƙarfin injiniya a cikin hukumar jama'a kasafin kuɗi ne da aka iyakance — ƙarfin sprint, ba fam ba — kuma ƙa'ida ɗaya tana aiki kai tsaye:

- A koyaushe a faɗi abin kwatance: takardar shaidar kasuwanci ta wani fasali ya kamata ta bayyana abin da ƙungiya ɗaya za ta iya kaiwa a makonnin aiki ɗaya, ba kawai ribar kanta ba.
- A ɗauki "muna da ƙarfin injiniya da ya rage" a matsayin farkon nazarin kuɗin damar da aka rasa, ba ƙarshensa ba — ragowar ƙarfi har yanzu yana da mafi kyawun madadin amfani, ko da wannan amfani shi ne biyan bashin fasaha (duba [bashin fasaha a matsayin zaizayar ƙimar jama'a (technical debt as public value erosion)](../bashin-fasaha-a-matsayin-zaizayar-ƙimar-jamaa/)).
- A haɗa wannan kai tsaye da [ƙimar kuɗi (value for money)](../ƙimar-da-aka-samu-daidai-da-kuɗi/): gwajin "tattalin arziki" na VFM ba shi da ma'ana ba tare da abin kwatancen kuɗin damar da aka rasa na gaskiya ba, da kuma [kuɗin jinkiri a shirye-shiryen jama'a (cost of delay in public programmes)](../kuɗin-jinkiri-a-shirye-shiryen-jamaa/), wanda ke ƙididdige farashin lokaci na dabarar madadin da aka bari iri ɗaya.

## Tarkuna

- **Kwatanta da "babu komai" maimakon madadin na gaba mafi kyau.** Green Book yana buƙatar tushen "mafi ƙaranci" daidai saboda ainihin kuɗin damar da aka rasa da wuya ya zama sifili; takardar shaidar kasuwanci da ta wuce matakin "babu komai" kawai ba ta nuna cewa ta fi madadin na gaske ba.
- **Watsi da gogayyar tsakanin ma'aikatu a kan tulu ɗaya.** Layukan kasafin kuɗi da suke kama da an keɓe su a cikin darakta ɗaya sau da yawa suna gogayya a matakin sama (nazarin kashe kuɗi, shirin babban jari) inda ainihin kuɗin damar da aka rasa ke bayyana.
- **Zaton cewa lokacin ma'aikata da aka saki ba shi da wata ƙima kuma.** Lokacin da aka "tanada" yana ƙirƙirar ƙima ne kawai idan aka mayar da shi zuwa wani abu mai daraja; idan madadin amfani bai wanzu ba, tanadin na kwatance ne kawai.
