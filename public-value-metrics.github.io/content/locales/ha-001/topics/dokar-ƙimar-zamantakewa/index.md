# Dokar Ƙimar Zamantakewa (Social Value Act)

Dokar Public Services (Social Value) Act 2012 wajibi ne na doka a Birtaniya da ke buƙatar hukumomin gwamnati a Ingila da Wales su yi la'akari da yadda abin da ake saya zai iya inganta jin daɗin tattalin arziki, zamantakewa, da muhalli na yankin da abin ya shafa, kuma su yi la'akari da tuntuɓa a kai, kafin fara tsarin sayen kwangilolin ayyukan jama'a. Ta fara aiki a Janairu 2013 a matsayin wajibi mai sauƙi na "a yi la'akari", kuma Procurement Policy Note (PPN) 06/20 ta ƙarfafa ta sosai a Janairu 2021, wadda ke buƙatar kwangilolin gwamnatin tsakiya su tantance ƙimar zamantakewa a fili — ba kawai su yi la'akari ba — tare da mafi ƙarancin nauyi a ƙa'idodin bayar da kwangila.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Kafin PPN 06/20, "yin la'akari" da ƙimar zamantakewa za a iya cika shi ta mai ba da kwangila ya lura cewa ya yi tunani a kai, ba tare da wata buƙata cewa ya shafi shawarar bayar da kwangila ba — wajibi mai sauƙin cikawa a takarda kuma a yi biris da shi a aikace. PPN 06/20 ta rufe wannan gibi ga sayen gwamnatin tsakiya: ta tilasta a ba da maki ga ƙimar zamantakewa a tantance tayi, a tsara shi kewaye da jigogi biyar na fifikon ƙasa — farfaɗowa daga COVID-19, magance rashin daidaiton tattalin arziki, yaƙi da sauyin yanayi, daidaiton dama, da jin daɗi — kuma yawanci ana aunawa ta tsarin National TOMs (Themes, Outcomes, Measures) wanda Social Value Portal ke kula da shi. Ga injiniyan manhaja da ke gina kayan aikin saye, kula da kwangila, ko tallafin tayi na ɓangaren jama'a, wannan shi ne tushen doka da abokin cinikinka ya wajaba ya gina a kai, ba abin da ake so kawai ba.

## Lissafi

Ƙimar zamantakewa batu ne mai siffar tsari; "lissafinsa" shi ne tsarin bayar da maki da yawancin hukumomi ke amfani da shi:

```
Total tender score = Price/cost weighting + Quality weighting + Social value weighting

PPN 06/20 (central government): social value weighting ≥ 10% of total score

Social value themes (PPN 06/20):
 1. COVID-19 recovery
 2. Tackling economic inequality
 3. Fighting climate change
 4. Equal opportunity
 5. Wellbeing
```

Masu tayi yawanci suna ƙimanta alƙawuransu a kan waɗannan jigogi ta amfani da [rumbun bayanan kuɗin raka'a](../rumbun-bayanan-kuɗin-rakaa/), kuma dabarar ƙimantawa ɗaya da ake amfani da ita a [ribar zamantakewa daga zuba jari](../ribar-zamantakewa-daga-zuba-jari/) tana aiki: alƙawari ya kamata ya kasance mai shaida, wanda za a danganta shi da kwangilar, kuma ba a ƙirga shi sau biyu da wani tallafin ba.

## Misali aiki

**Kwangilar IT ta ƙaramar hukuma**: kwangila ta £2 miliyan, shekaru 3, ana ba ta maki 60% inganci, 30% farashi, 10% ƙimar zamantakewa. Mai tayi A ya yi alƙawarin koyon sana'a 2, £150,000 na kashe kuɗi a ƙananan kwangilolin gida, da awanni 200 na koyar da ƙwarewar dijital kyauta ga makarantar yankin, an ƙimanta ta amfani da wakilai daga rumbun kuɗin raka'a a jimillar £90,000 na ƙarin ƙimar zamantakewa. Mai tayi B ya yi alƙawarin ƙaramin tsari da aka ƙimanta a £40,000. Idan hukumar ta ba da maki ga ƙimar zamantakewa bisa gwargwado da mafi ƙarfin tayi, Mai tayi A ya samu maki 10 cikakku; Mai tayi B ya samu 10 × (£40,000 ÷ £90,000) = maki 4.4 — gibin maki 5.6 wanda zai iya yanke shawarar kwangilar ko da inganci da farashi suna kusa.

**Mai tayi na ɓangaren sa kai**: ƙaramar VCSE (ƙungiyar sa kai, al'umma da kasuwancin zamantakewa) da ke neman kwangilar kula da lambuna a kan mai fafatawa na kasuwanci ba za ta iya yin gogayya da farashin raka'a kaɗai ba, amma tana amfani da wakilan Global Value Exchange don ƙimanta alƙawuran ɗaukar aikin al'umma da sa kai da take da su, tana ƙirƙirar shari'ar ƙimar zamantakewa mai shaida da ta cancanci maki tare da farashi da inganci.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Cin tayi da alƙawuran ƙimar zamantakewa da aka ƙimanta yana haifar da wajibcin tabbatar da isarwa a kansu ta hanyar kula da kwangila — kayan aikin da ke rubuta farawar koyon sana'a, kashe kuɗi na gida, da awannin horo a kan takamaiman alƙawuran da aka ba su maki a tayi, suna shiga taron nazarin kwangila maimakon a manta da su da zarar an sanya hannu. Jerin G-Cloud da Digital Marketplace suna ƙara buƙatar bayanan ƙimar zamantakewa a lokacin jera. Duba [ribar zamantakewa daga zuba jari](../ribar-zamantakewa-daga-zuba-jari/) don hanyar ƙimantawa da ke bayan alƙawuran, [rumbun bayanan kuɗin raka'a](../rumbun-bayanan-kuɗin-rakaa/) don wakilan da masu tayi ke amfani da su, da [sakamako da abin da aka samar](../sakamako-da-fitarwa-bambancin-outcomes-da-outputs/) don tabbatar alƙawuran da aka isar sakamako ne, ba ƙirga ayyuka kaɗai ba.

## Tarkuna

- **Wanke tayi da ƙimar zamantakewa ta ƙarya.** Alƙawura marasa tabbas ("muna goyon bayan al'ummar yankin") waɗanda ba za a iya aunawa ko riƙe su a kula da kwangila ba suna samun maki mai kyau amma ba sa isar da abin da za a tabbatar.
- **Ɗaukar ƙimar zamantakewa a matsayin mai raba kunnen doki.** PPN 06/20 tana buƙatar a tantance ƙimar zamantakewa a fili cikin ƙa'idodin bayar da kwangila, ba a yi amfani da ita ba bisa ƙa'ida don raba tayi biyu daidai.
- **Rashin bibiyar kula da kwangila.** Alƙawuran da aka ba maki a tayi sau da yawa ba a bibiye su a lokacin isarwa — duba [cimma amfanin shiri (benefits realization)](../cimma-faidodi/).
- **Rashin daidaiton tsarin aunawa tsakanin kwangiloli.** Amfani da majiyoyin wakilai daban-daban don alƙawura iri ɗaya a kwangiloli daban-daban yana sa kwatanta matakin jerin ayyuka ya zama ba shi da ma'ana, shi ya sa tsare-tsare gama-gari kamar National TOMs da rumbun kuɗin raka'a na bai-ɗaya suke wanzuwa.
