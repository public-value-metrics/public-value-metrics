# Haɗa Kowa da Dijital

Haɗa kowa da dijital (digital inclusion) horo ne na tabbatar da cewa "digital by default" bai zama "digital kawai" ba — cewa hidimomin jama'a da aka tsara a kan hanya mafi arha har yanzu suna aiki ga ƴan ƙasa da ba za su iya ko ba su so amfani da ita ba tare da taimako ba. GDS ya ƙirƙiri takamaiman tsarin isarwa, "assisted digital," a matsayin wajibi ga kowace hidimar dijital ta gwamnati, ba ƙari na zaɓi ba.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Government Digital Strategy na 2012 ya bayyana buri a fili: ya kamata a gina hidimomin dijital a matsayin digital by default, amma dabarar kanta ta amince cewa kusan 10% na manya na Birtaniya ba za su iya amfani da su ba tare da taimako ba, kuma ta ɗaure sassan gwamnati su samar da tallafin assisted digital — hanyar da ɗan adam ke shiga tsakani, ta waya, a cikin mutum, ko ta wani matsakanci — a matsayin ɓangare na hidimar, ba wani madadin dabam da aka manna daga baya ba. Wannan alƙawari yanzu shi ne batu na 5 na [ma'aunin hidimar dijital](../maaunin-hidimar-dijital/), "tabbatar da cewa kowa zai iya amfani da hidimar". Girman ci gaba da keɓewa ana bin diddiginsa ta Lloyds Banking Group's annual UK Consumer Digital Index: bugun 2024 ya gano cewa kusan mutane miliyan 1.6 a Birtaniya suna nan ba tare da intanet ba, kuma wannan rukunin ya karkata sosai zuwa mutane masu shekaru 70–79, masu samun ƙasa da £35,000, da waɗanda suka yi ritaya ko ba su da aiki — daidai al'ummar da suka fi dogara ga hidimomin jama'a da ake sake tsarawa. Rahoton ɗaya ya gano cewa 48% na ma'aikatan Birtaniya ne kawai za su iya kammala dukan ayyuka 20 a tsarin Essential Digital Skills, ma'ana keɓewa ba haɗin intanet ne na bibiyu ba, bakan ƙwarewa, kwarin gwiwa da amincewa ne wanda ma'aunin "yana da broadband" mai sauƙi ya rasa gaba ɗaya.

## Lissafi

Haɗa kowa da dijital tsari ne da binciken daidaito ba dabara ɗaya ba, amma yana haɗuwa da tantance ƙima ta ƙididdiga ta hanyar [auna rabo (distributional weighting)](../auna-rabon-faida-bisa-matsayin-mutane/):

```
Ƙimar sauya hanya mai sauƙi:
  ƙima = yawan da aka motsa × (kuɗin_tsohuwar − kuɗin_dijital)     [duba channel-shift-savings]

Ƙimar da aka gyara bisa haɗa kowa:
  ƙima = (yawan da aka motsa × tanadi mara nauyi)
        − (masu amfani da aka cire × kuɗin samar da assisted-digital)
        − (gyaran nauyin rabo don cutarwa ga rukunoni da aka cire
           waɗanda suka rasa samun hidima ko suka fuskanci ƙarancin ingancin hidima)

Assisted digital ba ragowar kuɗin gazawa ba ne — hanya ce da aka tsara
tare da nata [kuɗin kowace ma'amala](../kuɗin-kowace-maamala/),
yawanci mafi girma sosai a kowace ma'amala fiye da dijital na kai-da-kai amma
har yanzu yawanci ya fi arha da tsohuwar hanyar da yake maye gurbinta a ɓangare.
```

## Misali aiki

**Hidimar fa'ida ta ƙasa irin ta Universal Credit**: da'awoyi miliyan 2.5/shekara, an tantance cewa kimanin 10% na masu da'awa suna buƙatar tallafin assisted digital bisa zaton tsarin Government Digital Strategy.

```
Rukunin da aka cire/assisted-digital = 2,500,000 × 10% = da'awoyi 250,000/shekara

Kuɗin hanyar assisted-digital (tallafin waya + fuska-da-fuska,
ma'aikata don magance rauni da sarƙaƙiya) ≈ £9.50/da'awa
  = 250,000 × £9.50 = £2,375,000/shekara

Kuɗin dijital na kai-da-kai ga sauran 90% ≈ £0.40/da'awa
  = 2,250,000 × £0.40 = £900,000/shekara

Haɗaɗɗen kuɗin kowace ma'amala = (2,375,000 + 900,000) / 2,500,000
  = £1.31/da'awa

Ƙira da ta tsallake assisted digital don cimma ƙaramin lambar kuɗin kowace
ma'amala (misali £0.40 haɗaɗɗe, tare da yin watsi da masu da'awa 250,000
da aka cire) ba ta kawar da wannan kuɗin £2.375m ba — tana mayar da shi
zuwa haƙƙoƙin da ba a nema ba, ƙararraki, da buƙatar hidimar rikici ta ƙasa
da ke sauka a kasafi daban gaba ɗaya.
```

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Assisted digital hanya ce da aka tsara, wanda ke nufin tana da mu'amaloli, SLA da kayan auna kamar kowace: kayan aikin ma'aikacin shari'a na waya, tashar matsakanci ga Citizens Advice ko karamar hukuma, ko tsarin kiosk na cikin mutum. Ɗaukarta a matsayin tunani na baya — lambar waya a ƙananan rubutu maimakon hanyar da aka yi la'akari da ita tun gano — shi ne hanyar da ta fi yawa da hidimomi ke faɗuwa a batu na 5 na [ma'aunin hidimar dijital](../maaunin-hidimar-dijital/) a tantancewa. Haɗa kowa da dijital shi ne ruwan tabarau na daidaito a kowane maudu'i na wannan rukuni: yana iyakance yadda za a iya cimma [tanadin sauya hanyar hidima](../tanadin-sauya-hanyar-hidima/) da ƙarfi, layi ne da dole a haɗa shi da gaskiya a cikin [kuɗin kowace ma'amala](../kuɗin-kowace-maamala/), kuma shi ne amfani kai-tsaye na [auna rabo](../auna-rabon-faida-bisa-matsayin-mutane/) a yanayin hidimomin dijital — tanadin da ya sauka ba daidai ba a kan mutanen da suka riga suka kasance cikin keɓewar dijital da tattalin arziki ya kamata a rage masa nauyi, ba a ɗauke shi daidai da tanadin da aka yaɗa daidai a tsakanin jama'a ba.

## Tarkuna

- **Karanta "digital by default" a matsayin "digital kawai"**: rufe layin waya ko kanti da zarar karɓar dijital ta wuce iyaka, ba tare da tabbatar da cewa sauran rukunin suna da madadin da za a iya amfani da shi da gaske ba.
- **Auna haɗa kowa ta haɗin intanet na bibiyu**: "yana da broadband" ko "yana mallakar wayar hannu" wakili ne mara kyau na iya kammala takamaiman ma'amala — gibin Essential Digital Skills (48% kawai na ma'aikatan Birtaniya ke kammala dukan ayyuka 20, a cewar Lloyds 2024) yana nuna ƙwarewa da kwarin gwiwa suna da muhimmanci kamar samun dama.
- **Ƙididdige assisted digital a matsayin kuskuren zagaye**: kasafinsa a matsayin ƙaramin layin tanadi maimakon ingantacciyar hanya da take da nata [kuɗin kowace ma'amala](../kuɗin-kowace-maamala/), sannan a yi mamakin lokacin da aka rage masa kuɗi da ma'aikata a farawa.
- **Binciken waɗanda suka kammala dijital cikin nasara kawai**: binciken gamsuwa da amfani da aka gudanar duka a cikin hidima yana rasa mutanen da ba su isa nan ba, wanda shi ne daidai al'ummar da aikin haɗa kowa da dijital ke nufin kariya.
