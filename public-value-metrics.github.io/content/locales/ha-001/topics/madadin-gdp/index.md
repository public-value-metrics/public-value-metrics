# Madadin GDP

Madadin GDP ma'auni ne da aka gina don ɗaukar abin da Jimillar Kayan Cikin Gida (GDP) ke watsi da shi ta tsari: aikin kulawa da ba a biya ba, ƙarewar muhalli, rabon kuɗin shiga, da ko ci gaba yana inganta rayuwa da gaske. Mafi shahara su ne Genuine Progress Indicator (GPI) da Gross National Happiness (GNH) Index na Bhutan; mafi tasirin hujjar ɗaukar su da muhimmanci ita ce ta Hukumar Stiglitz-Sen-Fitoussi ta 2009. Ga injiniyoyin da ke gina allon gwamnati ko tsarin KPI, "wace lamba ce ke ƙidayuwa a matsayin ci gaba" shawara ce ta ƙira mai sakamako na gaske kan abin da ake ba da kuɗi.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Simon Kuznets, wanda ya gina asusun ƙasa na Amurka a shekarun 1930, ya gargaɗi Majalisa a 1934 cewa "da kyar za a iya fahimtar jin daɗin ƙasa daga auna kuɗin shigar ƙasa" — gargaɗin da lambar ta wuce kusan nan da nan. GDP yana ƙidaya tsabtace zubar mai a matsayin ci gaba kuma kula da yara da iyaye ke yi ba tare da biya ba a matsayin komai; ba ya bambanta kashe kuɗi da ke gina jin daɗi mai ɗorewa da kashe kuɗi da kawai ke rama cutarwar da ta riga ta faru. Hukumar Stiglitz-Sen-Fitoussi, wadda Shugaban Faransa Nicolas Sarkozy ya kira kuma Joseph Stiglitz, Amartya Sen, da Jean-Paul Fitoussi suka jagoranta, ta ba da rahoto a 2009 cewa ya kamata tsarin ƙididdiga ya mayar da hankali "daga auna samar da tattalin arziki zuwa auna jin daɗin mutane," kuma a bibiyi dorewa dabam da jin daɗin yanzu maimakon naɗa su cikin lamba ɗaya. Madadin GDP suna aiwatar da wannan shawara. GPI, wanda ƙungiyar bincike Redefining Progress ta haɓaka a shekarun 1990 kuma ya ginu a kan Measure of Economic Welfare na William Nordhaus da James Tobin na 1972, yana farawa daga amfani na mutum (kamar GDP) sannan ya ƙara fa'idodin da ba na kasuwa ba da GDP ke tsallakewa (aikin gida, aikin sa-kai) yayin da yake rage kuɗin kariya da ƙarewa (laifi, gurɓata, tafiya zuwa aiki, ɗiban albarkatu) da GDP ke ƙidaya a matsayin masu kyau ba daidai ba. GNH Index na Bhutan, wanda GNH Centre Bhutan ke gudanarwa (<https://www.gnhcentre.bt/>), ya wuce haka har yanzu, yana maye gurbin ci gaba a matsayin manufar ƙasar da aka bayyana a kundin tsarin mulki: yana tara alamomi 33 a cikin fannoni 9 — jin daɗin tunani, lafiya, ilimi, amfani da lokaci, bambancin al'ada, shugabanci, ƙarfin al'umma, bambancin muhalli, da matsayin rayuwa — zuwa maki ɗaya na bisa isa wanda ake amfani da shi kai tsaye don tace shawarwarin manufofin gwamnati.

## Lissafi

```
GPI = personal consumption expenditure
      + non-market benefits (household labour, volunteering, higher education)
      − defensive and social costs (crime, pollution, commuting, family breakdown)
      − depletion of natural and social capital (resource drawdown, farmland loss)

GNH sufficiency score, per domain:
  a person is "sufficient" in a domain once they clear its threshold on each indicator
  Happiness Index = (% of population sufficient in ≥ 6 of 9 domains)
                    + (weighted average shortfall of the "not-yet-happy" minority)
```

## Misali aiki

**Yanki, GPI**: amfanin mutum shi ne $50bn. Ƙara ƙimar aikin gida da na sa-kai da aka ƙiyasta na $12bn (ƙimar albashin maye gurbi — duba [ƙimar lokacin masu sa-kai](../ƙimar-lokacin-masu-aikin-sa-kai/)). Cire ƙiyasin kuɗin shekara na cunkoson tafiya zuwa aiki ($3bn), laifi ($4bn), da ƙarewar albarkatu na dogon lokaci ($6bn):

```
GPI = 50 + 12 − 3 − 4 − 6 = 49 ($bn)
```

Idan GDP ya girma daga $50bn zuwa $55bn a wannan shekarar (+10%), amma kuɗin kariya da ƙarewa sun girma da sauri fiye da amfani, GPI na iya faɗuwa ko da GDP yana tashi — "hasashen ƙofa" da masu binciken GPI ke ambata ga tattalin arziki masu girman kuɗin shiga tun kusan shekarun 1970, lokacin da ci gaba ke ci gaba da hawa yayin da GPI ya tsaya.

**Ɗan ƙasa, GNH**: wanda aka tambaya ya wuce ƙofar isa a fannoni 7 cikin 9 (lafiya, ilimi, matsayin rayuwa, ƙarfin al'umma, bambancin al'ada, bambancin muhalli, amfani da lokaci) amma ya gaza a jin daɗin tunani da shugabanci. Saboda 7 ≥ 6, ana ƙidaya shi "mai farin ciki" a ƙidayar kai; ma'aunin yana bin diddigin zurfin gazawarsa guda biyu dabam don wucewar kunkuntar kada a rikita ta da ta jin daɗi.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

- Allon KPI da aka ƙera bisa fitarwa ko kashe kuɗi kawai (tsarin GDP) zai rasa cutarwar da aka yi wajen samar da wannan fitarwa ta tsari — yawan tikitin tallafi da ake ɗauka a matsayin "haɗin kai" maimakon "damuwar mai amfani" shi ne sigar isar da manhaja ta ƙidaya zubar mai a matsayin ci gaba.
- Lissafin irin na GPI tsarin bincike ne mai amfani ga kowane rukunin [KPI na gwamnati](../manyan-alamomin-aiki-na-ɓangaren-jamaa/): ga kowane ma'aunin fitarwa na kanun labari, tambayi wane kuɗin kariya yake jawo a shiru (sake aiki, magance matsala, gajiyawa) kuma a cire shi, kamar yadda GPI ke cire kuɗin kariya daga amfani.
- Hanyar isar da GNH ta fannoni — ci/faɗi a kowane fanni, sannan a tara — daidai dabara ɗaya ce da [nazarin yanke shawara bisa ma'auni da yawa](../nazarin-yanke-shawara-bisa-maauni-da-yawa/) kuma ya cancanci sake amfani duk inda maki na lamba ɗaya zai ɓoye fanni mai muhimmanci da ya gaza.

## Tarkuna

- **Ɗaukar GPI a matsayin asusun ƙasa mai daidaito** — ba kamar GDP ba, GPI ba shi da hanya ɗaya ta ƙa'ida; binciken daban-daban suna auna kuɗin tafiya zuwa aiki, lokacin sa-kai, ko ƙarewar albarkatu daban, don haka kwatancen GPI tsakanin bincike ya fi rashin tabbaci sosai fiye da kwatancen GDP tsakanin ƙasashe.
- **Shigo da GNH gabaɗaya cikin wata al'adar manufofi dabam** — an saita nauyin fannoninsa da ƙofofin isa ta tuntuɓar 'yan Bhutan; kwafe lamba ba tare da tsarin tuntuɓar da ke ƙarƙashinta ba yana haifar da ma'auni maras ciki wanda babu wanda ya amince da shi.
- **Zaton madadin GDP yana maye gurbin kimanta fa'ida da kuɗi** — waɗannan alamomin bincike ne na gabaɗayan tattalin arziki, ba kayan yanke shawara don shiri ɗaya ba; yi amfani da [nazarin fa'ida da kuɗi na zamantakewa](../nazarin-kuɗi-da-amfani-na-zamantakewa/) don haka.
