# Cimma Fa'idodi (Benefits Realization)

Gudanar da cimma fa'idodi (benefits realization management) horo ne na gano, kafa tushe (baseline), bin diddigi, da *tabbatar da shaida* cewa fa'idodin da aka yi alkawari a shawarar kasuwanci (business case) sun tabbata bayan fara aiki. A zuba jarin gwamnatin Birtaniya yana cikin tsarin Five Case Model na Green Book na HM Treasury da jagorar gudanar da fa'idodi ta musamman ta Infrastructure and Projects Authority; in babu shi, "tsarin ya tsira wa ma'aikatan shari'a minti talatin a kowane da'awa" yana nan a matsayin iƙirari da ba a tantance ba har abada.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Shawarwarin kasuwanci alkawura ne; cimma fa'idodi shi ne tantancewa. Green Book yana buƙatar kowace shawarar kashe kuɗi ta ci gwaje-gwaje biyar — dabara, tattalin arziki, kasuwanci, kuɗi, da gudanarwa — kuma shawarar gudanarwa dole ta bayyana yadda za a cimma fa'idodi *kafin amincewa*: an ambaci masu mallaka, an kama tushe, kuma an tabbatar da ranakun auna. Jagorar Infrastructure and Projects Authority, *Benefits Management: A Guide to Realizing Benefits for Government Major Projects* (<https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/benefits-management-a-guide-to-realizing-benefits-for-government-major-projects>), tana wanzuwa ne saboda rahotannin IPA na kansa kan Government Major Projects Portfolio sun sha gano cewa kwarin gwiwar isarwa da cimma fa'idodi suna cikin rauni da ke maimaituwa a manyan shirye-shirye. Aiki na iya rufewa "a kan lokaci kuma cikin kuɗi" bisa ga matakan isarwarsa yayin da har yanzu ya kasa cimma fa'idodin da suka tabbatar da kashe kuɗin tun farko — bambancin da jagorar IPA take ɗauka a matsayin ainihin manufar wannan horo.

## Lissafi

```
Yawan cimmawa = fa'idodin da aka cimma / fa'idodin da aka hasashe   (ga kowace fa'ida, ga kowane lokaci)

Hanyoyin da ke sa a iya lissafi:
  a kama tushe KAFIN fara aiki (in ba haka ba bambancin ba za a iya aunawa ba)
  kowace fa'ida: mai mallaka mai suna, ma'auni, tushen bayanai, jadawalin auna
  hasashe da aka gyara don son zuciya na kyakkyawan fata (optimism bias) a lokacin tantancewa (umarnin Green Book)
  a rarraba fa'idodi zuwa kuɗi-mai-sakewa / ƙarfi-mai-sakewa / na inganci,
  a bi diddiginsu a bayyana su dabam
```

## Misali aiki

**Karamar hukuma**: shawarar kasuwanci ta tashar dijital ta neman izinin gini ta yi alkawarin, a kowace shekara: rage kuɗin bugawa da aika wasiƙa £300,000 (kuɗi), awanni 4,500 na jami'ai da aka 'yanta (ƙarfi), da ingantacciyar gamsuwar masu nema (ta inganci). Watanni goma sha biyu bayan fara aiki:

```
Fa'ida            Hasashe    An cimma   Yawa   Shaida
Tanadin kuɗi      £300,000   £210,000   70%    littafin kuɗi da shekarar tushe
Awannin jami'ai   4,500      3,200      71%    samfurin auna lokaci-da-motsi
Gamsuwa           +8pp       +11pp      138%   bayanan binciken masu nema

Matakan da aka ɗauka daga bita (ma'anar cimma fa'idodi):
an gano gibin kuɗi ya fito daga sassan hidima biyu da har yanzu suke sarrafa
aikace-aikacen takarda a matsayin banda → a rufe hanyar banda;
gyaran son zuciya na kyakkyawan fata na shawarar kasuwanci ta gaba an ɗaga daga 10% zuwa 25%
bisa kuskuren hasashen wannan shari'a.
```

Yawan cimmawa na 70% ba gazawa ba ne — ilimi ne da ke ba da damar hasashe na gaba ya fi daidaito. Shari'ar da ba a auna ba za ta riƙa iƙirarin 100% har abada, kuma ƙungiyar kuɗi ba za ta sami dalilin ƙalubalantarta ba.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Ƙungiyoyin injiniya suna amincewa da zuba jari a dandamali da kayan aiki bisa fa'idar da aka hasashe a kai a kai kuma kusan ba sa tantance su bayan haka — daidai cutar da gudanar da cimma fa'idodi aka ƙirƙira don gyarawa. Sigar sauƙi: kowane tsari da ya wuce iyakar muhimmanci ya ambaci mai mallakar fa'ida, ma'aunin tushe, da ranar bita ƙayyadaddiya (yawanci watanni shida bayan fara aiki), kuma yawan cimmawa daga tsare-tsaren baya ya kamata ya rage yadda ƙungiya ke amincewa da hasashen ƙungiya ko mai sayarwa na gaba. Wannan yana rufe da'ira zuwa [tantancewar Green Book](../kimanta-shawarwari-ta-green-book/), wadda ke tsara hasashen da wannan horo ke tantancewa, kuma shi ne dalilin da ke bayan binciken da aka ruwaito sosai cewa yawancin gwaje-gwajen generative AI ba su nuna wani riba da za a iya aunawa ba — duba [ƙaruwar aiki ta AI a fannin gwamnati](../ƙaruwar-aiki-ta-ai-a-fannin-gwamnati/) — domin gwaje-gwajen da *suka* dawo da ƙima kusan ba tare da banda ba su ne masu layin fa'ida mai suna, mai iya bi diddiginsa tun farko. Kuma yana dogara ne da bambance abin da aka isar da gaske da abin da aka cimma da gaske — duba [sakamako da abin da aka samar](../sakamako-da-fitarwa-bambancin-outcomes-da-outputs/).

## Tarkuna

- **Rashin tushe kafin fara aiki**: kuskuren da ba za a iya gyarawa ba — ba tare da shi ba, ba za a taɓa iya lissafin yawan cimmawa ba, sai dai a iƙirarta.
- **Fa'ida marar mai mallaka**: fa'ida ba tare da mai mallaka mai suna ba ba ta da wanda ke tattara bayananta, kuma kowane bitar jerin ayyuka yana rahoton ta a matsayin "gabaɗaya a kan hanya" ta tsohuwa.
- **Ƙidayar fa'idodi sau biyu a tsakanin jerin shirye-shirye**: ayyuka biyu duka suna iƙirarin ƙarfin ma'aikatan shari'a da aka 'yanta ɗaya a matsayin fa'idarsu — a riƙe rajistar fa'idodi guda ɗaya a tsakanin jerin don kama wannan.
- **Wasan kwaikwayon cimmawa**: auna da bayyana nasarorin inganci masu sauƙi a bayyane yayin da layukan kuɗi da ƙarfi ba a bincika su ba a ɓoye.
- **Rikitar da isarwa da cimmawa**: aiki da ya rufe matakansa "a kan lokaci kuma cikin kuɗi" ba ya faɗin komai game da ko fa'idar da aka hasashe ta taɓa faruwa — jagorar IPA tana ɗaukar waɗannan a matsayin tambayoyi biyu dabam da hanyoyin shaida biyu dabam.
