# Rumbun Bayanan Kuɗin Raka'a (Unit Cost Databases)

Rumbun bayanan kuɗin raka'a (unit cost database) ɗakin karatu ne na wakilan kuɗi da aka bincika a gaba, masu tushen shaida, don sakamakon zamantakewa — ƙimar tafiya daga rashin aiki zuwa aiki, rage kaɗaici, zaman haya mai ɗorewa — waɗanda ke ba mai aiki damar ƙimanta sakamako ba tare da ba da umarnin bincike na ƙimantawa na musamman kowane lokaci ba. Suna wanzuwa don ƙaramar ƙungiyar agaji da ke rubuta neman tallafi ta iya amfani da irin tsantsa da kamfanin shawara mai arziki, ta sake amfani da wakili da wani ya riga ya samo kuma ya wallafa.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

UK Social Value Bank na HACT, wanda aka haɓaka tare da masanin tattalin arziki Daniel Fujiwara ta amfani da hanyoyin ƙimanta jin daɗi, da Global Value Exchange, buɗaɗɗen rumbun wakilan kuɗi da jama'a ke ba da gudummawa, su ne biyun da aka fi amfani da su a ɓangaren uku da na jama'a na Birtaniya. Duka biyun suna wanzuwa saboda aikin ƙimantawa da ke ƙarƙashinsu — [ƙimantawar jin daɗin rayuwa](../ƙimantawar-jin-daɗin-rayuwa/) da [ƙimantawa ta fifikon da aka bayyana](../ƙimantawa-ta-fifikon-da-aka-bayyana/) — yana da tsada, mai buƙatar ƙwarewar hanya, kuma a hankali don yi daga farko ga kowane aiki. Ɗakin karatun wakilai da aka wallafa na bai-ɗaya yana mayar da abin da zai zama aikin bincike na watanni da yawa zuwa dubawa kawai, shi ya sa suke da muhimmanci ga ƙididdigar [ribar zamantakewa daga zuba jari](../ribar-zamantakewa-daga-zuba-jari/) da tantance tayi na [Dokar Ƙimar Zamantakewa](../dokar-ƙimar-zamantakewa/): ba tare da su ba, ƙimantawa mai tsanani za ta kasance mai araha ga ƙungiyoyi masu girman da za su iya ba da umarnin nasu binciken kawai.

## Lissafi

Rumbun kuɗin raka'a ba ya lissafa komai da kansa; yana ba da shigarwa ɗaya ga lissafin da ake yi a wani wuri:

```
Financial proxy value = market price, OR shadow price, OR wellbeing valuation,
                         OR stated-preference value
                         for a defined unit of outcome change
                         (e.g. "per person moving from unemployment to employment, per year")

Applied value = number of outcomes achieved × unit proxy value
```

Duba [farashin inuwa](../farashin-inuwa/) don yadda ake gina wakili idan babu farashin kasuwa, da [ribar zamantakewa daga zuba jari](../ribar-zamantakewa-daga-zuba-jari/) don yadda ƙimar da aka yi amfani da ita ke shiga rabo bayan gyare-gyaren deadweight da danganta tasiri.

## Misali aiki

**Ƙungiyar agaji (SROI na sabis na abokantaka)**: shigarwar rumbun kuɗin raka'a don "rage kaɗaici" tana ba da wakili na misali na £1,100 ga kowane mutum a shekara. An yi amfani da shi ga masu amfana 80: 80 × £1,100 = £88,000 jimillar ƙima. Idan rumbun ɗaya kuma yana da wakili don "ingantacciyar lafiyar hankali" wanda ya ɗauko daga abin binciken jin daɗi mai karo, haɗa wakilan biyu ga mutane 80 ɗaya zai ƙirga wani ɓangare na canji ɗaya sau biyu — rumbun yana ba da lambar, amma kauce wa wannan karo nauyin mai nazari ne.

**Ƙaramar hukuma (SROI na ƙungiyar neman aiki)**: ana amfani da shigarwar rumbun kuɗin raka'a don "tafiya daga rashin aiki zuwa aiki mai ɗorewa" ga mahalarta 45 a wakili na misali na £8,500 ga kowane mutum a shekara: 45 × £8,500 = £382,500 jimillar ƙima, kafin gyare-gyaren deadweight da danganta tasiri da aka nuna a [ribar zamantakewa daga zuba jari](../ribar-zamantakewa-daga-zuba-jari/).

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Ƙungiyoyin da ke gina kayan aikin bayar da rahoto don ƙungiyoyin agaji ko masu ba da kwangila suna amfana daga "kundin sakamako" na cikin gida — tebur da ke dacewa da kowane sakamako da samfuri ko sabis zai iya iƙirari da wakili mai suna, rumbun majiyarsa, ranar wallafa, da alamar sigar — domin ƙungiyoyi daban-daban a cikin ƙungiya ba su zaɓi ƙimomi da suka ɗan bambanta ga sakamako ɗaya ba. Naɗe buɗaɗɗen bayanan Global Value Exchange a bayan sabis na dubawa, tare da nuna majiya da kwanan wata koyaushe kusa da lambar, yana kiyaye wakili mai iya dubawa maimakon lambar sihiri da aka binne a cikin spreadsheet. Duba [ribar zamantakewa daga zuba jari](../ribar-zamantakewa-daga-zuba-jari/) da [Dokar Ƙimar Zamantakewa](../dokar-ƙimar-zamantakewa/) don manyan wurare biyu da ake amfani da waɗannan wakilai.

## Tarkuna

- **Ɗaukar wakilai a matsayin daidai.** Yawancin wakilan da aka wallafa matsakaicin samfuri ne daga binciken ƙimantawar jin daɗi masu faffadan tazarar amincewa; ambaton ɗaya zuwa fam yana ƙara girman daidaiton da binciken da ke ƙarƙashinsa ke goyon baya.
- **Ƙirga wakilai masu karo sau biyu.** Haɗa wakilai (misali "rage kaɗaici" da "ingantacciyar lafiyar hankali") da aka samo daga tsarin bincike masu karo yana ƙimanta canji ɗaya sau biyu.
- **Amfani da wakili daga wani yanayi ba tare da gyara ba.** Wakili da aka daidaita a kan yawan jama'ar ƙasa ɗaya da shekara ɗaya, da aka yi amfani da shi a wani wuri ba tare da gyaran hauhawar farashi ko yanayi ba, yana bayyana ƙima ba daidai ba a asirce.
- **Rashin duba asali.** Global Value Exchange buɗaɗɗe ne kuma jama'a ke ba da gudummawa, don haka ingancin shigarwa yana bambanta ta mai ba da gudummawa; a duba majiyar da ke ƙarƙashinta kafin a ambaci lamba a neman tallafi ko mika kai ga saye.
