# Kimanta Shawarwari ta Green Book (Tsarin Shari'o'i Biyar)

Green Book jagorar HM Treasury ce ta wajibi don kimantawa da tantance shawarwarin kashe kuɗin gwamnatin Burtaniya. Babban kayan aikinsa, tsarin shari'o'i biyar (five-case model), yana tilasta shari'ar kasuwanci ta amsa tambayoyi biyar dabam-dabam — shin kyakkyawan tunani ne, shin yana isar da ƙima, shin za a iya saye shi, shin za a iya biyan sa, kuma shin za a iya isar da shi — maimakon naɗe komai cikin lamba ɗaya da minista zai iya amincewa da ita kawai.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Kowace shawarar kashe kuɗin gwamnatin tsakiya ta Burtaniya da ta wuce iyakokin da aka ba ma'aikata dole ta wuce kimantawar Green Book kafin a saki kuɗi, kuma Bitar Green Book ta 2020 ta HM Treasury (da aka wallafa bayan suka cewa tsarin ya nuna ɓangarancin ga yankuna masu talauci, duba <https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/green-book-review-2020-findings-and-response>) ta tsaurara buƙatar kwatanta zaɓuɓɓuka da ainihin tushen "yi mafi ƙanƙanta" (do minimum) kuma a nuna dacewar dabara kafin ma a tantance ƙimar kuɗi. Tsarin shari'o'i biyar da kansa ya riga Green Book — ya samo asali ne daga Office of Government Commerce a matsayin tsarin shari'ar kasuwanci na ƙa'ida — amma bugun Green Book na 2022 ya saka shi a matsayin siffar wajibi ga kowace shari'ar kasuwanci da ke neman amincewar Treasury: <https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-green-book-appraisal-and-evaluation-in-central-government>.

Dalilin raba shari'ar zuwa biyar shi ne cewa shawara na iya faɗi a kowane fanni ɗaya ko da sauran sun yi kyau. Sake gina dandamalin fasahar sadarwa mai kyau a dabara kuma mai inganci a kuɗi na iya faɗi a shari'ar kasuwanci idan mai sayarwa ɗaya ne kawai zai iya isar da shi (yana haifar da haɗarin sayayya daga ɗaya), ko ya faɗi a shari'ar gudanarwa idan ma'aikatar ba ta da tarihin isar da shirye-shirye masu irin wannan girma. Maki ɗaya na "ƙimar kuɗi" yana ɓoye daidai irin wannan yanayin gazawa.

## Lissafi

Tsarin shari'o'i biyar tsari ne, ba dabara ba, amma kowace shari'a tana da gwajinta na ƙididdiga ko na shaida:

```
1. Strategic case
   Evidence of a spending objective linked to organisational strategy.
   Test: is there a case for change at all? ("do nothing" is always an option.)

2. Economic case
   Options appraisal against a "do minimum" baseline, using
   social cost-benefit analysis or cost-effectiveness analysis.
   Test: which option maximizes net public value?
   See ../social-cost-benefit-analysis/ and ../cost-effectiveness-analysis-in-government/

3. Commercial case
   Market engagement, procurement route, risk allocation between
   buyer and supplier.
   Test: is the preferred option procurable on acceptable terms?

4. Financial case
   Affordability within departmental budget limits, funding source,
   balance sheet treatment.
   Test: can we afford it, this year and every year after?

5. Management case
   Governance, project plan, benefits realization plan, risk register.
   Test: can this organization actually deliver it?
   See ../benefits-realization/
```

Shari'ar tattalin arziki ita ce inda ainihin kimantawar ƙididdiga take: ana kwatanta zaɓuɓɓuka bisa ƙimar yanzu ta riba da aka daidaita da [ƙimar rangwame ta zamantakewa](../kason-rangwame-na-zamantakewa/), ta amfani da hanyar [nazarin fa'ida da kuɗi na zamantakewa](../nazarin-kuɗi-da-amfani-na-zamantakewa/), ko, inda ba za a iya mayar da fa'idodi zuwa kuɗi da gaskiya ba, ta [nazarin ingancin kuɗi](../nazarin-ingancin-kuɗi-a-gwamnati/) ko [nazarin yanke shawara bisa ma'auni da yawa](../nazarin-yanke-shawara-bisa-maauni-da-yawa/).

## Misali aiki

**Ƙaramar hukuma**: majalisa da ke kimanta tsarin fasahar sadarwa na gyaran gidaje na £12 million tana gudanar da shari'o'i biyar kamar haka. Shari'ar dabara: tarin gyare-gyare yana karya ƙa'idar doka ta gidaje masu kyau cikin watanni 18 ba tare da shiga tsakani ba. Shari'ar tattalin arziki: zaɓuɓɓuka uku da aka ƙiyasta kuɗinsu a tsawon shekaru 10 na kimantawa a ƙimar rangwame ta 3.5% (bisa daidaitaccen ƙimar fifikon lokaci na zamantakewa na Green Book na 2022) — "yi mafi ƙanƙanta" (gyara tsohon tsarin, NPV −£4.1m), "saya" (dandamalin COTS, NPV +£2.3m), "gina" (dandamali na musamman, NPV +£0.6m da zarar an yi amfani da son rai (optimism bias) na 40% na ci gaban manhaja a kan kuɗin jari da ba a rage ba, bisa Annex A na Green Book). Saye yana cin shari'ar tattalin arziki. Shari'ar kasuwanci: akwai masu sayarwa biyu masu dacewa, gasa a bayyane tana yiwuwa — ta wuce. Shari'ar kuɗi: akwai jari daga Public Works Loan Board, kuɗin aiki ya dace da tsarin kuɗi na matsakaicin lokaci — ta wuce. Shari'ar gudanarwa: majalisar ta isar da tsarori biyu masu kama a shekaru biyar da suka wuce — ta wuce. Shawarar ta ci gaba da "saya."

**Ma'aikatar gwamnatin tsakiya**: shawara mai ƙarfin shari'ar tattalin arziki (NPV +£40m) amma inda mai sayarwa ɗaya ne kawai ke da izinin da ake buƙata ta faɗi a gwajin shari'ar kasuwanci don gasa, tana tilasta ko dai yafe sayayya daga ɗaya (tare da nauyin bincikensa) ko sake tsara ƙayyadaddun bayanai don buɗe kasuwa — shari'ar tattalin arziki kaɗai ba za ta taɓa bayyana wannan ba.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Ƙungiyoyin injiniya a cikin gwamnati ko ƙungiyoyin da aka ba tallafi yawanci suna ganin shari'ar tattalin arziki kaɗai, domin wannan ne ɓangaren da shugabancin samfur da injiniya ake nema su kare ("menene ROI na wannan ƙaura?"). Amma shari'ar kasuwanci da ke wucewa Treasury ko kwamitin tallafi tana buƙatar duka biyar, kuma injiniyoyi galibi su ne mafi dacewa da amsa shari'ar kasuwanci (shin za a iya saya wannan da gaske, ko yana kulle mu a tsarin mallaka na mai sayarwa ɗaya?) da shari'ar gudanarwa (shin muna da iyawar isarwa, ko wannan yana dogara ne da wasu mutane uku su ci gaba da zama?). Ɗauki buƙatar "lambobin shari'ar kasuwanci kawai" a matsayin buƙatar kashi ɗaya cikin biyar na ainihin yanke shawarar. Duba [ƙimar kuɗi](../ƙimar-da-aka-samu-daidai-da-kuɗi/) don yadda ake taƙaita sakamakon shari'ar tattalin arziki, da [jimillar kuɗin mallaka](../jimillar-kuɗin-mallaka-a-it-na-gwamnati/) don ainihin tushen ƙididdiga na shari'ar kuɗi.

## Tarkuna

- **Rubuta shari'ar tattalin arziki da farko sannan shari'ar dabara ta dace da ita.** Bitar Green Book ta 2020 ta gano daidai wannan yanayin gazawa yana tura ɓangaranci na kimantawa zuwa wurare da fannoni da tuni aka rubuta shaidarsu da kyau, yana kafa rashin daidaiton yanki; ya kamata shari'ar dabara ta kafa manufa kafin a kwatanta zaɓuɓɓuka.
- **Ɗaukar "yi mafi ƙanƙanta" a matsayin "kada a yi komai."** Daidaitaccen tushe shi ne zaɓi mafi ƙarancin kuɗi wanda har yanzu yake cika mafi ƙarancin wajibai na doka ko aminci, ba ƙaryar sifili na kashe kuɗi ba — kwatantawa da sifili na zahiri yana ƙara ƙimar kowane zaɓi da ake gani.
- **Tsallake shari'o'in kasuwanci da gudanarwa saboda shari'ar tattalin arziki tana da ƙarfi.** Shawara mai girman NPV da ba za a iya saya cikin gasa ko ƙungiyar da ke ɗaukar nauyi ta isar ba, ba shawara ce da za a iya ba da kuɗi ba; masu bitar Treasury suna ƙin yarda akai-akai a kan waɗannan dalilai ko da shari'ar tattalin arziki tana da gamsarwa.
- **Amfani da tsarin shari'o'i biyar sau ɗaya kawai, a farko.** Green Book yana buƙatar a sake duba shari'ar a kowace ƙofar amincewa ta gaba (shari'ar dabara ta farko, shari'ar kasuwanci ta zayyana, cikakkiyar shari'ar kasuwanci) yayin da kuɗi da shaida ke ƙarfafa — shari'ar da aka daskarar a matakin zayyana tana rasa ƙaruwar kuɗi da ƙofa ta gaba za ta kama.
