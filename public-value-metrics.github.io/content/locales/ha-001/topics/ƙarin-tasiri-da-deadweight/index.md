# Ƙarin Tasiri da Deadweight

Ƙarin tasiri (additionality) yana tambaya ko wani shiri ya haifar da sakamakon da ba zai faru ba in da babu shi. Deadweight shi ne kishiyarsa: kason sakamakon da zai faru ko da ba tare da shirin, tallafin kuɗi, ko rangwamen ba. Kusan duk wani iƙirarin tasiri daga shirin gwamnati ko ƙungiyar agaji yana ƙara girman tasirin har sai an cire deadweight, shi ya sa jagororin tantancewa na Birtaniya suke ɗaukarsa a matsayin gyara na farko kuma mafi muhimmanci ga kowace lamba da aka bayyana.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

"Mun taimaka wa kamfanoni 500 su bunƙasa" yana da kama da nasara, amma idan 300 daga cikin waɗannan kamfanoni za su bunƙasa ko da ba tare da shirin ba — saboda tattalin arzikin yankin yana farfaɗowa, saboda suna da wasu hanyoyin samun kuɗi, ko saboda tuni suna kan hanyar bunƙasa kafin shirin ya fara — to ainihin gudummawar shirin ta ƙarin tasiri ita ce 200, ba 500 ba. Littafin Magenta Book na HM Treasury (Ma'aikatar Kuɗi ta Birtaniya) da kuma "Additionality Guide" na HM Treasury/BIS wanda aka daɗe ana amfani da shi (an fara ƙirƙirar sa don shirye-shiryen raya yankuna da farfaɗo da birane, kuma ana amfani da shi sosai a tantancewar gwamnatin Birtaniya) sun tsara deadweight a matsayin gyara na farko a jerin lissafin tasirin gaske: tasirin gaba ɗaya ba deadweight, ba displacement, ba leakage, a gyara da tasirin ninkawa (multiplier), shi ne ainihin ƙarin tasiri. Tsallake wannan mataki shi ne hanya mafi yawa da ake ƙara girman iƙirarin tasirin gwamnati da fannin zamantakewa, da gangan ko ba da gangan ba — shirin tallafin kuɗi da ke auna sakamakon mahalarta kawai, ba tare da ƙungiyar kwatantawa ba, ba zai iya bambance tasirin kansa da abin da zai faru ko ta yaya ba.

Deadweight ba kaso ne ƙayyadadde ba; ya dogara gaba ɗaya da yanayin da ba a yi shiri ba (counterfactual) na takamaiman jama'a da shiri (duba [nazarin counterfactual](../nazarin-counterfactual/)). Tantancewar raya yankuna a Ingila a ƙarƙashin tsoffin Hukumomin Raya Yankuna (Regional Development Agencies) galibi sun gano ƙimar deadweight tsakanin kashi 20–60% bisa ga nau'in tallafin kasuwanci, shi ya sa tantancewa masu inganci suke bayyana kewayon da aka gyara da deadweight maimakon lamba guda da aka ɗauka, kuma shi ya sa masu bayar da kuɗi irin su National Lottery Community Fund da Big Society Capital suke buƙatar waɗanda suka karɓi tallafi su magance deadweight a fili a rahoton sakamako maimakon bayyana yawan mahalarta gaba ɗaya kawai.

## Lissafi

Jerin gyaran tasirin gaske na yau da kullum, kamar yadda aka tsara a jagororin tantancewa na Birtaniya (Magenta Book; HM Treasury/BIS Additionality Guide; jagororin tantancewa na ESIF da asusun tsari):

```
Sakamakon gaba ɗaya
  − Deadweight       (abin da zai faru ko ta yaya)
  − Displacement     (aiki/fa'ida da aka mayar daga wani wuri, ba a ƙirƙira ba — duba
                       displacement-and-attribution)
  − Leakage          (fa'idar da ta tafi wajen rukunin/yankin da aka yi niyya)
  × Multiplier        (ƙarin ayyukan tattalin arziki na kai-tsaye/na haddasawa, in akwai fa'ida)
  = Ainihin ƙarin tasiri
```

Ƙimar deadweight a matsayin rabo:

```
Ƙimar deadweight = sakamakon da zai faru ba tare da shiri ba
                   / jimillar sakamakon gaba ɗaya da aka lura

Ainihin ƙarin sakamako = Sakamakon gaba ɗaya × (1 − Ƙimar deadweight)
```

## Misali aiki

**Shirin tallafin kuɗi ga kasuwanci**: wani tsarin tallafi na yanki ya ba da rahoton cewa kamfanoni 500 da aka tallafa sun ƙara ɗaukar ma'aikata a shekara mai zuwa, matsakaicin ayyuka 3 kowanne — iƙirari na gaba ɗaya na ayyuka 1,500.

Ƙungiyar kwatantawa da aka daidaita ta kamfanoni makamantansu waɗanda ba a tallafa musu ba (duba [nazarin counterfactual](../nazarin-counterfactual/)) ta nuna cewa kashi 40% na ƙaruwar ɗaukar ma'aikata a kamfanonin da aka tallafa zai faru ko ta yaya, bisa ga yadda ƙungiyar da aka daidaita ta yi a lokaci ɗaya.

```
Ƙimar deadweight = 40%
Ainihin ƙarin ayyuka = 1,500 × (1 − 0.40) = ayyuka 900
```

Nasarar da shirin zai iya bayyanawa da gaskiya ita ce ayyuka 900, ba 1,500 ba — raguwar kashi 40% daga gyaran deadweight kaɗai, tun kafin a yi la'akari da displacement ko leakage.

**Shirin ɗaukar aiki na ƙungiyar agaji**: wata ƙungiyar agaji ta samar da aiki ga mutane 200 da suka daɗe ba su da aiki, a kuɗi £600,000 (£3,000 ga kowane wanda aka sanya, a gaba ɗaya). Bayanan kasuwar ƙwadago na ƙasa sun nuna cewa, ba tare da wani shiri ba, kusan 15% na ƙungiyar makamanciyarsu ta marasa aikin dogon lokaci suna samun aiki a lokaci ɗaya ta hanyar sauye-sauyen kasuwar aiki na al'ada.

```
Ƙimar deadweight = 15%
Ainihin ƙarin wanda aka sanya = 200 × (1 − 0.15) = 170
Ainihin kuɗin kowane ƙarin wanda aka sanya = £600,000 / 170 ≈ £3,529
```

Lambar kuɗin kowane wanda aka sanya ta gaba ɗaya (£3,000) tana rage ainihin kuɗin gudummawar ƙarin tasirin ƙungiyar agajin da kusan 15%.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Ƙarin tasiri da deadweight suna da muhimmanci kai-tsaye ga duk wanda yake gina manhajar auna tasiri ko sarrafa tallafin kuɗi ga fannin gwamnati ko zamantakewa:

- Tsarin rahoton sakamako ya kamata ya tattara ƙungiyar kwatantawa ko ta tushe tun a tsarin gininsa, ba sakamakon mahalarta kaɗai ba — ƙara counterfactual bayan tsari ya fara aiki ba tare da shi ba ya fi wahala sosai fiye da gina tattara shi tun farko (duba [nazarin counterfactual](../nazarin-counterfactual/)).
- Allunan sa ido (dashboards) da ke bayyana yawan mahalarta gaba ɗaya kawai za su ƙara girman tasiri ga masu bayar da kuɗi da hukumomin sa ido; a inda ƙiyasin deadweight yake akwai (daga takardun tantancewa ko ƙungiyar kwatantawa), ya kamata manhajar ta nuna lambar da aka cire deadweight a kusa da ta gaba ɗaya, ba a maimakonta ba.
- Wannan yana da alaƙa kai-tsaye da [dawowar jari ta zamantakewa](../ribar-zamantakewa-daga-zuba-jari/), wadda rabon SROI ɗinta ba ya da inganci sai bayan an cire deadweight (da displacement) daga sakamakon da aka iƙirari na gaba ɗaya — na'urar lissafin SROI da ta tsallake wannan mataki za ta fitar da rabon da aka ƙara girmansa wanda ba zai jure bincike ba.

## Tarkuna

- **Bayyana sakamakon gaba ɗaya kamar duka ƙarin tasiri ne.** Wannan shi ne kuskuren auna tasiri mafi yawa a rahoton tallafi da shirye-shirye; koyaushe a tambaya "ko wannan zai faru ko ta yaya?" kafin a buga lamba babba.
- **Zaton ƙimar deadweight guda ɗaya ta shafi kowane wuri.** Deadweight yana bambanta sosai bisa fanni, jama'a, da yanayin tattalin arzikin yanki; a yi amfani da ƙungiyar kwatantawa ko shaida ta musamman ga fanni maimakon sake amfani da lamba daga tantancewar da ba ta da alaƙa.
- **Rikitar da deadweight da displacement.** Deadweight yana game da sakamakon counterfactual na mahalarta iri ɗaya; displacement yana game da tasiri ga wasu mutane ko wurare — duba [displacement da attribution](../ƙauratarwa-da-danganta-tasiri/). Haɗa su yana kai ga ƙidaya sau biyu ko rashin ƙidaya gyaran.
- **Deadweight da mahalarta suka faɗa da kansu.** Tambayar masu cin gajiya "ko wannan zai faru ba tare da taimakonmu ba?" tana haifar da ƙiyasin deadweight maras ƙarfi koyaushe (mahalarta suna yawan danganta nasara ga shirin); ƙungiyar kwatantawa mai zaman kanta ta fi aminci sosai.
