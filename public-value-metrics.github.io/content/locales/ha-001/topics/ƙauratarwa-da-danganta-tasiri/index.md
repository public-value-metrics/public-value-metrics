# Ƙauratarwa da Danganta Tasiri

Ƙauratarwa (displacement) tana faruwa ne lokacin da fa'idar shirin da ake gani ta samu ta hanyar ɗauke aiki ko fa'ida daga wani wuri, maimakon ƙirƙirar wani sabon abu — nasararka asarar wani ce. Danganta tasiri (attribution) kuma ita ce tambayar da ke da alaƙa da ita: nawa ne daga sakamakon da aka lura sa-hannunka zai iya ɗauka daidai, lokacin da wasu masu ruwa da tsaki da abubuwa suma suka taimaka. Dukansu gyare-gyare ne na yau da kullum a jagororin kimanta ayyukan gwamnati na Burtaniya, tare da tasirin da da ma zai faru (deadweight) da ɓarna (leakage), kuma ana tsallake su akai-akai a iƙirarin tasiri da ke kama da ƙarfi fiye da yadda suke.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Shirin tallafin kasuwanci na ƙaramar hukuma wanda ke taimaka wa shaguna 50 su ƙaura zuwa yankin farfaɗo da birni zai iya bayar da rahoton "an tallafa wa kasuwanci 50, an ƙirƙiri ayyuka 200" — amma idan waɗannan kasuwancin kawai sun ƙaura ne daga kan titin kasuwa maƙwabta maimakon faɗaɗa, to an ƙauratar da ayyukan ne, ba a ƙirƙire su ba, kuma ainihin tasirin gaba ɗaya a matakin gundumar (ko yankin) zai iya kusan zama sifili. Magenta Book na HM Treasury da Additionality Guide da aka daɗe ana amfani da shi suna ɗaukar ƙauratarwa a matsayin cirewa da dole saboda labarun nasara na gida suna da yawa ko da ba sa haifar da wata ainihin fa'ida ta ƙasa ko yanki — ƙima kawai ta ƙaura ne, galibi ga asarar yankin ko masu aikin da suka rasa ta. Jagororin kimanta asusun tsari (structural funds) (da aka yi amfani da su don tsoffin shirye-shiryen Asusun Raya Yankuna na EU da magadansu na cikin gida, kamar UK Shared Prosperity Fund) suna tsara wannan a matakai uku na fili: ƙauratarwa ta gida (cikin gari ɗaya), ta yanki (cikin yanki ɗaya), da ta ƙasa (a faɗin Burtaniya), saboda shiga tsakani na iya zama ƙari a mataki ɗaya yayin da take ƙauratarwa zalla a mataki mafi faɗi — shirin ayyuka da ke jawo ma'aikata daga garin maƙwabta ba shi da tasiri a ƙasa ko da yana kama da nasarar gida.

Danganta tasiri ita ce 'yar'uwar wannan matsala a isar da hidima mai yawan haɗin gwiwa, wanda yanzu ya zama al'ada a ayyukan fannin zamantakewa da hidimomin jama'a na hukumomi da dama. Lokacin da ƙungiyoyi uku suka haɗu wajen isar da hidimar hana rashin matsuguni, rahoton shekara na kowace ƙungiya zai iya da'awar yabo ga raguwar kwana a tituna ɗaya — idan aka haɗa rahotannin, tasirin da aka yi da'awa zai iya wuce ainihin canjin da aka gani, wani lokaci sau da yawa. Jagorar Magenta Book game da nazarin gudummawa (contribution analysis) ta wanzu musamman saboda danganta tasiri bazuwar ga mai aiki ɗaya galibi ba zai yiwu ba a isar da hidima ta hukumomi da dama, kuma amsar gaskiya sau da yawa ita ce "mun ba da gudummawa ga wannan sakamako" maimakon "mu ne muka haifar da wannan sakamako."

## Lissafi

Ƙauratarwa a matsayin ɓangare na jerin ƙididdige tasiri na yau da kullum (duba [ƙarin tasiri da tasirin da da ma zai faru](../ƙarin-tasiri-da-deadweight/) don cikakken jerin):

```
Net additional impact = Gross outcome − Deadweight − Displacement − Leakage, × Multiplier

Displacement rate = benefit/activity diverted from elsewhere
                     / total gross benefit/activity observed
```

Danganta tasiri, inda masu aiki da yawa ke ba da gudummawa ga sakamako ɗaya, galibi ana bayyana ta a matsayin kason gudummawa ba kashi daidai ba, domin ba za a iya aunawa da irin tsauraran da ake auna ƙauratarwa ba:

```
Attributable share ≈ f(strength of causal contribution, other actors' contributions,
                        external/contextual factors)

Claimed impact should never exceed:
  Σ (each partner's attributable share) ≤ 100% of the total observed outcome
```

## Misali aiki

**Tallafin farfaɗo da titin kasuwa**: shirin tallafin titin kasuwa na majalisa ya ba da rahoton ayyukan sayarwa 200 da aka ƙirƙira a yankin da aka tallafa. Binciken bincike na bin diddigi ya gano cewa 60 daga cikin waɗannan ayyuka sun fito ne daga kasuwancin da suka ƙaura daga titin kasuwa maƙwabta da ba a tallafa masa ba a cikin gundumar ɗaya, kuma wasu 30 sun fito ne daga manyan sarƙoƙin kasuwanci da ke buɗe rassa waɗanda da sun buɗe a wani wuri a yankin ko da ba tallafin.

```
Gross jobs claimed = 200
Local displacement = 60 (moved within the borough)
Regional displacement = 30 (would have opened regionally anyway)

Net additional jobs (borough level) = 200 − 60 = 140
Net additional jobs (regional level) = 200 − 60 − 30 = 110
```

Kanun labarin na gaskiya yana dogara ne da matakin yanki da mai ba da kuɗi ya damu da shi — shari'ar kasuwanci ta Treasury da aka kimanta a matakin ƙasa ko yanki ya kamata ta yi amfani da 110, ba 140 na matakin gundumar ba, kuma tabbas ba 200 na ɗanyen ƙidaya ba.

**Hidimar rashin matsuguni ta hukumomi da dama**: ƙungiyoyi abokan hulɗa uku (majalisa, ƙungiyar agaji ta gidaje, da asusun kiwon lafiya) tare suke isar da hidimar rage kwana a tituna. Mutanen da ke kwana a tituna a yankin sun ragu da 30 a cikin shekarar. Rahoton shekara na kowace ƙungiya yana da'awar "mun rage kwana a tituna da 30" — idan aka haɗa, rahotanni uku suna da'awar an taimaka wa mutane 90, sau uku na ainihin raguwar. Nazarin gudummawa da ke ba kowane abokin hulɗa kaso (misali, 40% majalisa, 35% ƙungiyar agaji, 25% asusun kiwon lafiya, bisa rubutaccen matsayi da kimantawa mai zaman kanta) zai bayar da rahoton 12, 10.5, da 7.5 daban-daban, wanda ya haɗu daidai zuwa 30 da aka lura.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Ƙauratarwa da danganta tasiri suna tsara yadda ya kamata a gina tsarin bin diddigin tasiri da bayar da rahoton sakamako don isarwa a wurare da yawa ko tare da abokan hulɗa da yawa:

- Faɗin yanki da na ƙungiya ya kamata su zama fannoni bayyanannu na farko a kowane allon tasiri — lamba da aka ruwaito "ga gundumar" da lamba ɗaya da aka ruwaito "ga yankin" lambobi ne daban-daban, kuma tsarin da ke rikita su zai haifar da lambobin da ba za a iya daidaita su a matakin jerin shirye-shirye ba.
- Inda abokan hulɗa da yawa ke isarwa tare, tsarin sakamako ya kamata ya rubuta kason gudummawa (ko aƙalla ya yi alamar danganta tare) maimakon barin sashen rahoton kowane abokin hulɗa ya yi da'awar 100% na sakamakon da aka raba da kansa — in ba haka ba haɗa jimillar a matakin jerin shirye-shirye zai wuce gona da iri kan jimillar tasiri, wani lokaci da yawa.
- Wannan yana da alaƙa da [dawowar jari ta zamantakewa](../ribar-zamantakewa-daga-zuba-jari/) da [rahoton sakamakon tallafi](../rahoton-sakamakon-tallafi/): lissafin SROI ko IRIS+ da ke watsi da ƙauratarwa ko ya wuce gona da iri wajen danganta sakamakon da aka raba zai haifar da ƙima mai ƙumbura da ba za ta tsira daga bincike ko sake yi ba.

## Tarkuna

- **Bayar da rahoton nasarar gida ba tare da duba ƙauratarwa mafi faɗi ba.** Shiri na iya kama da nasara sosai a mafi ƙanƙantar matakin rahoto yayin da ba shi da tasiri ko ma yana da mummunan tasiri a mataki mafi faɗi; koyaushe ka bayyana matakin yankin da ƙimar ƙarshe ta shafa.
- **Barin kowane abokin hulɗa a isarwa ta haɗin gwiwa ya ɗauki cikakken yabo.** Sai dai idan an amince da kason gudummawa kuma an rubuta, haɗa rahoto a tsakanin abokan hulɗa zai wuce gona da iri kan jimillar tasiri — ka tabbata da'awar matakin abokin hulɗa ba ta wuce jimillar da aka lura ba.
- **Ɗaukar danganta tasiri a matsayin kashi daidai alhali hukunci ne.** Nazarin gudummawa, ba kamar yanayin da ba a samu shiga ba (counterfactual) bazuwar ba, yana ba da ƙiyasi mai kariya, ba hujja da aka auna ba; ka gabatar da shi tare da rashin tabbas da ya dace maimakon daidaito na ƙarya.
- **Watsi da ƙauratarwa a shiga tsakani da ke fuskantar kasuwa.** Tallafin kasuwanci, shirye-shiryen ɗaukar aiki, da farfaɗo da wurare nau'o'i ne da ke da ƙauratarwa mai yawa; ka ɗauki duba ƙauratarwa a matsayin wajibi a kansu, ba na zaɓi ba.
