# Ƙimar Lokacin Masu Aikin Sa Kai

Ƙimar lokacin masu aikin sa kai (volunteer time value) ƙiyasin kuɗi ne da ake bai wa aikin da ba a biya ba, wanda aka fi amfani da shi don bayyana ainihin girman tattalin arzikin ƙungiyar agaji — asusunta tare da aikin da ba ta biya ba — ko don yin shari'ar cewa wani shisshigi ya fi ingancin kuɗi fiye da yadda kasafin kuɗinsa na tsabar kuɗi kaɗai ke nunawa. Hanyoyin ƙasa guda biyu sun fi rinjaye: ƙiyasin Independent Sector na Amurka da hanyar Hukumar Ƙididdiga ta Ƙasa (ONS) / NCVO ta Birtaniya, kuma suna ba da farashi daban-daban ga awa ɗaya ta aiki.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Kowace shekara, Independent Sector, tare da Do Good Institute na Jami'ar Maryland, yana wallafa ƙimar awa ta ƙasa ta lokacin masu aikin sa kai, wadda aka gina daga bayanan albashi na Bureau of Labor Statistics — musamman matsakaicin kuɗin shiga na awa na ma'aikatan samarwa da marasa kulawa a cikin albashin kamfanoni masu zaman kansu da ba na noma ba, tare da gyaran fa'idodin ƙari — kuma an rarraba ta jihohin Amurka. Sabuwar fitowarsa ta sanya ƙimar a **$36.14 a kowace awa don 2025**, ƙaruwar 3.9% daga shekarar da ta gabata, tare da ƙimar matakin jiha daga sama da $50 a Washington, DC zuwa ƙasa da $20 a Puerto Rico. A Birtaniya, ONS ta ƙiyasta daban kuɗin maye gurbin sa kai na hukuma a **£14.43 a kowace awa** (ƙiyasin 2017), kuma UK Civil Society Almanac 2024 na NCVO yana amfani da bayanan shigar sa kai — kusan mutane miliyan 14.2 da ke sa kai a hukumance a 2021–22 — don ƙiyasta jimillar gudummawar sa kai ta ɓangaren a kusan **£biliyan 18**, kusan 0.8% na GDP na Birtaniya.

Dalilin da ya sa wannan yake da muhimmanci fiye da kwalliyar lissafi: shirin da ya dogara sosai da aikin sa kai zai iya kama da mai arha sosai a ƙididdigar tsabar kuɗi ta [kuɗi a kowane sakamako](../kuɗin-kowane-sakamako/) fiye da wanda ke dogara da ma'aikata masu albashi, ko da ainihin kuɗin albarkatu — abin da zai kashe don maye gurbin wannan aiki — yana kama ko ya fi. Masu ba da tallafi da masu tantancewa da ke watsi da ƙimar lokacin sa kai suna ƙirga ƙasa da ainihin kuɗin tsarin isarwa mai nauyin sa kai a tsari, wanda ke karkatar da kwatancen inganci da tsarin ma'aikata masu albashi da ke isar da sakamako ɗaya.

## Lissafi

```
Value of volunteer time = Volunteer hours contributed × hourly rate

Rate choice matters and changes the answer:
  - Replacement cost approach: the wage of a paid worker who would do the
    same task (e.g. a replacement-cost rate for a qualified youth worker,
    not a generic average wage) — most defensible for task-specific valuation
  - Opportunity cost approach: the volunteer's own forgone wage — most
    defensible for valuing what the volunteer gave up
  - National average approach: Independent Sector's or ONS's single
    blended rate — most defensible for headline, cross-sector comparability
```

Hanyoyin uku na iya bambanta da yawa a awa ɗaya (lauya da ke sa kai a matsayin amintaccen kwamiti yana da kason kuɗin damar da aka rasa daban da kason matsakaicin ƙasa), don haka kowace lamba da aka ba da rahoto dole ta bayyana wace hanya ta samar da ita.

## Misali aiki

**Ƙungiyar agaji ta Birtaniya, hanyar matsakaicin ƙasa**: awannin sa kai 5,000 a shekara, an ƙimanta a £14.43/awa (ƙiyasin kuɗin maye gurbin na ONS):

```
Value = 5,000 × £14.43 = £72,150
```

Idan kashe kuɗin ƙungiyar a wannan shekara £300,000 ne, ainihin kuɗin albarkatunta — tsabar kuɗi tare da aikin sa kai — £372,150 ne, kusan 24% fiye da lambar tsabar kuɗi kaɗai. Ƙididdigar kuɗi a kowane sakamako da lambar tsabar kuɗi ta £300,000 kaɗai tana ƙirga ƙasa da ainihin kuɗi da irin wannan gwargwado.

**Ƙungiyar agaji ta Amurka, hanyar matsakaicin ƙasa**: awannin sa kai 2,000 an ƙimanta a $36.14/awa (fitowar Independent Sector 2025):

```
Value = 2,000 × $36.14 = $72,280
```

**Wannan ƙungiya ta Amurka, hanyar kuɗin damar da aka rasa**: idan masu sa kai galibi ƙwararrun da suka yi ritaya ne waɗanda kuɗin shigarsu na baya ya kai matsakaicin $60/awa, ƙimantawar kuɗin damar da aka rasa zai zama $120,000 — kashi biyu bisa uku sama da lambar matsakaicin ƙasa, yana nuna dalilin da ya sa dole a bayyana hanyar.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Tsarin da ke rubuta awannin sa kai (kayan aikin tsara aiki, dandamalin kula da masu sa kai) ya kamata su ɗauki awanni a matakin aiki ko matsayi, ba jimilla kaɗai ba, domin a iya amfani da kason kuɗin maye gurbi ga kowane matsayi maimakon kason matsakaicin ƙasa ɗaya ga ma'aikatan sa kai gauraye (awa ta amintacce da awa ta mai kula ba daidai suke a tattalin arziki ba). Ajiye kason da hanyar da aka yi amfani da ita tare da ƙimar da aka ƙididdige — ba lambar kuɗi ta ƙarshe kaɗai ba — yana ba rahoton da ke ƙasa (asusun shekara, ƙididdigar [ribar zamantakewa daga zuba jari](../ribar-zamantakewa-daga-zuba-jari/), rahotannin masu ba da tallafi) damar sake samar da lambar ko ƙalubalantarta daga baya maimakon ɗaukar ta a matsayin tsayayyen abu maras fahimta. Duba [kuɗi a kowane sakamako](../kuɗin-kowane-sakamako/) don dalilin da ya sa watsi da ƙimar lokacin sa kai yana ƙirga ƙasa da ainihin kuɗin isarwa a tsari.

## Tarkuna

- **Amfani da kason ƙarshe ɗaya ga ayyuka daban-daban a tsari.** Kason albashin matsakaicin ƙasa da aka yi amfani da shi ga awa ta ƙwararru ta kyauta (shari'a, kuɗi, asibiti) yana ƙasƙantar da ita sosai; a daidaita kason da aikin da aka maye gurbin a duk inda aikin na ƙwarewa ne.
- **Ƙirga sau biyu da kuɗin ma'aikata masu albashi.** Idan masu sa kai suna maye gurbin aikin da da za a biya, a tabbatar ƙimantawar ƙari ce ga tsabar kuɗin da aka kashe, ba a shimfiɗa ta a kan ƙiyasin ma'aikata da aka riga aka ƙara girmansa ba.
- **Ambaton tsohon kasonsa ba tare da kwanan wata ba.** Kason Independent Sector da ONS suna canzawa kowace shekara (ko ana sake ƙiyasta su lokaci-lokaci kawai, a yanayin ONS); lambar lokacin sa kai ba tare da kwanan wata ba a rahoto kusan ba ta da ma'ana don kwatantawa.
- **Ɗaukar ƙimar lokacin sa kai a matsayin dukiyar tara kuɗi.** Gyaran lissafin kuɗi ne don fahimtar ainihin kuɗin albarkatu, ba sabon kuɗi da ƙungiyar agaji za ta kashe ba; haɗa su yana ɓatar da kwamitin da ke karanta asusun.
