# Shekarun Rayuwa da Aka Daidaita da Jin Daɗi (WELLBY)

WELLBY maki ɗaya ne na ƙarin gamsuwa da rayuwa, a ma'aunin jin daɗi na 0–10 na yau da kullum, ga mutum ɗaya na shekara ɗaya. Shi ne takwaran tsari na QALY da ake amfani da shi a tattalin arzikin lafiya — raka'a ɗaya da ke ba ka damar kwatanta shisshigi waɗanda sakamakonsu ba su da wani abu a tare — amma an gina shi a kan jin daɗin da mutum ya bayyana ba yanayin lafiya na asibiti ba, kuma an shimfiɗa shi a cikin "Wellbeing guidance for appraisal: supplementary Green Book guidance" na HM Treasury (2021).

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Tantance kuɗi da amfani yana buƙatar raka'a ɗaya don kwatanta tallafin kulob na matasa da shirin amincin hanya da sabis na lafiyar hankali, waɗanda ba su da ma'aunin sakamako ɗaya. Tattalin arzikin lafiya ya warware wannan ga shisshigi na asibiti da QALY: shekarar rayuwa da aka daidaita da inganci, an auna daga 0 (matacce) zuwa 1 (cikakkiyar lafiya). Jagorar jin daɗi ta HM Treasury tana faɗaɗa irin wannan dabara zuwa kashe kuɗin jama'a da ba na lafiya ba, tana amfani da tambayar gamsuwa da rayuwa ta ONS da aka daidaita ("Overall, how satisfied are you with your life nowadays?", ana amsawa 0–10) a matsayin matakalar sakamako maimakon ma'aunin yanayin lafiya. WELLBY na 1 yana nufin gamsuwa da rayuwa ta mutum ɗaya ta tashi da cikakken maki ɗaya na shekara ɗaya (ko, daidai, gamsuwar mutane goma ta tashi da maki 0.1 kowanne na shekara — WELLBYs suna taruwa a cikin jama'a kamar yadda QALYs ke yi). Jagorar HM Treasury ta ƙayyade ƙimar kuɗi ta misali ga kowane WELLBY (kusan £13,000, farashin 2019/20) da aka samo ta daidaita bayanan jin daɗi da aka bayyana tare da wasu hanyoyin ƙimar shekarar rayuwa, tana ba masu tantancewa hanyar ƙimanta sakamako — rage kaɗaici, haɗin kan al'umma, samun wurin kore — wanda dabarun [ƙimantawar jin daɗin rayuwa](../ƙimantawar-jin-daɗin-rayuwa/) a baya za su iya bayyanawa kawai, ba kwatantawa a tushe ɗaya da kashe kuɗin lafiya ko aminci ba.

## Lissafi

```
WELLBY = Δ life satisfaction (0–10 scale) × number of years the change persists
        (summed across all people affected)

Monetized wellbeing benefit = WELLBYs generated × value per WELLBY (HMT reference value)

cf. QALY = Δ health-state utility (0–1 scale) × years lived in that state
```

Ma'aunin gamsuwa na 0–10 da ma'aunin amfanin QALY na 0–1 ba za a iya musanya su ba tare da matakin sauyawa ba; jagorar HM Treasury tana tattauna daidaita biyun domin, misali, shisshigin lafiya da aka tantance a QALYs da shisshigin zamantakewa da aka tantance a WELLBYs ba za a ƙirga su sau biyu a asirce ba ko a bar su ba za a iya kwatanta su ba a cikin [tantancewar Green Book](../kimanta-shawarwari-ta-green-book/) ɗaya.

## Misali aiki

**Sabis na kaɗaici na ƙaramar hukuma**: shirin abokantaka yana hidima ga tsofaffi 400 da ke kaɗai. Binciken biyo baya ya nuna matsakaicin gamsuwa da rayuwa yana tashi daga 5.2 zuwa 6.0 (ribar maki 0.8), kuma ana ƙiyasta tasirin zai ci gaba na shekaru 2 kafin ya ɓace.

```
WELLBYs = 400 people × 0.8 points × 2 years = 640 WELLBYs

Monetized value = 640 × £13,000 = £8,320,000
```

A kan kuɗin shirin na shekara £300,000 (£600,000 a shekaru 2), rabon amfani-da-kuɗi kusan 8,320,000 / 600,000 ≈ **13.9:1** ne — lambar da yanzu za ta iya zama a cikin tebur ɗin tantancewa ɗaya da kuɗin kowane QALY da aka kauce wa na shirin lafiya ko tanadin lokacin tafiya na shirin sufuri.

**Ƙungiyar agaji, ƙaramin girma**: shirin fasahar al'umma yana isa ga mahalarta 50 tare da ribar gamsuwa da aka auna ta maki 0.3, tana ɗorewa shekara 1.

```
WELLBYs = 50 × 0.3 × 1 = 15 WELLBYs
Monetized value = 15 × £13,000 = £195,000
```

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

- Duk wani sabis na 'yan ƙasa da ke tara abin binciken gamsuwa da rayuwa ko jin daɗi (yawancin dandamalin ƙananan hukumomi da lafiya da kulawa suna yi, bisa tambayoyin jin daɗi huɗu na ONS) zai iya ƙididdige WELLBYs kai tsaye daga bututun bayanai da ke akwai maimakon ba da umarnin tantance tattalin arziki na musamman ga kowane sauyin sabis.
- WELLBYs suna ba ƙungiyoyin injiniya da ke gina rahoton [Dokar Ƙimar Zamantakewa](../dokar-ƙimar-zamantakewa/) ko [ribar zamantakewa daga zuba jari](../ribar-zamantakewa-daga-zuba-jari/) mai raba na ƙasa da HM Treasury ta amince da shi, suna kauce wa yawaitar "maki na tasiri" na musamman da ba za a iya kwatanta su tsakanin kwangiloli ko masu samarwa ba.
- Domin WELLBYs suna taruwa tsakanin mutane da lokaci, suna haɗuwa da kyau cikin bibiyar sakamako a matakin jama'a da ake amfani da ita a tsarin [lissafin alhaki bisa sakamako](../alhaki-bisa-sakamako/) — dashboard na sabis zai iya bayar da rahoton WELLBYs da aka samar kowane kwata kamar yadda tsarin lafiya ke bayar da rahoton QALYs da aka samu.

## Tarkuna

- **Zaton ribar gamsuwa da aka bayar da rahoton kai ta faru ne gaba ɗaya saboda shisshigin** — ba tare da yanayin da ba tare da shiri ba (rukunin kwatance ko tsarin kafin/bayan tare da sarrafawa), ba za ka iya raba ribar WELLBY da yanayin gaba ɗaya ba; duba [nazarin yanayin da ba tare da shiri ba (counterfactual)](../nazarin-counterfactual/).
- **Haɗa WELLBYs da QALYs a jimilla ɗaya ba tare da daidaitawa ba** — jagorar HM Treasury a bayyane take cewa biyun suna amfani da ma'auni daban-daban da ka'idojin ƙima daban-daban; jimlarsu ba tare da tunani ba tana ƙirga walwala mai karo sau biyu.
- **Amfani da ƙimar kuɗi ta tunani ba tare da tantancewa ba** — lambar £-ga-WELLBY ƙiyasin matsakaicin ƙasa ne mai ainihin tazarar rashin tabbas; jagorar HM Treasury tana ba da shawarar nazarin ji-ji-da-hankali, ba ɗaukarsa a matsayin ƙimar musaya tsayayyiya ba.
