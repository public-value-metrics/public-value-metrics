# Katin Kimanta Ƙimar Jama'a (Public Value Scorecard)

Katin kimanta ƙimar jama'a yana daidaita katin kimanta daidaitacce (balanced scorecard) na Robert Kaplan da David Norton na 1992 — wanda aka gina don kamfanoni masu inganta riba a ra'ayoyin kuɗi, abokin ciniki, tsarin cikin gida, da koyo-da-girma — ga ƙungiyoyi da burinsu na ƙarshe manufa ne, ba riba ba. Yana tilasta wa hukuma ta jama'a ta ba da rahoton aiki a fuskoki da yawa da ba za a iya rage su ba a lokaci guda, maimakon rushe komai zuwa lamba ɗaya da ke ɓoye sadaukarwa.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Hujjar asali ta Kaplan da Norton, a cikin Harvard Business Review, ita ce cewa ma'aunin kuɗi ɗaya alamar baya ce da ba ta gaya muku komai game da *dalilin* da aikin zai canza a kwata mai zuwa. A ɓangaren masu zaman kansu maganin shi ne ra'ayoyi huɗu da aka haɗa. A gwamnati, "alwatikar dabara" ta Mark Moore (daga *Creating Public Value*, 1995) tana ba da tsari makamancinsa: dole ne hidima ta isar da **ƙimar jama'a** (sakamakon manufa), ta kiyaye **halacci da goyon baya** (goyon bayan siyasa da jama'a), kuma ta zama **mai yiwuwa a aikace** (mai iya isarwa da albarkatu da ƙwarewar da ake da su da gaske) a lokaci guda. *Balanced Scorecard: Step-by-Step for Government and Nonprofit Agencies* na Paul Niven (2003) littafin jagorar ma'aikata ne na mayar da akwatuna huɗu na Kaplan da Norton zuwa wannan alwatika — yawanci yana sake sa wa "kuɗi" suna "kula da albarkatu," yana sanya "manufa" a sama maimakon "ƙimar masu hannun jari" a ƙasa, kuma yana ɗaukar ra'ayoyin abokin ciniki da masu ruwa-da-tsaki daidai da juna, ba ƙasa da riba ba. Dalilin da wannan ke da muhimmanci ga ƙungiyar isarwa shi ne cewa hidimar dijital ta jama'a da ake yi mata hukunci kawai bisa ma'aunin kuɗi ko inganci (kuɗi ga kowace mu'amala, misali) za ta rage zuba jari bisa tsari a fuskokin halacci da sakamako da ma'aunin kuɗi ba ya gani.

## Lissafi

Katin kimanta ƙimar jama'a tsari ne, ba dabara ba, amma tsarinsa ƙayyadadde ne kuma ya cancanci a maimaita shi daidai:

```
Perspective          Public-sector question                    Example indicator
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mission / outcomes    Are we achieving the public value we      Population outcome measure
                       exist to create?                         (see outcomes-vs-outputs)
Stewardship of        Are we using public money efficiently     Cost per outcome, budget
  resources            and within authorized limits?             variance
Customer / user        Are users and citizens able to access    Completion rate, satisfaction
                        and benefit from the service?
Legitimacy / support    Do political principals, oversight       Trust metrics, audit findings,
                        bodies, and the public still back us?    complaints upheld
Internal process /      Do we have the capability and process   Staff turnover, cycle time,
  learning              to keep improving?                       backlog age

A defensible scorecard reports 3–5 indicators per perspective, chosen so that
no single perspective can be gamed without the damage showing up in another.
```

## Misali aiki

**Sashen kulawar zamantakewa na manya na ƙaramar hukuma**: katin kimanta na hidimar dawo da 'yancin kai (reablement) (tallafi na ɗan gajeren lokaci don taimaka wa mutane su dawo da 'yancin kansu bayan zaman asibiti) yana bayar da rahoton:

```
Mission:       68% of service users no longer need ongoing care after 6 weeks (target 65%)
Stewardship:   cost per completed reablement episode = £1,850 (budget assumption £2,000)
Customer:      user satisfaction 82%, average wait for service start 4.1 days
Legitimacy:    3 upheld complaints per 1,000 episodes; adult safeguarding board rates
               service "good"
Process:       staff vacancy rate 14%, average caseload 23 (safe caseload ceiling: 25)
```

Idan aka karanta su a ware, lambobin manufa da kula da albarkatu suna kama da labarin nasara kai tsaye: ƙasa da kasafin kuɗi kuma sama da manufar sakamako. Idan aka karanta su tare da layin tsari, kaso 14% na guraben aiki a gaban rufin nauyin shari'o'i na 25 yana nuna cewa ana sayen kyakkyawan sakamakon ta hanyar gudu kusa da matakan ma'aikata marasa aminci — gargaɗin da lambar manufa ita kaɗai ba za ta taɓa bayyana ba, kuma daidai kuskuren da KPI na ra'ayi ɗaya (duba [KPI na ɓangaren jama'a (public sector KPIs)](../manyan-alamomin-aiki-na-ɓangaren-jamaa/)) ke gayyata.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Ga ƙungiyar da ke gina allon bayanai na cikin gida ko na jama'a, katin kimanta hujja ce kai tsaye a kan na'urar "maki na lafiya" ɗaya: a gina allo ɗaya ga kowane ra'ayi, kuma a ƙi matsin samfura na haɗa su zuwa hasken zirga-zirga, domin matakin haɗawa shi ne daidai inda bayanin sadaukarwa ke lalacewa. Yana kuma dacewa da tsarin OKR na ƙungiyar samfura: OKR na manufa ba tare da OKR na kula da albarkatu ko tsari da aka haɗa ba yana maimaita kuskuren ma'auni ɗaya da Kaplan da Norton suka yi rubutu a kansa a 1992. Duba [ƙimar jama'a (public value)](../ƙimar-jamaa/) don ainihin ka'idar Moore ta abin da akwatin "manufa" ya kamata ya ƙunsa, da [ma'aunan amana da halacci (trust-and-legitimacy-metrics)](../maaunin-amana-da-halaccin-mulki/) don yadda ake cika ra'ayin halacci da alamomi na gaske, masu tushe maimakon wakili da babu wanda zai iya kare shi.

## Tarkuna

- **Rushe katin kimanta zuwa maki ɗaya**: matsakaicin ra'ayoyi huɗu zuwa lamba ɗaya yana sake dawo da ainihin matsalar — mummunan makin halacci da kyakkyawan makin kula da albarkatu ya ɓoye — da katin kimanta ya wanzu don hanawa.
- **Kwafi ra'ayin "kuɗi" na ɓangaren masu zaman kansu ba tare da canji ba**: ra'ayin kula da albarkatu na hukumar jama'a game da zama cikin kasafin kuɗi ne da aka ba da izini, sau da yawa an keɓe su, ba game da haɓaka kuɗin shiga ba — sake sa sunan Niven ba na ado ba ne.
- **Zaɓar alamomin da ƙungiyar da ke mallakar katin kimanta za ta iya motsawa ita kaɗai**: alamar halacci da aka samo daga ƙungiya ɗaya da take yi wa hukunci (bayar da rahoton korafe-korafe da kai, misali) ba shaida mai zaman kanta ba ce.
- **Gina katin kimanta sau ɗaya ba tare da sake duba nauyi ko alamomi ba**: Kaplan da Norton sun yi niyyar bitar dabara ta shekara; katin kimanta da aka daskare tsawon shekaru yana ɓacewa daga manufar da aka gina shi don bibiya.
