# Ma'aunin Jarin Zamantakewa (Social Capital)

Ma'aunin jarin zamantakewa yana ƙididdige hanyoyin sadarwa, amana, da shiga harkokin jama'a waɗanda ke ba al'ummomi da cibiyoyi damar yin aiki cikin inganci — "jijiyar haɗin kai" wadda ba ta da layi a kowane takardar ma'auni (balance sheet), amma a bayyane take rage kuɗi da ƙunci idan tana nan, kuma tana ƙara su idan ba ta nan. Tsarin zamani ya fito daga littafin Robert Putnam "Bowling Alone" (2000), wanda ya bambanta jarin haɗin kai na cikin gida (bonding) wato dangantaka cikin rukuni mai kama da juna, da jarin gadar haɗin kai (bridging) wato dangantaka tsakanin rukunoni daban-daban; Hukumar Ƙididdiga ta Ƙasa ta Birtaniya (ONS) ta gina tsayayyen tsarin alamomi don bin sawunsa a matakin ƙasa.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Babban iƙirarin Putnam, wanda ya tabbatar ta raguwar membobin ƙungiyoyin jama'a, halartar coci, da shiga ƙungiyoyin ma'aikata a Amurka a ƙarshen ƙarni na ashirin, shi ne cewa jarin zamantakewa yana hasashen sakamakon da tattalin arzikin gargajiya ke wahalar bayyanawa: ƙarancin laifi, jin daɗin yara, ingantacciyar ƙaramar gwamnati, da saurin farfaɗowa bayan girgiza. Jarin haɗin kai na cikin gida (ƙarfafan dangantaka cikin ƙaramin rukuni) yana da kyau ga tallafin juna amma yana iya taurare ya zama rufe kai; jarin gadar haɗin kai (rarraunan dangantaka tsakanin rukunoni daban-daban) shi ne yawanci yake da alaƙa da samun dama, kwararar bayanai, da amincewa da cibiyoyi. ONS ta ɗauki wannan da muhimmanci har ta gina tsarin alamomi na ƙasa — jerinta na "Social Capital in the UK" (<https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/wellbeing/bulletins/socialcapitalintheuk/latest>) yana bin ginshiƙai huɗu: dangantaka ta kai, goyon bayan hanyar sadarwar zamantakewa, shiga harkokin jama'a, da amana da ƙa'idojin haɗin kai, kowanne an gina shi daga tambayoyin bincike da aka kafa (Community Life Survey, Understanding Society). Ga ayyukan dijital na ɓangaren jama'a, jarin zamantakewa yana da muhimmanci sau biyu: sakamako ne da wasu shirye-shirye ke ƙoƙarin ginawa (tallafin juriyar al'umma, social prescribing), kuma shigarwa ne da ke ƙayyade yadda za a karɓi sabis — sabis da aka kai wa al'umma mai amana da kyakkyawar hanyar sadarwa zai yaɗu ta baki ta hanyar da irin wannan sabis a yanki mara amana ba zai yaɗu ba.

## Lissafi

```
ONS four-pillar framework (indicators, illustrative):

Personal relationships:      % with someone to rely on in a crisis
Social network support:      % who could borrow money from friends/family if needed
Civic engagement:            % who volunteered or took civic action in past 12 months
Trust and cooperative norms: % agreeing "most people can be trusted"

No single ONS composite score is published — the pillars are reported
separately, deliberately, because aggregating them into one index would
hide which specific pillar is weak.

Putnam's bonding/bridging split (framework, not a formula):
  bonding capital ≈ density of ties within a homogeneous group
  bridging capital ≈ frequency/strength of ties across distinct groups
```

## Misali aiki

**Hoton jarin zamantakewa na unguwa**: zaɓen ra'ayi irin na Community Life Survey a wani yanki ya gano cewa 78% suna da wanda za su dogara da shi a lokacin rikici (dangantaka ta kai), 61% za su iya aron kuɗi idan an buƙata (goyon bayan hanyar sadarwa), 24% sun yi aikin sa kai a shekarar da ta gabata (shiga harkokin jama'a), kuma 41% sun yarda da "yawancin mutane ana iya amincewa da su" (amana da ƙa'idoji) — idan aka kwatanta da matsakaicin ƙasa na kusan 85%, 70%, 30%, da 45% bi da bi (misali ne, a daidaita da bulletin na ONS na yanzu). Yankin yana ƙasa a kowane ginshiƙi amma mafi tsanani a amana (41% da 45% na ƙasa, gibin maki 4) da shiga harkokin jama'a (24% da 30%, gibin maki 6) — wannan yana nuna shiga harkokin jama'a, ba amana ba, a matsayin mafi girman gibi na dangi da ya cancanci zuba jari da aka nufa (shirin tallafin al'umma, misali) maimakon shirin gama-gari na "gina amana".

**Haɗin kai na cikin gida da gadar haɗin kai, tsarin sabis**: shirin samar da aiki a cikin al'umma mai ƙarfin dangantaka ya gano cewa shawarwari suna tafiya da sauri a cikin al'ummar (babban jarin haɗin kai na cikin gida: labari yana yaɗuwa cikin kwanaki) amma shirin yana wahalar isa ga mazauna a wajen wannan hanyar sadarwa (ƙarancin jarin gadar haɗin kai: karɓa a wajen ainihin al'umma kusan sifili ne bayan watanni). Gyaran da ake nufi ba "ƙarin talla" ba ne, sai dai gina gadojin haɗin kai da gangan — a haɗa kai da ƙungiyoyin da suke *wajen* hanyar sadarwar da ke akwai, domin jarin haɗin kai na cikin gida shi kaɗai ba zai iya warware matsalar jarin gadar haɗin kai ba.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

- Dandamalin dijital da ke tura taimakon juna, aikin sa kai, ko tallafin al'umma (sabis na "local connector", misali) a zahiri suna gina kayayyakin jarin gadar haɗin kai; ma'aunin nasararsu ya kamata ya zama bambancin hanyoyin sadarwa da aka ƙirƙira, ba adadin mu'amaloli kaɗai ba — duba [gwamnati a matsayin dandamali](../gwamnati-a-matsayin-dandamali/) don faffadan tsarin kayayyakin more rayuwa da wasu ke gina ƙima a kansu.
- Inda ka'idar sauyin shiri ta nufi jarin zamantakewa a fili a matsayin sakamako (asusun juriyar al'umma, sabis na social prescribing), [ka'idar sauyi](../kaidar-sauyi/) da [samfurin dabaru](../samfurin-dabaru-na-shiri/) ɗinsa ya kamata su ambaci takamaiman ginshiƙin (amana, shiga harkokin jama'a, goyon bayan hanyar sadarwa) da yake sa ran ya motsa, maimakon sakamako maras bambanci na "gina al'umma" wanda ba za a iya auna shi da tushen ONS ba.
- Alamomin jarin zamantakewa ruwan tabarau ne mai amfani na adalci tare da [Index of Multiple Deprivation](../maaunin-talauci-mai-fuskoki-da-dama-na-ingila/): yanki zai iya zama talaka a kuɗi amma mai arziki a zamantakewa, ko akasin haka, kuma su biyun suna nuna shisshigi daban-daban.

## Tarkuna

- **Haɗa ginshiƙai huɗu na ONS cikin maki ɗaya** — ONS da gangan ba ta yin haka; lamba ɗaya tana ɓoye wane ginshiƙi ne ke jawo ƙarancin sakamako, kuma ɗaukar matsakaici yana ɓoye al'umma mai amana amma ba ta shiga harkokin jama'a da wadda take akasin haka.
- **Tsammanin jarin zamantakewa koyaushe yana da kyau** — jarin haɗin kai na cikin gida mai yawa a rukuni rufaffe zai iya yin ƙin cibiyoyin waje (har da ayyukan gwamnati); nazarin Putnam da kansa yana ɗaukar haɗin kai na cikin gida da gadar haɗin kai a matsayin alheri daban-daban masu tasiri daban-daban, wani lokaci masu karo da juna.
- **Amfani da ma'aunin jarin zamantakewa na bincike a matsayin ma'aunin aiki na lokaci-lokaci** — binciken da ke ƙarƙashinsa (Community Life Survey, Understanding Society) ana yin sa kowace shekara ko ƙasa da haka; a ɗauki bayanan jarin zamantakewa a matsayin alamar yanayi mai jinkirin motsi, ba abin da dashboard na sabis zai sabunta kowane mako ba.
