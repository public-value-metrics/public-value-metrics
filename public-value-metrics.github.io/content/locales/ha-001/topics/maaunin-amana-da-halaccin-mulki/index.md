# Ma'aunin Amana da Halaccin Mulki

Halacci da goyon baya yana ɗaya daga cikin ƙafafu uku na "triangle na dabara" na Mark Moore a cikin *Creating Public Value* (1995) — tare da ƙimar jama'a kanta da ƙarfin aiki — kuma shi ne ƙafar da aka fi bari ba tare da aunawa ba, domin ba kamar kasafi ko ƙirga abin da aka samar ba, halacci ba shi da lamba ɗaya a bayyane. Ma'aunin amana da halacci iyali ne na ma'aunin wakilai da gwamnatoci ke amfani da su don cike wannan gibi: binciken amana ga cibiyoyi, ƙimar amincewa da hukumomin sa ido, bayanan korafe-korafe da ɗaukaka ƙara, da alamomin goyon bayan siyasa/majalisa.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Hujjar Moore ita ce cewa manajan jama'a da ke isar da ƙima ta gaske amma ya rasa halaccin siyasa da na jama'a zai rasa a ƙarshe muhallin ba da izini da ake buƙata don ci gaba da isarwa — ana yanke kuɗi, ana ƙuntata iznin aiki, kuma ana jefa sabis cikin yunwa ko da sakamakonsa yana da kyau. Don haka halacci ba abin da aka manta da shi na hulɗar jama'a da aka manna a katin auna isarwa ba ne; shigarwa ne mai ɗaukar nauyi ga ko manufa za ta iya ci gaba kwata-kwata, shi ya sa yake zama ra'ayi daidai da sauran a cikin [katin ƙimar jama'a (public value scorecard)](../katin-kimanta-ƙimar-jamaa/) ba bayanin ƙasa ba. Shirin binciken "Trust in Government" na OECD shi ne babban ƙoƙarin kasashe da dama na ƙididdige wannan: yana bibiyar kason 'yan ƙasa a ƙasashen mambobin OECD da ke cewa suna da amincewa ga gwamnatin ƙasarsu, kuma bayanansa na dogon lokaci sun nuna amana tana da saurin shafuwa da girgiza — rikicin kuɗi na 2008 da annobar COVID-19 duka sun haifar da sauye-sauye masu kaifi a matakin ƙasa, galibi ana biye da murmurewa a ɓangare kawai, tare da nazarin OECD yana gano akai-akai cewa *ƙwarewa* da ake gani (shin gwamnati tana isar da abin da ta ce za ta yi) da *adalci/mutunci* da ake gani (shin ana ganin gwamnati tana aiki ba tare da cin hanci ko son kai ba) su ne manyan abubuwa biyu da ke tuƙa lambar amana, daban da gamsuwa da kowace ma'amala. Gwamnatoci kuma suna ƙara ƙoƙarin aiwatar da halacci a mataki mai laushi — masu sa ido masu zaman kansu da masu bincike na Birtaniya (National Audit Office, Parliamentary and Health Service Ombudsman, masu sa ido na ɓangare kamar Ofsted da Care Quality Commission) suna aiki a matsayin binciken halacci na hukuma, suna mayar da "shin jama'a har yanzu suna amincewa da wannan sabis" zuwa ƙima da za a iya dubawa.

## Lissafi

Amana da halacci batu ne mai siffar tsari wanda ma'aunin wakilansa masu amfani su ne:

```
Institutional trust index (OECD-style)
  = % of survey respondents answering "yes" to a confidence-in-government question,
    tracked over time, disaggregated by demographic group

Legitimacy proxy set (no single number substitutes for the construct):
  - Upheld complaints per 1,000 service users (ombudsman or internal complaints data)
  - Judicial review / appeals success rate against the body's decisions
  - Independent regulator/inspectorate rating (e.g. "outstanding" to "inadequate" bands)
  - Legislative/oversight committee confidence votes or critical report frequency
  - Freedom of information request volume and disclosure/refusal rate, as a proxy
    for perceived transparency

Legitimacy is corroborated, not calculated: a defensible legitimacy assessment
triangulates several of the above rather than relying on any single proxy.
```

## Misali aiki

**Hukumar haraji ta ƙasa**: daidaita halacci ta hanyoyi uku don rahoton ƙimar jama'a na shekara.

```
OECD-style trust proxy (department-specific confidence survey):
  58% of respondents say they trust the authority to "treat me fairly" (down from
  64% two years prior)

Complaints data:
  Upheld complaints: 4.2 per 1,000 taxpayer interactions (up from 3.1 per 1,000)

Ombudsman referrals:
  Referrals to the independent Adjudicator's Office: 1,850 in the year, of which
  61% upheld in full or part against the authority (up from 48% the prior year)

Reading across all three: trust is falling, upheld complaints are rising, and
independent ombudsman findings are increasingly siding against the authority —
three independent signals converging on the same direction, which is what makes
this a credible legitimacy finding rather than noise in any one series.
```

Motsin ɗayan waɗannan lambobi kaɗai zai zama shaida mai rauni; ma'auni uku masu zaman kansu suna motsi tare a lokaci ɗaya shi ne tsarin da ke sa iƙirarin halacci ya zama mai kariya.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Ba kasafai ake samar da ma'aunin halacci ta dashboard na ƙungiya ɗaya ba, wanda kansa darasin tsari ne: a gina bututun rahoto da za su iya karɓa da daidaita bayanai daga majiyoyin waje masu zaman kansu (tsarin shari'o'in ombudsman, rafin ƙimar masu sa ido, masu sayar da bincike) maimakon tsara rahoton halacci a matsayin ma'aunin cikin gida kaɗai, domin iƙirarin halacci na cikin gida ("mun ba kanmu ƙima mai amana") yana ɗaukar nauyin shaida kaɗan — matsalar 'yancin kai ɗaya da aka lura ga ra'ayin halacci a cikin [katin ƙimar jama'a](../katin-kimanta-ƙimar-jamaa/). Bututun bayanan korafe-korafe da ɗaukaka ƙara sun cancanci irin tsantsar ingancin bayanai kamar kowane bututun sakamako da ke ciyar da kwangilolin [biyan kuɗi bisa sakamako](../biyan-kuɗi-bisa-sakamako-da-bondin-tasirin-zamantakewa/), domin bayanan korafi da aka ba da rahoto ƙasa ko aka rarraba ba daidai ba yana ƙasƙantar da matsalar halacci a asirce kafin ta bayyana a binciken amana bayan shekara guda. Duba [ma'aunin gamsuwar 'yan ƙasa](../maaunin-gamsuwar-ɗan-ƙasa/) don takwaransa na matakin ma'amala na wannan ma'aunin matakin cibiya, da [ƙimar jama'a](../ƙimar-jamaa/) don cikakken tsarin triangle na dabara na Moore wanda wannan ƙafa ta ke ciki.

## Tarkuna

- **Ɗaukar gamsuwa a matsayin wakilin halacci**: ɗan ƙasa zai iya gamsuwa da hanyar mu'amalar ma'amala ɗaya yayin da ba ya amincewa da cibiyar gaba ɗaya (ko akasin haka) — duba [ma'aunin gamsuwar 'yan ƙasa](../maaunin-gamsuwar-ɗan-ƙasa/) don dalilin da ya sa dole a ba da rahoton su daban.
- **Dogaro da ma'auni guda na bayar da rahoton kai**: binciken amana da aka yi a cikin gida ba tare da tabbatarwa mai zaman kanta ba (bayanan ombudsman, ƙimar masu sa ido) yana da sauƙin watsi da shi a matsayin ba kai maki; a daidaita ta hanyoyi da dama.
- **Watsi da rarraba ta ƙungiyoyin jama'a**: jimillar lambar amana ta ƙasa na iya ɓoye halacci mai bambanci sosai tsakanin takamaiman rukunoni (ta shekaru, ƙabila, kuɗin shiga, ko yanki) — fitowar Trust in Government ta OECD kanta tana rarrabawa saboda wannan dalili.
- **Karanta faɗuwar guda ɗaya da girgiza ta jawo a matsayin yanayi na dindindin**: lambobin amana suna motsi da ƙarfi a kusa da rikice-rikice (faɗuwar kuɗi, annoba, abin kunya mai suna) kuma suna murmurewa a ɓangare; bayanai guda bayan girgiza bai kamata a miƙa shi zuwa raguwa ta dogon lokaci ba tare da ƙarin bayanai ba.
