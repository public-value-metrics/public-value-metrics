# Alhaki Bisa Sakamako (Outcomes-Based Accountability)

Alhaki Bisa Sakamako (OBA), wanda ake kira kuma Alhaki Bisa Sakamako na Ƙarshe (Results-Based Accountability, RBA), tsarin Mark Friedman ne na raba tambayoyi biyu da rahoton ɓangaren jama'a yakan haɗe su wuri ɗaya: "shin al'umma tana cikin ƙoshin lafiya?" (alhakin al'umma) da "shin wannan takamaiman shiri yana yin kyau?" (alhakin aiki). Haɗa biyun, a cewar Friedman, shi ne mafi yawan dalilin da ake zargin shirye-shirye masu kyau da yanayin al'umma da ba su da ikon canzawa.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Friedman ya bayyana tsarin a cikin *Trying Hard Is Not Good Enough* (2005), yana jayayya cewa yawancin rahoton jama'a ko dai yana nutsar da masu yanke shawara cikin alƙaluman matakin al'umma da hukuma ɗaya ba ta iko da su (yawan ɗaukar ciki a matasa, yawan rashin aikin yi, tsawon rayuwa) ko kuma yana nutsar da su cikin ƙidayar ayyukan matakin shiri (abokan ciniki da aka gani, tura-mutane da aka yi) da ba sa faɗin ko rayuwar kowa ta inganta. Gudummawar RBA ƙaramin ƙamus ne mai tsari da ke raba su: sakamakon al'umma (yanayin ƙoshin lafiya na al'umma gaba ɗaya, kamar "ana haifar yara cikin lafiya") ba na hukuma ɗaya ba ne kuma yana buƙatar abokan haɗin gwiwa da yawa su motsa tare; ma'aunin aiki (yadda wani takamaiman shiri ke hidimta wa takamaiman abokan cinikinsa) na hukuma ɗaya ne kuma ya kamata a yi hukunci a kansa kawai bisa abin da wannan hukuma za ta iya tasiri da gaske. "Tambayoyi uku na aiki" na Friedman — nawa muka yi, yaya kyau muka yi, kuma shin wani ya fi kyau? — yanzu an saka su a kwangilolin hidimar jama'a na jihohi da gundumomi a Amurka kuma, ta hanyar kamfanin shawara da kayan aikin Clear Impact da ya dace da RBA, ana amfani da su sosai wajen bayar da kwangila na ƙananan hukumomi a Birtaniya da ƙasashen Commonwealth. Abin da ke kan layi na zahiri ne na kwangila: bai kamata a hana shirin gidaje kuɗi ba saboda yawan rashin matsuguni a birni ya tashi sakamakon dalilai na tattalin arziki da ba su cikin ikonsa, amma lallai ya kamata a hana shi kuɗi idan abokan cinikinsa ba a samar musu matsuguni ba.

## Lissafi

```
Population accountability (the "big picture" a community, region, or nation shares):
  Result       — a condition of wellbeing (e.g. "residents are economically secure")
  Indicator(s) — a measure of that condition (e.g. unemployment rate, median household income)
  → no single programme owns the indicator; movement requires many contributors

Performance accountability (what one programme is responsible for):
  How much did we do?      — activity volume (clients served, units delivered)
  How well did we do it?   — quality/efficiency (% completing programme, cost per client)
  Is anyone better off?    — the outcome that matters (% in employment 6 months after
                              programme, before/after or against a comparison group)

A programme is judged on the third performance question, never on the population
indicator directly, unless its scale and design could plausibly move it alone.
```

## Misali aiki

**Shirin tallafin aikin yi da birni ke ba da kuɗi**, mahalarta 500 a shekara, wanda ƙaramar hukuma ta ba da kwangila ƙarƙashin tsarin aiki irin na RBA:

```
Population indicator (context, not the programme's scorecard):
  City unemployment rate: 6.2% (up from 5.8% the prior year, driven by a factory closure
  outside the programme's control)

Performance measures (the programme's actual accountability):
  How much:   500 participants enrolled (target 480) — met
  How well:   78% completion rate; cost per completer = £340,000 / 390 completers ≈ £872
  Better off: of 390 completers, 260 in sustained employment at 6 months = 66.7%
              versus a matched comparison group's 41% (see counterfactual-analysis)
```

Idan aka karanta shi a matsayin alhakin al'umma, shirin yana kama da yana gazawa — yawan rashin aikin yi na birni ya ƙaru a lokacinsa. Idan aka karanta shi a matsayin alhakin aiki na RBA, shirin yana nasara: ya cimma burin yawansa, ya riƙe inganci, kuma ya samar da sakamakon aikin yi da ya fi na ƙungiyar kwatance da aka daidaita da maki 25.7 na kashi, yayin da alamar al'umma ta motsa saboda dalilai (rufe masana'anta) da ba su cikin ikon shirin gaba ɗaya.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

RBA yana taswira kai tsaye a kan sanannen bambanci na SRE: alamomin al'umma kamar ma'aunan Arewa-Tauraro na matakin kasuwanci ne da babu ƙungiyar injiniya ɗaya da ke mallakar su daga farko zuwa ƙarshe (kuɗin shiga na kamfani, kason kasuwa), yayin da ma'aunan aiki kamar SLO na ƙungiya ne — abubuwan da shawarwarin tsarin ƙungiyar ke motsawa da gaske. Allon bayanai da ke bayar da rahoton duka biyu ba tare da lakabin wanne ne wanne ba yana gayyatar daidai kuskuren dangantawa da RBA aka gina don hana: injiniyan da ke kan kira yana zargin kansa kan ma'aunin da ƙungiyar dogaro ke iko da shi. Lokacin bayar da kwangila ko gina kayan aikin rahoto don kwangilolin sakamako, a gina "nawa / yaya kyau / shin ya fi kyau" a matsayin filaye na farko, masu tacewa dabam-dabam, maimakon KPI ɗaya da aka haɗe — ƙa'ida ɗaya ce da raba alamomin jagora da na baya a [KPI na ɓangaren jama'a (public sector KPIs)](../manyan-alamomin-aiki-na-ɓangaren-jamaa/). RBA kuma shi ne dabarar alhaki a ƙarƙashin [biyan kuɗi bisa sakamako da bondin tasirin zamantakewa (payment by results and social impact bonds)](../biyan-kuɗi-bisa-sakamako-da-bondin-tasirin-zamantakewa/): kwangilar PbR za ta iya biyan adalci ne kawai bisa ma'aunin aiki na "ya fi kyau", ba bisa alamar al'umma ba, sai dai idan matakin shi ne babban mai tuƙa shi da gaske.

## Tarkuna

- **Biyan ko hukunta shiri bisa alamar al'umma da ba ya iko da ita**: wannan shi ne kuskuren da RBA ya wanzu don hanawa; a koyaushe a bibiyi ko shirin babba ne ko ƙarami mai bayar da gudummawa ga sakamakon al'umma kafin a haɗa shi da sakamako.
- **Bayar da rahoton "nawa" kamar "ya fi kyau"**: ƙidayar ayyuka (abokan ciniki da aka gani) su ne bayanai mafi sauƙin tattarawa kuma mafi rashin bayani; a dage cewa a amsa tambayar "shin wani ya fi kyau" da ainihin bayanan sakamako, kuma da yanayin kwatance (counterfactual) idan zai yiwu (duba [nazarin yanayin kwatance (counterfactual-analysis)](../nazarin-counterfactual/)).
- **Ɗaukar alamomin RBA a matsayin tabbatattu har abada**: hanyar Friedman a fili ta maimaituwa ce — zagayen "bayanai, labari, abin da ke aiki, tsarin aiki" — ba aikin tsara katin ƙima na sau ɗaya ba.
- **Rashin ƙungiyar kwatance ga "ya fi kyau"**: sauyin kafin/bayan ba tare da yanayin kwatance ba yana haɗa tasirin shiri da yanayin da al'umma za ta nuna ko ta yaya.
