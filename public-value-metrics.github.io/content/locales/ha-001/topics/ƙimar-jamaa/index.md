# Ƙimar Jama'a (Public Value)

Ƙimar jama'a ita ce darajar da gwamnati ko ƙungiya ta ɓangaren zamantakewa ke ƙirƙira wa 'yan ƙasa gaba ɗaya — ba kawai fitarwar da take samarwa ko kuɗin da take kashewa ba, sai ko al'umma ta fi kyau saboda ƙungiyar ta wanzu kuma ta yi aiki yadda ta yi. "Alwatika ta dabara" (strategic triangle) ta Mark Moore ta 1995 ita ce daidaitaccen gwaji: ana tabbatar da wani shiri na jama'a ne kawai idan yana da *halacci da goyon baya*, *ƙima ta zahiri*, da *iya isarwa a aikace*, duka uku a lokaci guda.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Ƙimar ɓangaren masu zaman kansu tana da sauƙin ƙididdigewa: kuɗin shiga ban da kuɗi, abokan ciniki da za su iya tafiya su yanke hukunci. Ƙimar jama'a ba ta da irin wannan alamar kasuwa. Hidimar gidan yari, hukumar haraji, da ƙungiyar kare yara duk suna samar da abubuwan da 'yan ƙasa ba za su iya kawai su ƙi sayen su ba, kuma "abokin ciniki" (mai biyan haraji, mai laifi, yaro) sau da yawa ba mutum ɗaya yake da shugaban siyasa da ke ba da izinin kasafin kuɗi ba. *Creating Public Value: Strategic Management in Government* na Moore (Harvard University Press, 1995) ya samar da tsarin da ya ɓace: ya kamata manaja ya iya faɗin (1) wace ƙimar jama'a shirinsa ke ƙirƙira, (2) daga ina halaccinsa da kuɗinsa na bin ta suke zuwa — minista, majalisa, umarni, tallafi — da (3) ko ƙungiyarsa za ta iya isar da ita da gaske da mutane, fasaha, da hanyoyin da ke hannunta. Shirin da ya yi kyau a ƙafa ɗaya ko biyu kawai na alwatika ba a riga an tabbatar da shi ba, ko da kuwa niyyarsa mai kyau ce.

Wannan yana da muhimmanci a aikace domin yawancin gazawar software a ɓangaren jama'a ba gazawar fasaha ba ne. Tsari na iya zama kyakkyawa a fasaha kuma mai iya isarwa a aikace kuma ya gaza saboda babu wani a muhallin ba da halacci — ministoci, kwamitocin sa ido, jama'a — da ya so abin da yake inganta shi da gaske. Hidimar dijital ta Universal Credit da NHS National Programme for IT duka an ambace su a wallafe-wallafen gudanar da jama'a na Birtaniya a matsayin shari'o'in da ƙafafun aiki da halacci na alwatikar ba su dace da ƙafar manufa ba.

## Lissafi

Ƙimar jama'a tsari ne, ba dabara ba, amma yana tsara shari'o'in zuba jari da suka kasance marasa ƙarfi zuwa tambayoyi uku da za a iya gwadawa:

```
Strategic triangle test — proceed only if all three hold:

1. Legitimacy and support: Who has authorized this, and does the authorizing
   environment (legislature, minister, council, board, public opinion) still
   back it as resources are committed?

2. Public value: What specific, describable good does this produce for
   citizens or society — safety, health, opportunity, trust, fairness —
   and for whom?

3. Operational capacity: Can the organization actually deliver it with
   current staff, technology, partners, and legal authority — or a
   credible plan to acquire them?
```

Shiri mai rauni yawanci yana faɗuwa a aƙalla ƙafa ɗaya: mai iya isarwa a fasaha amma babu umarni (gwajin raba bayanai da babu wanda ya sa hannu a kai); sananne amma ba za a iya isarwa ba (hidimar dijital da aka yi alƙawari amma babu ƙarfin injiniya); ko wanda aka ba da izini kuma mai iya isarwa amma babu ƙima a cikinsa (allon bayanai da babu wanda ke amfani da shi).

## Misali aiki

**Ƙaramar hukuma**: ƙungiyar dijital ta majalisa tana ba da shawarar kayan aikin AI na tantance buƙatun tallafin gidaje.

- *Halacci*: majalisar zartarwa ta amince da dabarar dijital-farko, amma zaɓaɓɓun membobin da ke da alhakin tsaron zamantakewa ba su sa hannu kan yanke shawara ta atomatik musamman ba — gibi ne, ba hasken kore ba.
- *Ƙimar jama'a*: saurin sarrafawa (alfanun da aka yi da'awa: kwanaki 10 zuwa kwanaki 2) ƙima ce ta gaske ne kawai idan ba a ƙi masu neman tallafi bisa kuskure ba; da'awar ƙima dole ta haɗa da daidaito, ba kawai sauri ba.
- *Ƙarfin aiki*: majalisar tana da masanin bayanai ɗaya kuma babu tsarin sa ido kan samfurin, don haka ba za a iya isar da juyawar kwanaki 2 da aka yi da'awa a yanzu a ƙimar kuskuren da aka bayyana ba.

Ƙafafu biyu cikin uku sun faɗi. Tsarin Moore ya ce: kada a ci gaba kamar yadda aka tsara — da farko a sami izini a fili ga yanke shawara ta atomatik kuma a gina ƙarfin sa ido, ko "ƙimar jama'a" da aka yi da'awa a takardar shaidar kasuwanci ƙagaggiya ce.

**Gwamnatin tsakiya**: hidimar shigar da takardu ta yanar gizo ta hukumar haraji tana da halacci mai ƙarfi (umarnin doka) da ƙarfin aiki mai ƙarfi (ƙungiyar da ke ba da hidima da aminci) amma ƙimar jama'a mai rauni idan karɓa ya yi ƙasa saboda waɗanda dijital ta ƙyale su — duba [haɗa kai da dijital (digital inclusion)](../haɗa-kowa-da-dijital/) — ana tura su zuwa hanyar da ba za su iya amfani da ita ba. Alwatikar tana bayyana abin da allon bayanai na isarwa kawai zai ɓoye.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Ƙimar jama'a ita ce babban ra'ayi da duk wannan ma'ajiya ke ƙarƙashinsa: [ƙimar kuɗi (value for money)](../ƙimar-da-aka-samu-daidai-da-kuɗi/) tana ba da gwajin tattalin arziki/inganci/tasiri na ko an yi amfani da albarkatu da kyau; [kuɗin damar da aka rasa a kashe kuɗin jama'a (opportunity cost in public spending)](../kuɗin-damar-da-aka-rasa-a-kashe-kuɗin-jamaa/) tana ƙididdige abin da kuɗin zai iya yi; kuma [ƙarin tasiri da nauyin mataccen sakamako (additionality and deadweight)](../ƙarin-tasiri-da-deadweight/), [sauya wuri da dangantawa (displacement and attribution)](../ƙauratarwa-da-danganta-tasiri/), da [nazarin yanayin kwatance (counterfactual analysis)](../nazarin-counterfactual/) tare suna gwada ko ƙimar da aka yi da'awa ta gaske ce maimakon zato. Ga injiniyoyi, alwatikar dabara kyakkyawan gwajin kafin-mutuwa ne ga kowane shawarar samfurin ɓangaren jama'a:

- Kafin tsara fasali, a tambayi wane ne ya ba da izini kuma ko wannan izini yana nan — fasalin da aka gina don minista da ya sauya wuri yana iya rasa ƙafar halaccinsa shiru.
- A ɗauki "shin za mu iya gina shi" da "shin ya kamata mu gina shi" a matsayin tambayoyi daban-daban da gaske; ƙarfin injiniya yana amsa ƙafar ta uku kawai ta alwatikar.
- Takardun buƙatun samfura na hidimomin jama'a ya kamata su bayyana da'awar ƙimar jama'a a fili, ba kawai labarin mai amfani ba, domin ƙimar mai amfani da ƙimar jama'a ba koyaushe iri ɗaya ba ne (duba [sakamako da fitarwa](../sakamako-da-fitarwa-bambancin-outcomes-da-outputs/)).

## Tarkuna

- **Ɗaukar ƙarfin aiki a matsayin isasshen dalili.** "Za mu iya gina shi" yana amsa ƙafa ɗaya ne kawai ta alwatikar; ƙungiyoyi masu ƙarfin isarwa akai-akai suna kai abubuwan da babu wanda ya ba da izini suna so kuma ba sa ƙirƙirar wani alheri na jama'a da za a iya kwatantawa.
- **Rikita halacci da doka.** Shiri na iya zama na doka amma ba shi da goyon bayan siyasa da jama'a da ake buƙata don ɗorewa ta wahalar lokacin isarwa; kariyar doka ba daidai take da umarni ba.
- **Zaton ƙimar jama'a ita ce duk abin da ma'aikatar da ke bayar da kwangila ta ce.** Samfurin Moore yana buƙatar da'awar ƙima ta zama mai iya gwaji a kan ainihin muradun 'yan ƙasa, ba kawai mai ba da kuɗi ya tabbatar ba — in ba haka ba tsarin ya rushe zuwa yiwa kai shaida.
