# Kuɗin Kowane Mai Cin Gajiya

Kuɗin kowane mai cin gajiya (cost per beneficiary) shi ne jimillar kuɗin shirin da aka raba da adadin mutanen da suka karɓi hidima — duk wanda aka taɓa, ko da yanayinsa ya canza da gaske ko a'a. Shi ne lambar inganci mafi sauri da ƙungiya za ta iya samarwa, domin "wa muka yi wa hidima" kusan koyaushe yana nan a tsarin sarrafa lamura, yayin da "wa aka taimaka" yawanci ba ya nan.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Masu ba da kuɗi suna neman kuɗin kowane mai cin gajiya kullum, kuma da dalilai masu ƙarfi: ana samun shi nan take, ana iya kwatanta shi a jerin shirye-shirye daban-daban, kuma yana da gaskiya game da isa ta yadda iƙirarin sakamako — waɗanda suke ɗaukar lokaci kafin a tabbatar kuma sun fi sauƙin ƙarawa girma — ba su da. UK Charities SORP (Statement of Recommended Practice), wanda ke jagorantar yadda ƙungiyoyin agaji ke ba da rahoto a ƙarƙashin FRS 102, yana buƙatar rahotannin shekara na masu amana su bayyana nasarori bisa manufofi, amma yawancin ƙananan ƙungiyoyin agaji har yanzu bayanan lissafin gudanarwarsu suna komawa kan kuɗin naúra na isa saboda suna da arha a samar kuma masu sauƙin tantancewa.

Haɗarin shi ne ɗaukar kuɗin kowane mai cin gajiya kamar yana amsa tambayar da ba zai iya amsawa ba: ko kuɗin ya yi aiki. Duba [kuɗin kowane sakamako](../kuɗin-kowane-sakamako/) don ma'aunin da yake amsa hakan da gaske, da [sakamako da abin da aka samar](../sakamako-da-fitarwa-bambancin-outcomes-da-outputs/) don bambancin tushe. Kuɗin kowane mai cin gajiya ma'aunin rarrabuwa ne kuma na isa mai inganci — yana gaya wa mai ba da kuɗi yadda kuɗi ke tafiya — amma ƙaramin kuɗin kowane mai cin gajiya na iya nufin ko dai ingantaccen aiki ko hidima mai laushi da ba ta canza komai.

## Lissafi

```
Kuɗin kowane mai cin gajiya = Jimillar kuɗin shirin / Adadin mutane na musamman da aka yi wa hidima

Bambanci:
Kuɗin kowane sakamako      = Jimillar kuɗin shirin / Adadin mutanen da suka cimma sakamakon da aka ayyana

Kuɗin kowane mai cin gajiya koyaushe ≤ kuɗin kowane sakamako, domin al'ummar sakamako ƙaramin ɓangare ce
(sau da yawa ƙarami) na al'ummar masu cin gajiya.
```

## Misali aiki

**Bankin abinci, shekara ɗaya da misalin kuɗin kowane sakamako**:

- Jimillar kuɗin shirin: £450,000
- Gidaje na musamman da aka yi wa hidima (fakiti uku ko fiye): 1,800

```
Kuɗin kowane mai cin gajiya = £450,000 / 1,800 = £250 ga kowane gida da aka yi wa hidima
```

A kwatanta ma'aunan biyu gefe-da-gefe:

| Ma'auni | Mai rarrabawa | Sakamako |
|---|---|---|
| Kuɗin kowane mai cin gajiya | gidaje 1,800 da aka yi wa hidima | £250 |
| Kuɗin kowane sakamako | gidaje 630 da suka cimma tsaron abinci | £714 |

Mai ba da kuɗi da ya ga £250 kaɗai na iya yanke hukunci cewa wannan ƙungiyar agaji ce mai inganci sosai. Mai ba da kuɗi da ya ga lambobin biyu zai iya yin tambaya mafi amfani: shin gibin tsakanin isa (1,800) da sakamako (630) gibin tattara bayanai ne, gibin ƙira, ko kuma cikakken nuni na yadda cimma tsaron abinci ke da wuya da taimakon abinci kaɗai?

**Ƙungiyar agaji ta horar da aiki, misali na bayani**: kuɗin kowane mai cin gajiya (wanda aka yi wa rajista) = £2,000; kuɗin kowane sakamako (aiki mai ɗorewa a watanni 6) = £11,000, saboda 18% na masu rajista ne kawai suke kammala shirin kuma su sami aiki mai ɗorewa. Lambobin biyu su bambanta sau biyar abu ne da ya zama ruwan dare duk inda yawan kammalawa ko dorewa ke ƙasa — ƙungiyar horo da bankin abinci iri ɗaya suke a tsari a nan.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Kuɗin kowane mai cin gajiya shi ne ma'auni na ƙa'ida a manhajar ƙungiyoyin sa-kai domin shi ne ma'aunin da ke fitowa daga rikodin mai cin gajiya ba tare da ƙarin aiki ba: ƙirƙiri lamari, rubuta hidima, ƙidaya layuka. Gina tsarin da ke tallafawa kuɗin kowane sakamako ma yana nufin da gangan ƙara wani abu na biyu na farko — lamarin sakamako, mai kwanan wata kuma wanda aka ayyana shi dabam da isar da hidima — da jure jarabar barin "an rufe lamari" ya tsaya maimakon "an cimma sakamako." Lokacin ƙayyade iyakar dandalin sarrafa tallafi ko CRM, a tambayi wane daga cikin ma'aunan biyu kowane allon sa ido yake nunawa da gaske, a sanya masa lakabi daidai; haɗa su a cikin tayal ɗaya na "tasiri" ɗaya ne daga mafi yawan dalilan matakin manhaja na tarkuna da ke ƙasa. Duba [rumbun bayanan kuɗin naúra](../rumbun-bayanan-kuɗin-rakaa/) don auna ma'aunan biyu da zarar an sanya musu lakabi daidai.

## Tarkuna

- **Gabatar da kuɗin kowane mai cin gajiya a matsayin tasiri.** Yana auna isa, ba canji ba. A sanya wa allon sa ido da rahotanni lakabin "kuɗin kowane mutum da aka yi wa hidima," ba "kuɗin kowane mutum da aka taimaka ba."
- **Ƙidaya sau biyu a tsakanin shirye-shirye.** Mutum da ya karɓi fakitin abinci da shawarar basussuka daga ƙungiyar agaji ɗaya mai cin gajiya ɗaya ne, ba biyu ba, idan mai rarrabawa yana nufin ya bayyana isa ta musamman; a yanke shawara a rubuta wace ƙa'ida ake amfani da ita.
- **Ɗaukar ƙaramar lamba a matsayin koyaushe mafi kyau.** Ƙungiyar abincin rana ta shiga-ta-fita za ta ci nasara koyaushe kan hidimar sarrafa lamura mai zurfi a kuɗin kowane mai cin gajiya, saboda taɓa wani a hankali yana ci ƙasa. Wannan ba ya faɗin komai game da wanda ke samar da canji mai ɗorewa a kowane fam.
- **Musanya masu rarrabawa a ɓoye tsakanin rahotanni.** Lambar kuɗin kowane mai cin gajiya da aka ambata a rahoton shekara ɗaya bisa "waɗanda aka yi wa rajista" kuma a na gaba bisa "waɗanda suka kammala" ba za a iya kwatanta ta shekara-da-shekara ba; a bayyana mai rarrabawa kowane lokaci.
