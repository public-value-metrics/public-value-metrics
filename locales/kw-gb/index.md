# Metryksow Talvedh Poblek

Kynsa rann a'n matematik, ensamplys ha rezonnans a dalvedh poblek, skrifys rag ynjynnoryon medhelweyth a dhrehav rag kuntellow governans ha sektor sokel a-dro dhe'n bys. Yn Kernewek rag redyoryon yn Kernow ha'n Rywvaneth Unys. Pub restren a drit unn vetryk po konsept: styr, perag y talvos, an matematik, ensampel oberys, kevrenn gans ynjynnerieth medhelweyth, trapow ha fentynyow.

Nowydh omma? Dallethewgh gans [talvedh poblek](locales/kw-gb/temow/gwerth-poblek/), [talvedh rag arghans](locales/kw-gb/temow/talvedh-rag-arghans/) ha [teori chanj](locales/kw-gb/temow/teori-chanj/) — an tri mennas may sev pub tra aral warnedha.

## Selyow talvedh poblek

- [Gwerth Poblek](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/public-value/) — Triant strategek Mark Moore: leghalita, kannas ha kapasyta oberyansek avel prov a dalvedh poblek
- [Talvedh rag Arghans (VFM)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/value-for-money/) — Ekonomieth, efisyans, effeythusnes — ha'n pedharves E kontestys, ekwita
- [Kost Chauns yn Spenna Poblek](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/opportunity-cost-in-public-spending/) — Pub punt a spenn rann yw punt na veu spennys war an towlen nessa wella
- [Skeul Diskont Sokel](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/social-discount-rate/) — Skeul diskont ow tiskynna an Lyver Gwyrdh, ha perag y teuth 3.5% dhe vos govyn politek bew
- [Pwysheans Dasrannans](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/distributional-weighting/) — Perag y vern punt moy dhe deylu kleth — ha fatel bris an Lyver Gwyrdh henna
- [Additivans ha Pols Marow](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/additionality-and-deadweight/) — A wrussa hemma hwarvos dre vyth? An govyn a dhiminh an moyha a gleymow effeyth
- [Displasyans ha Attribusyans](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/displacement-and-attribution/) — Pan yw dha waynans kolli nebonan aral, ha pan nyns yw dha dhevnydh leun
- [Dhivyskans Konterfaktek](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/counterfactual-analysis/) — Heb konterfaktek, heb dustuni — drehevel an bys heb an interventyon

## Arvreythyans ekonomek governansek

- [Arvreythyans Green Book (Model Pymp Kas)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/green-book-appraisal/) — Model pymp kas HM Treasury: an kas strategek, ekonomek, kenwerthek, arghansek ha gerya
- [Dhivyskans Kost-Prow Sokel (SCBA)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/social-cost-benefit-analysis/) — Prisya pub tra, dhe gonta an pyth na vo prisys gans marghasow nevra
- [Dhivyskans Kost-Effeythyans y'n Gwarnans](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cost-effectiveness-analysis-in-government/) — Kost rag pub unnenn sewyans, pan na yllir treylya sewyansow yn onest dhe arghans
- [Dasserghyans Dewisow Liesmeus (MCDA)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/multi-criteria-decision-analysis/) — Skorya dewisyow erbyn skeulyow pesys pan vo unn metryk a gudhya an treskavyansow
- [Prisya Da-vos (WELLBY)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/wellbeing-valuation/) — WELLBYs hag amendyans da-vos: prisya polici yn unnow bodh bewnans
- [Prisya dre Dewis Deklarys](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/stated-preference-valuation/) — Govyn orth tus pyth a baydhsens — prisya kondysyonel ha'y gudyn kredyans
- [Gwerthya dre Preferrans Diskwedhys](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/revealed-preference-valuation/) — Dyskudha talvedh dhiworth pyth a wra tus yn gwir, dhiworth prisyow hedonek bys yn medhodow kost kerdhes
- [Pris Skeus](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/shadow-pricing/) — Prisya pyth na'th eus pris marghas: karbon, termyn ha dafar na vo kenwerthys

## Talvedh sokel ha musur effeyth

- [Dehwelans Sokel war Ynworrans (SROI)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/social-return-on-investment/) — An skeul SROI, ha'n dyssiplin a'y gwith dhiworth bos niver marghasa
- [Teori Chanj](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/theory-of-change/) — Mappya an chain kawsek dhiworth gwrians dhe sewyans hir-termyn kyns musura tra vyth
- [Model Lojik](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/logic-model/) — Mewnbosow, gwriansow, produsyow, sewyansow, effeyth — an ostrenn atebyans a dowlen
- [Sewyansow erbynn Dyllyansow](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/outcomes-vs-outputs/) — Perag nyns yw "500 den a dheuth" an keth kleym avel "bewnansow 500 den a wellhas"
- [Deres Talvedh Sokel (Social Value Act)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/social-value-act/) — Dyled an Rywvaneth Unys a 2012 dhe gonsydra talvedh sokel yn kemeres poblek
- [Ostelyow Kost an Unnenn](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/unit-cost-databases/) — Bank Talvedh Sokel HACT ha Global Value Exchange: henwellow prisys kyns may na dhallytho den vyth dhiworth zero
- [Fordhow Arvreythyans Effeyth](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/impact-evaluation-methods/) — RCTs, dyvyrsyans-yn-dyvyrsyansow ha keskemmeres skor propensita rag polici na yllir y dhevnydhya yn labor
- [Arvreythyans Effeyth erbyn Arvreythyans Argerdh](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/impact-evaluation-vs-process-evaluation/) — A wrug ev ober, erbyn a wharva herwydh dhelinyans — dew govyn dihaval, dew vedhod dihaval

## Metryksow performans ha delivrans governansek

- [KPIs an Sektor Poblek](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/public-sector-kpis/) — Dewis tennoryon a berth lagha Goodhart
- [Skorkard Gwerth Poblek](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/public-value-scorecard/) — Treylya skorbord kesstowl Kaplan ha Norton rag kannas nyns yw prow
- [Atebelder Selys war Sewyansow (OBA)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/outcomes-based-accountability/) — "Results-Based Accountability" Mark Friedman: sewyansow poblans erbyn atebyans performans
- [Paymentys herwydh Sewyansow ha Bondys Impakt Sokyal (PbR/SIB)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/payment-by-results-and-social-impact-bonds/) — Paydhya rag sewyansow, nyns gwriansow — ha perag yw dhelinyans an kehedhyans an gwari oll
- [Produktivita Servyssow Poblek](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/public-service-productivity/) — Musurya kressyans produs yn servisow heb pris marghas, dhiworth medhodoleth an ONS ha wosa
- [Metrikow Lowender Sitisenyon](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/citizen-satisfaction-metrics/) — Bodh net, anketow fydhyans, ha'n bwlch ynter ansawor servis ha talvedh perthvedhys
- [Standardys Servys ha Metrikow Trensaksyon](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/service-standards-and-transaction-metrics/) — Savonegow servis stil GOV.UK: skeul gorfenna, kost rag pub treuslans, degemmeres didhital
- [Metryksow Fydhyans ha Leghalita](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/trust-and-legitimacy-metrics/) — Perag yw leghalita unn a dri previs Moore, ha fatel assay governansow hy musura

## Metryksow sektor sokel ha philanthropi

- [Kost rag Pub Sewyans](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cost-per-outcome/) — Ekonomieth unnenn a gharite: kost rag pub den gweresys yn gwir, nyns kost rag pub den tuchys
- [Kost rag Pub Kowethyer](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cost-per-beneficiary/) — Kesyn moy krev ha skonn a gost rag pub sewyans, ha perag y wovynn grantyoryon hwath
- [Talvos Kemmyskys](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/blended-value/) — Dehwelans arghansek, sokel hag amgylchynnek avel unn linenn isel dihyvarthys
- [Kost-effeythieth Altrewieth Effeythus](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/effective-altruism-cost-effectiveness/) — Rezonnans kost-rag-pub-bewnans-sawys stil GiveWell, ha'y hendhasow furv QALY
- [Skeul Kostow Hollek Kharite](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/charity-overhead-ratio/) — Perag yw govyn "pygemmys a dhe dhe'n gweres" an govyn kamm, ha grantyoryon a'n gwovynn hwath
- [Dehwelyans Budhek an Ri](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/donor-return-on-investment/) — Pyth a dhasgev ro-dhoner yn gwir, ha perag nyns yw ROI an gharite
- [Raportya Devethyow Grant (IRIS+)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/grant-outcomes-reporting/) — Metryksow IRIS+ ha standardya pyth a wovynn grantyoryon dhe wrantys rapportya
- [Talvedh Termyn Bolenegor](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/volunteer-time-value/) — Prisya lavur anpaydhys heb y dhilea na'y worgeskewi

## Talvedh poblek yn governans didhital

- [Savonek Servis Didhital](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/digital-service-standard/) — Pryns dhelinyans GOV.UK hag arvreythyans servis avel yet talvedh poblek
- [Kost rag Pub Trehans](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cost-per-transaction/) — Ekonomieth penn an kanel didhital, ha'y dro kovskrifow an moyha kemmyn
- [Sawyansow Treylyans Kanel](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/channel-shift-savings/) — Pyth a wharva yn gwir orth an linenn pellgowser ha'n konter pan a servis war-linenn
- [Kefrysans Didjital](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/digital-inclusion/) — Didhital gweresys, an bwlch didhital, ha'n talvedh kellys pan wra "didhital dre ragosodhek" ervyn
- [Governans avel Platform (GaaP)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/government-as-a-platform/) — Kesrannow rannys avel ynfrastrukter poblek: GOV.UK Notify, Pay, ha ekonomieth daverya
- [Gwerth Data Ygor](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/open-data-value/) — Prisya data heb pris, dhiworth estymyansow McKinsey bys studhyansow kas rag pub tebel-data
- [Gwerth Diogeleth Kiber y'n Sektor Poblek](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/public-sector-cybersecurity-value/) — Prisya dhiminhans peryl rag data dinasek a vensa terri y dhisklerya
- [Talvos AI yn Gwarnans](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/ai-in-government-value/) — Arvreythya degemmeres AI yn servisow poblek erbyn an keth barr talvedh rag arghans avel pub tra aral

## Da-vos, ekwita ha tennoryon sokel

- [Dewisyow rag GDP](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/gdp-alternatives/) — An Tennor Fordh Wir, Hapynnes Kenedhlek Hollek, ha dhallow-dall poblek GDP
- [Kevrennlyver Displegyans Denel (HDI)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/human-development-index/) — Hyr-vewnans, deskans ha enyans kesgemmysk yn unn niver — ha pyth a dhisplana
- [Menegva Bohosogneth Liesmeusek (MPI)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/multidimensional-poverty-index/) — Konta dyvlansow, nyns enyans hepken, dres yeghes, deskans ha savonegow bewnans
- [Bledhynyow Bewnans Amendys dre Da-vos (WELLBY)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/wellbeing-adjusted-life-years/) — An WELLBY: unnenn stil QALY rag polici may ma arghans bodh bewnans, nyns yeghes
- [Kevrennlyver Diwoesys Lies (IMD)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/index-of-multiple-deprivation/) — Rannans dyvlans ranndiryow byghan Pow Sows, ha fatel wra hi ledya arghans ha hevelepter
- [Metryksow Kapital Sokel](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/social-capital-metrics/) — Fydhyans, rosweythow ha kevrenn sivek avel alhwedh ekonomek heb linenn yn lyver
- [Kontyans Kapital Naturek](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/natural-capital-accounting/) — Gorra an amgylchynnans war an kovskrif, dhiworth Towlen Amgylchynnek 25 Bledhen an Rywvaneth Unys ha wosa
- [Ekwita Ynter-henedhyow ha Diskonting Sostalita](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/intergenerational-equity-and-sustainability-discounting/) — Perag y hyll diskonta an dyvenyth pell orth kenyver skeul posytiv hy skubya a'n dhivyskans

- [Metryksow Ruth yn Delivrans an Governans](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/flow-metrics-in-government-delivery/) — Lagha Little, WIP hag efisyans fluv devnydhys dhe lin kasow yn le bord sprint
- [Dyvas Teknegel avel Dhirywyans Talvedh Poblek](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/technical-debt-as-public-value-erosion/) — Penn-sommen ha usyans, hag ekonomieth kleves hir-termyn system boblek koth
- [Kost Perthyans Hollek yn TT an Governans (TCO)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/total-cost-of-ownership-in-government-it/) — Mentenans yw 50-80% a'n bil; fyll an pris prena naïv yn TT poblek
- [Byldya po Prena y'n Gwarnans](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/build-vs-buy-in-government/) — Keshes amendys war beryl gans an term a dhelayans prisys, rag sektor a bren an moyha
- [Kowlwiryans Prow](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/benefits-realization/) — Awdyta bos prowyow gwaytys an kas negys deskes yn gwir, wosa mos yn bew
- [Produktivita AI y'n Rann Poblek](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/ai-productivity-in-the-public-sector/) — Pyth a styr an fundh dustuni a produktivita developer AI rag bagasow ynjynnerieth an sektor poblek
