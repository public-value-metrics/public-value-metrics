# Ribar Zamantakewa daga Zuba Jari (SROI)

Ribar zamantakewa daga zuba jari (social return on investment) tsari ne na aunawa, ƙimantawa, da lissafin faffadan ra'ayin ƙima — ta zamantakewa, muhalli, da tattalin arziki — da bayyana shi a matsayin rabo da albarkatun da aka zuba, misali "£1.44 na ƙimar zamantakewa ga kowane £1 da aka zuba". An ƙirƙire shi don faɗaɗa dabarar lissafin kuɗi zuwa sakamakon da kasuwanni ba sa ba shi farashi, ba tare da rasa horon lissafi ba: kowace lamba a SROI dole ta kasance mai iya bin sawunta zuwa sakamakon da masu ruwa da tsaki suka ƙayyade, tushen shaida, da gyara a fili don abin da da ma zai faru ko da ba tare da shirin ba.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Social Value UK da Social Value International ne ke kula da SROI, magadan SROI Network, wanda littafinsa "A Guide to Social Return on Investment" (2012) har yanzu shi ne hanyar nazari ta tunani. Tsarin yana tsaye ne a kan ƙa'idoji bakwai — a haɗa masu ruwa da tsaki, a fahimci abin da ke canzawa, a ƙimanta abubuwan da ke da muhimmanci, a haɗa abin da ke da tasiri kawai, kar a yi iƙirari fiye da kima, a zama a bayyane, a tabbatar da sakamakon — kuma ƙa'ida ta biyar, "kar a yi iƙirari fiye da kima", ita ce wadda yawancin rahotannin SROI na zahiri suke kasawa a kanta. Rabon da aka samar ta tsallake gyaran tasirin da da ma zai faru (deadweight) da danganta tasiri (attribution) ba SROI ba ne; lambar tallace-tallace ce sanye da tufafin SROI. Injiniyoyin manhaja da ke gina kayan aikin bayar da rahoto don ƙungiyoyin agaji, kamfanonin zamantakewa, ko masu ba da kwangila su san bambancin, domin kayan aikin ko dai zai tilasta horon ko ya sauƙaƙa tsallake shi.

## Lissafi

SROI yana dogara ne da [ka'idar sauyi](../kaidar-sauyi/) don gano wane sakamako yake cikin iyaka, kuma yana bayyana su ta amfani da sarkar lissafi iri ɗaya da [samfurin dabaru](../samfurin-dabaru-na-shiri/):

```
SROI ratio = Present value of outcomes / Value of inputs

Process:
 1. Establish scope and identify stakeholders whose outcomes will be measured
 2. Map outcomes (a theory of change, evidenced with stakeholders, not assumed)
 3. Evidence outcomes and give them a value using financial proxies
 4. Establish impact: gross value − deadweight − attribution − displacement, then apply drop-off
 5. Calculate the SROI: net present value of impact ÷ value of inputs
 6. Report, use, and embed — the ratio is a communication device, not the endpoint
```

An rufe deadweight, danganta tasiri, da karkatar da tasiri a [ƙarin tasiri da tasirin da da ma zai faru](../ƙarin-tasiri-da-deadweight/) da [karkatar da tasiri da danganta shi](../ƙauratarwa-da-danganta-tasiri/); duka ukun suna wanzuwa don raba ainihin tasirin [yanayin da ba tare da shiri ba (counterfactual)](../nazarin-counterfactual/) daga jimillar sakamakon.

## Misali aiki

**Shirin samar da aiki na ƙaramar hukuma**: kuɗin shigarwa na shekara £250,000. Mutane sittin sun koma aiki mai ɗorewa; wakilin kuɗi na wannan sakamako (ƙaruwar jin daɗi, rage dogaro da tallafi, da harajin da aka samu a haɗe) £8,500 ne ga kowane mutum a shekarar farko — duba [rumbun bayanan kuɗin raka'a](../rumbun-bayanan-kuɗin-rakaa/) don inda ake samun irin waɗannan wakilai.

- Jimillar ƙimar sakamako: 60 × £8,500 = £510,000
- Ragewa deadweight (40% da alama za su sami aiki ba tare da shirin ba): £510,000 × 0.60 = £306,000
- Ragewa danganta tasiri (30% na sauran canjin yana faruwa ne saboda tallafin wasu hukumomi): £306,000 × 0.70 = £214,200
- Sakamakon shekara ta 2 a raguwar 30%: £214,200 × 0.70 = £149,940, an rage ƙimarsa a 3.5%/shekara (duba [kason rangwame na zamantakewa](../kason-rangwame-na-zamantakewa/)): £149,940 ÷ 1.035 = £144,870
- Jimillar ƙimar yanzu ta tasiri: £214,200 + £144,870 = £359,070
- **Rabon SROI: £359,070 ÷ £250,000 = 1.44**, ana bayar da shi a matsayin "£1.44 na ƙimar zamantakewa ga kowane £1 da aka zuba"

**Ƙungiyar agaji**: sabis na abokantaka na £60,000 yana rage kaɗaici ga tsofaffi 80, an ƙimanta a wakili na £1,100/mutum/shekara. Jimillar ƙima £88,000; bayan deadweight 35% da danganta tasiri 15%, tasirin ƙarshe £88,000 × 0.65 × 0.85 = £48,620 ne, rabon SROI na 0.81 — ƙasa da daidai-da-daidai, wanda sakamako ne mai kyau kuma mai amfani, ba gazawar rubutawa ba.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Na'urar ƙididdiga ta SROI da ke bari mai amfani ya shigar da adadin sakamako da ƙimar wakilai amma ba ta da filin tilas na deadweight, danganta tasiri, ko ka'idar sauyi da aka haɗa za ta samar da rabo da aka ƙara girma ta asali, domin tsallake gyare-gyare ita ce hanya mafi sauƙi. A gina horon cikin tsarin bayanai: kowane layin sakamako ya kamata ya nuna rukunin masu ruwa da tsaki, adadin da aka tabbatar, wakilin kuɗi tare da majiyarsa, da filayen deadweight/danganta tasiri da ba na zaɓi ba. Duba [sakamako da abin da aka samar (outcomes vs outputs)](../sakamako-da-fitarwa-bambancin-outcomes-da-outputs/) don bambancin da taswirar sakamakon SROI ke dogara da shi, da [samfurin dabaru](../samfurin-dabaru-na-shiri/) don sarkar da kayan aikin ya kamata ya yi kama da ita a tsarin bayanansa.

## Tarkuna

- **Tsallake deadweight da danganta tasiri.** Babban rabo ba tare da waɗannan gyare-gyare ba jimillar kuɗi ne, ba tasirin ƙarshe ba, kuma ƙa'idodin Social Value UK a bayyane suke buƙatar duka biyun.
- **Kwatanta rabo tsakanin ƙungiyoyi.** Rabon SROI yana dogara ne da zaɓin iyaka da wakilai da ake yi a kowane yanayi; ɗaukar rabon 4:1 daga rahoto ɗaya a matsayin "mafi kyau" da rabon 2:1 daga wani yana watsi da cewa zato ba a daidaita su ba kamar rabon lissafin kuɗi.
- **Ƙirga wakilai masu karo sau biyu.** Haɗa wakilin "rage kaɗaici" da na "ingantacciyar lafiyar hankali" ga masu amfana ɗaya zai iya ninka ƙimar canji ɗaya.
- **Tsallake haɗa masu ruwa da tsaki.** Ƙa'ida ta ɗaya tana buƙatar a ƙayyade sakamako tare da mutanen da ke fuskantarsu, ba mai nazari da ke gina samfurin ya ɗauka kawai ba.
