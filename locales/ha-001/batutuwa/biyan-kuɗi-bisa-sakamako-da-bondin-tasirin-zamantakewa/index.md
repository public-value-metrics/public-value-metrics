# Biyan Kuɗi Bisa Sakamako da Bondin Tasirin Zamantakewa (Payment by Results and Social Impact Bonds)

Biyan kuɗi bisa sakamako (payment by results, PbR) yana biyan mai bayar da hidima bisa sakamakon da aka tabbatar an cimma, ba ayyukan da aka yi ba. Bondin tasirin zamantakewa (social impact bond, SIB) wani tsari ne na ba da kuɗi na PbR inda masu zuba jari masu zaman kansu ko na agaji ke ba da kuɗin isar da hidima tun da farko kuma mai bayar da kwangila na gwamnati yana mayar musu da kuɗin — tare da riba — kawai idan sakamakon da aka auna ba tare da son kai ba ya kai ƙayyadaddun iyaka, ta haka yana mayar da haɗarin isarwa daga mai biyan haraji zuwa mai zuba jari.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

SIB na farko a duniya an ƙaddamar da shi a HMP Peterborough a watan Satumba 2010: Social Finance ta tara £5 miliyan daga masu zuba jari 17 don ba da kuɗin "One Service," wanda ke aiki da fursunonin gajeren hukunci (ƙasa da watanni 12) don rage komawa aikata laifi, tare da Ma'aikatar Shari'a (Ministry of Justice) da Big Lottery Fund suka amince su mayar wa masu zuba jari kuɗi kawai idan al'amuran sake yanke hukunci sun faɗi da aƙalla 7.5% a kan ƙungiyar kwatance ta ƙasa da aka daidaita. Ƙungiyar ƙarshe ta gwajin Peterborough ta rubuta raguwar sake yanke hukunci 9.7%, wanda ya wuce iyaka da sauƙi, kuma an mayar wa masu zuba jari kuɗi tare da riba. Tsarin ya yi muhimmanci domin ya warware wata matsalar bayar da kwangila ta musamman: gwamnati tana son biyan sakamako maimakon shigarwa, amma ba za ta iya ɗaukar haɗarin kuɗi na matakin da zai iya kasa aiki ba, don haka tsarin SIB ya mayar da wannan haɗari ga masu zuba jari da suke son ɗaukar nauyinsa. Government Outcomes Lab (GO Lab) a Blavatnik School of Government na Oxford yanzu yana kula da mafi cikakken ma'ajin shaida na jama'a kan aikin PbR da SIB a duniya, yana bin diddigin bonds na tasiri sama da 200 a duniya kuma yana wallafa bincike kan waɗanne siffofin tsari ke da alaƙa da nasara ko gazawa. Darasin da ma'ajin shaidar ke komawa gare shi akai-akai shi ne cewa *ma'aunin sakamakon da aka zaɓa*, da wanda ke ɗaukar haɗarin rasa shi, yana ƙayyade kusan komai game da yadda kwangilar PbR ke aiki a aikace.

## Lissafi

```
PbR payment = base payment (if any) + Σ (outcome achieved × unit price per outcome)

Social impact bond investor return:
  Investor outlay   = upfront capital funding service delivery
  Outcome payment   = commissioner pays only if outcome ≥ threshold, scaled by
                       how far above threshold performance lands
  Investor return   = outcome payments received − investor outlay
                       (a rate of return, often capped, reflecting risk taken)

Key design parameters that determine the whole contract's behaviour:
  Outcome metric        — must be an outcome, not an output (see outcomes-vs-outputs)
  Comparison/counterfactual — usually a matched cohort (see counterfactual-analysis)
  Payment threshold      — minimum improvement before any payment triggers
  Payment curve           — linear, stepped, or capped above the threshold
  Attribution/deadweight discount — see additionality-and-deadweight
```

## Misali aiki

**Peterborough One Service** (adadin na misali daga kimantawar da aka wallafa):

```
Investor capital raised:         £5,000,000
Cohort:                          ~3,000 short-sentence male prisoners over two cohorts
Threshold:                       ≥7.5% reduction in reconviction events vs matched
                                  national comparison group, or no payment
Cohort 1 result:                 8.4% reduction — below the contractual bar for that
                                  cohort alone under the original rules
Combined/final cohort result:    9.7% reduction — above threshold
Outcome payment:                 government (Ministry of Justice / Big Lottery Fund)
                                  pays per percentage point above threshold, funding
                                  investor repayment plus a return
```

**Kwangilar PbR ta ƙaramar hukuma (na misali)**: ana ba da kwangilar hidimar shiga tsakani a iyali a £4,000 ga kowane iyali da aka tura (biyan aiki) tare da £6,000 ga kowane iyali da ba shi da sake tura kariyar yara watanni 12 bayan rufewa (biyan sakamako). An tura iyalai 200, an rufe shari'o'i 150, 96 sun kasance ba tare da sake tura wa ba a watanni 12:

```
Activity payment  = 200 × £4,000 = £800,000
Outcome payment   = 96 × £6,000  = £576,000
Total contract cost = £1,376,000 for 96 confirmed sustained outcomes
Cost per confirmed outcome ≈ £14,333 (see cost-per-outcome)
```

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Biyan kuɗi bisa sakamako matsala ce ta daidaita ƙwarin gwiwa kafin ta zama matsalar bayanai, kuma tsarin bayanai shi ne inda wannan daidaito ke tsayuwa ko ya karye. Tabbatar da sakamako mai zaman kansa, mai bayyana ɓarna, shi ne dukkan wasan: mai bayar da kwangila da mai bayar da hidima suna da ƙwarin gwiwa masu adawa da juna kan yadda ake rubuta shari'a mai rikitarwa, don haka tsarin da ke rubuta sakamako yana buƙatar hanyar bin diddigin bincike, yarjejeniyar raba bayanai da mai tabbatarwa mai zaman kansa (sau da yawa wata hukuma dabam da mai bayar da hidima, wani lokaci hukumar ƙididdigar hukuma da ke daidaita da bayanan 'yan sanda ko tallafi), da bayar da juzu'i mara sauyawa na ma'anar sakamako — daidai da kuskuren "sake ayyana ma'auni" a [KPI na ɓangaren jama'a (public sector KPIs)](../manyan-alamomin-aiki-na-ɓangaren-jamaa/). Ƙididdigar dangantawa tana dogara da hanyoyin ƙungiyar da aka daidaita na [nazarin yanayin kwatance (counterfactual-analysis)](../nazarin-counterfactual/), waɗanda ke buƙatar lambar da za a iya maimaitawa da bincika, ba maƙunsar bayanai ta sau ɗaya ba. Kuma dole ne ma'aunin kansa ya zama sakamako na gaske, ba ayyukan wakilci ba — duba [sakamako da fitarwa](../sakamako-da-fitarwa-bambancin-outcomes-da-outputs/) — domin kwangilar PbR da ke biyan fitarwa kawai tana sake wa kuɗin aiki na yau da kullun suna da ƙarin kuɗin mu'amala. Inda ake ƙirƙirar samfurin dawowar zamantakewa ta SIB gaba, wannan kimantawa yawanci yana aro kai tsaye daga hanyar [ribar zamantakewa a kan jari (social return on investment)](../ribar-zamantakewa-daga-zuba-jari/).

## Tarkuna

- **Biyan sakamakon wakilci da ake sauƙin murɗawa**: "halartar zama" aiki ne da aka sanya wa sutura ta sakamako; a dage a kan ma'aunin da ke nuna ainihin sauyin da ake nema (komawa aikata laifi, aikin yi, tsayayyen matsuguni).
- **Rashin ingantaccen yanayin kwatance (counterfactual)**: ba tare da ƙungiyar kwatance da aka daidaita ba, ingantuwa na iya zama komawa ga matsakaici ko babban yanayi, ba tasirin shirin ba — duba [nazarin yanayin kwatance (counterfactual-analysis)](../nazarin-counterfactual/) da [ƙarin tasiri da nauyin mataccen sakamako (additionality-and-deadweight)](../ƙarin-tasiri-da-deadweight/).
- **Ƙiyasta ƙasa da kuɗin mu'amala da kimantawa**: tabbatarwa mai zaman kanta, haɗa bayanai, da gudanar da kwangila na tsare-tsaren PbR/SIB akai-akai suna kaiwa lambobi biyu na kashi cikin ƙimar kwangila — ma'ajin shaida na GO Lab ya rubuta wannan a matsayin dalilin da ke maimaituwa na dakatar da tsare-tsare.
- **Zaɓe ko "ajiye" abokan ciniki**: masu bayar da hidima da ake biya bisa sakamako suna da ƙwarin gwiwa kai tsaye na ba abokan cinikin da za su yi nasara ko ta yaya fifiko tare da yin watsi da mafi wuyar shari'o'i — a tsara matakan biyan kuɗi ko daidaita haɗin shari'o'i don magance shi.
