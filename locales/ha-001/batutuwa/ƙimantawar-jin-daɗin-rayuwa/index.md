# Ƙimantawar Jin Daɗin Rayuwa (WELLBY)

Ƙimantawar jin daɗin rayuwa (wellbeing valuation) tana ba da farashin tasirin manufa kai tsaye a sharuɗɗan gamsuwa da rayuwa, ta amfani da WELLBY (shekarar rayuwa da aka daidaita da jin daɗi) a matsayin raka'arta — WELLBY ɗaya yana daidai da canjin maki ɗaya a ma'aunin gamsuwa da rayuwa na 0–10, wanda aka ɗorar na shekara ɗaya. Madadin da HM Treasury ta amince da shi a hukumance ne ga ƙimanta kowane amfani ta yardar biya (willingness-to-pay).

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

"Wellbeing guidance for appraisal: supplementary Green Book guidance" na HM Treasury (2021, <https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/green-book-supplementary-guidance-wellbeing>) ya shigo da bayanan jin daɗin da aka bayyana cikin tantancewar gwamnatin tsakiya a hukumance, yana ba masu nazari hanyar ƙimanta sakamako — haɗin kai na zamantakewa, lafiyar hankali, aminci, shiga harkokin jama'a — wanda hanyoyin [stated preference](../ƙimantawa-ta-fifikon-da-aka-bayyana/) da [revealed preference](../ƙididdige-ƙima-ta-fifikon-da-aka-bayyana-a-aikace/) suke wahalar ba da farashi mai gamsarwa, domin mutane sau da yawa ba su da kyau wajen hasashen yadda kaya zai shafi gamsuwarsu da rayuwa. Jagorar, wadda aka haɓaka tare da What Works Centre for Wellbeing, tana ƙayyade ƙimar kuɗi da aka ba da shawara ga kowane WELLBY — £13,000 (farashin 2021, ana sabunta lokaci-lokaci) — wadda aka samo daga alaƙar da aka lura a manyan binciken jin daɗi (musamman Annual Population Survey na ONS, wanda ke yin tambayoyin jin daɗi huɗu na ONS4 tun 2011) tsakanin kuɗin shiga da gamsuwa da rayuwa, tana ba masu nazari ƙimar sauyawa zuwa fam idan ana buƙatar kwatancen da aka ƙimanta da sauran tantance-tantancen Green Book.

Hanyar tana da muhimmanci domin tana juya dabarar ƙimantawa ta yau da kullum: maimakon tambayar nawa mutane za su biya don sakamako (stated preference) ko samo ƙima daga ma'amalar kasuwa mai alaƙa (revealed preference), tana auna tasirin sakamakon kan gamsuwa da rayuwa da aka bayyana kai tsaye, tana kauce wa gibin tsakanin abin da mutane ke cewa suna so da abin da ke sa su fi kyau da gaske. Wannan kuma shi ne babban iyakarta — gamsuwa da rayuwa da aka bayyana kai tana shafuwa da daidaitawa da tasirin tsarawa wanda ƙwararre mai hankali dole ya sarrafa.

## Lissafi

```
WELLBY = 1 life-satisfaction point (0-10 scale) sustained for 1 person for 1 year

Total WELLBYs from a policy =
  Σ (change in life satisfaction score) × (number of people affected)
    × (duration in years, discounted at the social discount rate)

Monetized value = Total WELLBYs × value per WELLBY
  (HM Treasury recommended value: £13,000 per WELLBY, 2021 prices,
   subject to periodic revision — check current guidance before use)
```

Wannan ya bambanta da [shekarar rayuwa da aka daidaita da jin daɗi](../shekarun-rayuwa-da-aka-daidaita-da-jin-daɗi/) ta tattalin arzikin lafiya, wadda yawanci aka kafa ta a ma'aunin ingancin rayuwa mai alaƙa da lafiya (EQ-5D da makamantansa) maimakon gamsuwa da rayuwa ta gaba ɗaya; su biyun suna da alaƙa amma ba za a iya musanya su ba, kuma tantance-tantancen Green Book ya kamata su bayyana a fili wane ma'auni da hanyar samun bayani ne ke ƙarƙashin lambar WELLBY da aka ba da rahoto.

## Misali aiki

**Ƙaramar hukuma**: majalisa tana gudanar da shirin abokantaka na al'umma ga tsofaffin mazauna da ke kaɗai, tana hidima ga mutane 400. Binciken kafin/bayan da ke amfani da tambayar gamsuwa da rayuwa ta ONS4 yana nuna matsakaicin maki na mahalarta yana tashi daga 5.8 zuwa 6.5 — ribar maki 0.7 — wanda ya ɗore na tsawon shekaru 2 da aka ba da kuɗin shirin.

```
WELLBYs generated = 400 people × 0.7 points × 2 years = 560 WELLBYs
Monetized value = 560 × £13,000 = £7.28m
Programme cost = £450,000 over 2 years

Benefit-cost ratio ≈ £7.28m / £0.45m ≈ 16:1
```

Rabo mai tsayi haka ya kamata ya jawo bincike maimakon bikin — jagorar jin daɗi ta Green Book tana gargaɗi a bayyane game da ɗaukar ribar da aka bayyana kai ta ƙaramin samfuri a zahiri ba tare da duba tasirin zaɓe ba (shin mazauna mafi son jama'a, masu yuwuwar ingantuwa ne kawai suka shiga shirin?) kuma ba tare da rukunin kwatance ba; tantancewa mai kyau za ta cire canjin yanayin da ba tare da shiri ba da aka lura a waɗanda ba su shiga ba, duba [nazarin yanayin da ba tare da shiri ba (counterfactual)](../nazarin-counterfactual/).

**Gwamnatin ƙasa**: kwatanta shirye-shiryen samar da aiki biyu ta amfani da WELLBYs maimakon kuɗin shiga kaɗai yana ɗaukar cewa rashin aiki yana ɗauke da kuɗin jin daɗi fiye da asarar kuɗin shiga — binciken jin daɗi na Birtaniya yana gano akai-akai cewa rashin aiki yana rage gamsuwa da rayuwa fiye da yadda asarar kuɗin shiga kaɗai za ta nuna, saboda tasirin da ba na kuɗi ba na rasa tsari, manufa, da hulɗar zamantakewa. Shirin da aka tantance a ribar kuɗin shiga kaɗai zai ƙasƙantar da ƙimarsa idan aka kwatanta da wanda aka tantance ƙari a WELLBYs.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Ƙimantawar jin daɗi da wuya ta isa ga ƙungiyoyin injiniya kai tsaye, amma tana tsara abin da ake ayyana a matsayin "nasara" ga samfuran ɓangaren zamantakewa da ayyukan jama'a — dandamalin abokantaka na dijital, kayan aikin tantance lafiyar hankali, ko dandamalin al'umma ga mazauna da ke kaɗai ya kamata ya yi tsammanin za a auna tasirinsa a ƙarshe ta wannan hanya, wanda ke nufin nazarin samfuri dole ya rubuta *wa* aka isa da kuma na *tsawon lokaci nawa*, ba ƙirga amfani kaɗai ba. A gina kayan binciken jin daɗi (ONS4 ko makamantan da aka tabbatar) cikin tantance sabis daga farko maimakon a manna shi daga baya; sake ƙara tushen jin daɗi bayan sabis ya fara yana rasa kwatancen kafin/bayan gaba ɗaya. Duba [sakamako da abin da aka samar](../sakamako-da-fitarwa-bambancin-outcomes-da-outputs/) da [hanyoyin tantance tasiri](../hanyoyin-kimanta-tasiri/).

## Tarkuna

- **Rashin yanayin da ba tare da shiri ba ko rukunin kwatance.** Ribar jin daɗi ta kafin/bayan ba tare da sarrafa abin da da ma zai faru ba tana ƙara girman tasirin shirin; duba [nazarin yanayin da ba tare da shiri ba (counterfactual)](../nazarin-counterfactual/) da [ƙarin tasiri da tasirin da da ma zai faru](../ƙarin-tasiri-da-deadweight/).
- **Ƙananan samfura da aka zaɓa kai.** Binciken jin daɗi na mahalarta shiri da suka zaɓi shiga yana fuskantar son zuciya na zaɓe — mutanen da suka shiga suka kuma tsaya da alama sun riga sun kasance suna hawa sama.
- **Ɗaukar sauyawar £-ga-WELLBY a matsayin daidai.** Ƙimar kuɗin ƙa'idar manufa ce da aka samo daga regressions na kuɗin shiga-jin daɗi, ba farashin kasuwa ba; a yi amfani da ita don iya kwatantawa tsakanin tantance-tantancen Green Book, ba a matsayin iƙirari game da abin da jin daɗi "ya cancanta" ba.
- **Rikita WELLBYs da QALYs masu alaƙa da lafiya.** Su biyun suna auna gine-gine daban-daban a ma'auni daban-daban; duba [shekarun rayuwa da aka daidaita da jin daɗi](../shekarun-rayuwa-da-aka-daidaita-da-jin-daɗi/) don bambancin tattalin arzikin lafiya kuma kar a ɗauki matsakaicin biyun tare.
