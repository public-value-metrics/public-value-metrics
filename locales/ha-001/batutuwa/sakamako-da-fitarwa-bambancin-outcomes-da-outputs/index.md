# Sakamako da Fitarwa: Bambancin Outcomes da Outputs

Fitarwa (output) ita ce samfurin kai tsaye, wanda ake iya ƙidaya, na wani aiki — tana wanzuwa a daidai lokacin da aka kai hidima, ko da wane tasiri take da shi. Sakamako (outcome) shi ne sauyin da ke biyo baya ga mutane, wuri, ko tsarin da abin ya shafa. "Mutane 500 sun halarci taron neman aiki" fitarwa ce: gaskiya ce ko da babu ɗayansu da ya sami aiki. "Fatan samun aikin mutane 500 ya inganta" da'awar sakamako ce, kuma tana buƙatar shaidar sauyi, ba kawai shaidar halartar taro ba — rikicewar da ke haifar da rahotannin tallafi na ɓatarwa fiye da kusan kowane kuskuren aunawa a fannin.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Littafin Magenta na HM Treasury da masu bayar da tallafi kamar National Lottery Community Fund duka suna buƙatar rahoton sakamako musamman saboda fitarwa ita ce abin da shirye-shirye ke bayarwa da kansu: ba su da tsada a ƙidaya, a koyaushe akwai su, kuma a koyaushe suna da kyau. Ƙidayar fitarwa ba za ta taɓa raguwa ba sakamakon gazawar shirin — ƙarin zama da aka gudanar a koyaushe "ƙari ne", yayin da sakamako zai iya bayyana cewa shirin ba ya aiki. National Audit Office ya yi suka akai-akai ga shirye-shiryen gwamnati da ke bayar da rahoton matakan ayyuka kamar shaidar nasara ne; tsarin software da ke sauƙaƙa bayar da rahoton fitarwa kawai yana ƙarfafa wannan da kansa, domin fitarwa ba ta buƙatar tattara bayanan bin diddigi, sakamako kuwa yana buƙata.

## Lissafi

Babu dabara, amma akwai gwaji mai aminci don rarraba ma'auni:

```
Output test:  is it countable at the point of delivery, true even if the recipient is unaffected?
Outcome test: does it require a before/after or with/without comparison to be meaningful?

If a number can be true with zero benefit to anyone, it is an output.
```

Wannan yana cikin sarkar [samfurin dabaru (logic model)](../samfurin-dabaru-na-shiri/) mafi faɗi kuma yana dogara ne da alaƙar sakamako da aka ayyana a [taswirar sauyi (theory of change)](../kaidar-sauyi/); mayar da sakamako zuwa kuɗi yana amfani da hanyoyin da ke cikin [ribar zamantakewa a kan jari (social return on investment)](../ribar-zamantakewa-daga-zuba-jari/).

## Misali aiki

**Ƙaramar hukuma (tallafin aikin yi)**: fitarwa — mutane 500 sun halarci tarurrukan neman aiki. Sakamako — a bin diddigi na watanni 12, 140 cikin 500 (28%) suna cikin aiki mai dorewa (watanni 6 ko fiye). Ƙungiyar kwatance mai halaye iri ɗaya amma babu damar shiga shirin tana da tushen aikin yi na 15% a lokaci ɗaya. Ƙarin sakamako na net: 28% − 15% = maki 13 na kashi, don haka an ƙiyasta 500 × 0.13 = mutane 65 ƙari suna aiki waɗanda da ba su yi ba — sakamakon da za a iya dangantawa, daban da adadin halarta 500 ko ƙidayar aikin yi na ɗanye 140.

**Ƙungiyar agaji (ƙungiyar karatu)**: fitarwa — zaman karatu 1,200 da aka yi wa yara 300. Sakamako — matsakaicin shekarun karatu ya inganta da watanni 8 cikin lokaci na watanni 6, a kan tushen ci gaba na halitta da ake tsammani na watanni 6 a cikin watanni 6. Ƙarin sakamako na net: 8 − 6 = ƙarin ingantuwar shekarun karatu na watanni 2 ga kowane yaro da za a iya dangantawa ga shirin, ba cikakken adadin watanni 8 ba.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Rajistar abubuwan da suka faru da tsarin mu'amala suna auna fitarwa kusan kai tsaye — kallon shafi, zama, tikiti da aka rufe, ganawa da aka yi rajista — domin tsarin ne ke samar da su yayin aikinsa. Sakamako yana buƙatar samfurin bayanai da ke kama mutum ɗaya a wani lokaci na gaba a kan tushe ko kwatance, wanda dole ne a tsara da gangan: binciken bin diddigi, rajistar gudanarwa da aka haɗa, ko ƙungiyar kwatance. Kayan aikin rahoto da ke goyon bayan na farko kawai zai karkatar da ƙungiya a hankali zuwa rahoton fitarwa kaɗai ko da wane buƙata mai bayar da tallafi ya yi. Duba [samfurin dabaru (logic model)](../samfurin-dabaru-na-shiri/) don inda sakamako ke zaune a sarkar alhaki, [kuɗi ga kowane sakamako (cost-per-outcome)](../kuɗin-kowane-sakamako/) don mayar da wannan bambanci zuwa ma'aunin kuɗin naúra, da [KPI na ɓangaren jama'a (public sector KPIs)](../manyan-alamomin-aiki-na-ɓangaren-jamaa/) don babban tsarin zaɓar ma'auni.

## Tarkuna

- **Bayar da rahoton fitarwa kamar sakamako ne.** "Mutane 500 sun halarta" yana nuna alfanu ba tare da nuna shi ba; a rubuta halarta a fili a matsayin fitarwa.
- **Rashin tushe ko ƙungiyar kwatance.** Adadin sakamako ba tare da yanayin kwatance ba — duba [nazarin yanayin kwatance (counterfactual analysis)](../nazarin-counterfactual/) — ba zai iya raba tasirin shiri da abin da da ya faru ko ta yaya ba.
- **Inganta ma'aunin da aka ba da kuɗi saboda shi.** Idan an haɗa kuɗi da yawan fitarwa, ƙungiyoyin isarwa suna haɓaka halarta fiye da sauyi mai dorewa bisa hankali, kuskuren dokar Goodhart ne.
- **Wanke sakamako.** Sake yiwa ma'aunin fitarwa lakabi da kalmomin da suka yi kama da sakamako ("sakamakon haɗin kai: masu halarta 500") ba tare da wani auna bin diddigi a bayansa ba.
