# Kuɗin Kowace Ma'amala

Kuɗin kowace ma'amala (cost per transaction) shi ne babban ma'aunin tattalin arzikin naúra na hidimar dijital ta gwamnati: jimillar kuɗin isar da wata hanya, da aka raba da adadin ma'amaloli da aka kammala ta cikinta. Shi ne lambar farko a tsohon GOV.UK Performance Platform, kuma shi ne lambar da ta ba da kuɗin shekaru goma na zuba jari na "digital by default" — wanda shi ne daidai dalilin da ya sa shi ma ma'aunin da ya fi saurin yin wasa da shi.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Digital Efficiency Report na Cabinet Office na 2012 ya bayyana kwatancen kuɗin hanyoyi da kalmomin da suka tsaya: an gano ma'amaloli na dijital suna ci kusan sau 20 ƙasa da na waya da kusan sau 50 ƙasa da na fuska-da-fuska, tare da lambobin gwamnatin gida na bayani na kusan £0.15 ga kowace ma'amalar yanar gizo da £2.83 ta waya da £8.62 ta fuska-da-fuska. Wannan kwatancen guda ya zama hujjar sake tsara hidimomi 25 na misali da aka ambata a Government Digital Strategy, da kowace shawarar kasuwanci ta sashe da ta ambaci tanadin sauya hanya tun daga lokacin. Lambar tana da amfani sosai a matsayin alamar girman adadi, amma rabon ya dogara gaba ɗaya da abin da aka ƙidaya a kowane ɓangare: kuɗin hanyar waya mai adalci ya haɗa da ma'aikatan cibiyar kira, kwangilar waya, horo da wuri; kuɗin dijital mai adalci ya haɗa da ɗaukar nauyin sabar, albashin ƙungiyar samfur mai gudana, lokacin teburin tallafi don tafiye-tafiyen da suka gaza, da hanyar taimakon dijital da batu na 5 na [ma'aunin hidimar dijital](../maaunin-hidimar-dijital/) ke buƙata. A cire isassun waɗannan daga ɓangaren dijital kuma kowace hidima tana kama da arha.

## Lissafi

```
Kuɗin kowace ma'amala = jimillar kuɗin hanyar da aka raba / ma'amaloli da aka kammala

Jimillar kuɗin hanyar da aka raba ya kamata ya haɗa da:
  + ɗaukar nauyin sabar da kayayyakin more rayuwa
  + kuɗin ƙungiyar samfur/injiniya/tallafi (an rarraba a lokaci)
  + kuɗin abun ciki da ƙirar hidima (an rarraba a lokaci)
  + kuɗin taimakon dijital / tallafin samun dama
  + kuɗin buƙatar gazawa (masu amfani da suka gaza a dijital kuma suka koma waya)
  − kuɗin gini na lokaci ɗaya an rarraba shi a tsawon rayuwar hidima da ake tsammani,
    ba a kashe duka a shekara ta ɗaya ba

Dabarar lissafi ta gama-gari:
  "Kuɗin ƙarshe na kowace ma'amala" (ɗaukar nauyin sabar kawai, da zarar an gina)
  ana ambatonsa kamar "matsakaicin kuɗin kowace ma'amala" (jimillar kuɗi har
  da ƙungiyar da ke ci gaba da ginawa da gudanar da shi). Biyun na iya
  bambanta sau 10 ko fiye ga hidima mai babbar ƙungiyar isarwa mai aiki.
```

## Misali aiki

**Hidimar sabunta harajin ababen hawa**: ma'amaloli miliyan 4/shekara.

```
Lambar ƙarshe kaɗai (dabarar):
  Ɗaukar nauyin sabar + sarrafa biyan kuɗi kawai = £180,000/shekara
  Kuɗin kowace ma'amala = 180,000 / 4,000,000 = £0.045
  → lambar da aka ambata a shawarar kasuwanci

Cikakkiyar lamba (ta gaskiya):
  Ɗaukar nauyin sabar + biyan kuɗi         £180,000
  Ƙungiyar samfur/injiniya (FTE 8)          £720,000
  Teburin tallafi (ma'amaloli da suka gaza/aka tambaya)   £310,000
  Layin waya na taimakon dijital            £140,000
  Jimilla                                   £1,350,000
  Kuɗin kowace ma'amala = 1,350,000 / 4,000,000 = £0.3375

Cikakkiyar lambar har yanzu tana da arha kusan sau 8 fiye da £2.83
na kwatancen hanyar waya daga Digital Efficiency Report — ainihin
tanadi mai kariya — amma sau 7.5 sama da lambar ƙarshe kaɗai da aka
ambata a gajeriyar sigar. Lambobin biyu "gaskiya" ne; ɗaya kawai ne
za a iya kwatanta da kuɗin hanyar waya da ake sanya ta a gabansa.
```

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Kuɗin kowace ma'amala shi ne wurin da shawarwarin gine-gine suke zama lambar kuɗi: hidimar da ke ƙaruwa ta atomatik cikin tsabta kuma ba ta buƙatar shiga tsakani ta hannu tana saukar da wannan lambar a kan lokaci; wadda ke samar da yawan tikitin tallafi daga yanayin kuskure masu rikitarwa tana ƙara ta ko da ingancin sabar. Shi ne ma'auni abokin ma'aunin batu na 10 na [ma'aunin hidimar dijital](../maaunin-hidimar-dijital/) ("ayyana abin da nasara take nufi, ka buga bayanan aiki") da na [ma'aunin hidima da ma'aunin ma'amala](../ƙaidodin-hidima-da-maaunan-muamala/), wanda ke bayyana cikakken saitin KPI da wannan lambar ke ciki. Yana kuma shiga kai-tsaye cikin lissafin [tanadin sauya hanyar hidima](../tanadin-sauya-hanyar-hidima/) kuma ya kamata a daidaita shi da [jimillar kuɗin mallaka a IT na gwamnati](../jimillar-kuɗin-mallaka-a-it-na-gwamnati/) don kada kuɗin dandamali da hidimomin da aka raba su faɗi a ɓoye.

## Tarkuna

- **Kuɗin ƙarshe da aka sanya wa tufafin matsakaici**: ambaton kuɗin ɗaukar nauyin sabar kawai da zarar an gina hidima, tare da bar ƙungiyar da ke ci gaba da kulawa, maimaitawa da tallafawa — duba misali aikin da ke sama.
- **Cire kuɗin taimakon dijital**: hanya ba ta bin "digital by default" ba, kuma ba a kama ainihin kuɗinta ba, idan hanyar waya/takarda da [haɗa kowa da dijital](../haɗa-kowa-da-dijital/) ke buƙata an ƙididdige kuɗinta dabam ko an yi watsi da ita.
- **Yin watsi da buƙatar gazawa**: ma'amaloli da suka fara a dijital kuma suka gaza, suna haifar da kiran waya ko fom na takarda ko ta yaya, kuɗin hanyar dijital ne, ba na hanyar da ta kama gazawar ba.
- **Kwatanta ma'amaloli masu sarƙaƙiya daban-daban tsakanin hanyoyi**: kiran waya yana ɗaukar mafi yawan lamura masu wahala (masu dogaro da yawa, gyaran kuskure, masu nema masu rauni); kwatanta matsakaicin kuɗin waya da matsakaicin kuɗin dijital yana ƙara girman rabon sai dai idan an daidaita cakuɗar ma'amaloli.
