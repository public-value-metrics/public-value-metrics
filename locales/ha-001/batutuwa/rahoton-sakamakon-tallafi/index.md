# Rahoton Sakamakon Tallafi (IRIS+)

Rahoton sakamakon tallafi (grant outcomes reporting) aikin ne da masu karɓar tallafi ke ba masu ba da kuɗi rahoton ma'aunin sakamako na ƙa'ida, masu kwatantuwa — maimakon kowanne mai ba da kuɗi ya ƙirƙiri samfurin rahotonsa na musamman. IRIS+, wanda Global Impact Investing Network (GIIN) ke kulawa, shi ne ma'auni da aka fi karɓa: kundin ma'aunin aikin zamantakewa, muhalli, da kuɗi da aka riga aka ayyana wanda masu zuba jari na tasiri da, ƙara yawa, ƙungiyoyin bayar da tallafi ke buƙata ko ba da shawarar masu karɓar tallafi su yi amfani da su.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Kafin rahoto na ƙa'ida, kowane gidauniya yana tambayar masu karɓar tallafi alamomi daban-daban a tsari daban, kuma ƙungiyar agaji matsakaiciya mai masu ba da kuɗi goma za ta iya gudanar da tsarin rahoto goma a lokaci guda don aiki mai haɗuwa — sanannen abin da ke haifar da nauyin rahoto da daidaita sakamakon tallafi ke wanzuwa don rage. IRIS+ yana magance wannan ta hanyar ba masu ba da kuɗi da masu karɓa harshe na gama gari: Rukunonin Ma'aunin Tushe (Core Metrics Sets) da aka haɗa ta jigo (misali gidaje masu araha, samun makamashi mai tsabta, haɗa kowa cikin harkokin kuɗi), kowane ma'auni an ayyana shi daidai har "ayyuka da aka ƙirƙira" ko "gidaje da aka yi wa hidima" suna nufin abu ɗaya duk wanda ya ruwaito shi, kuma an daidaita su da Manufofin Ci Gaba Mai Dorewa na MDD (SDGs) don mai ba da kuɗi ya iya haɗa bayanan matakin mai karɓa zuwa labarin SDG na matakin jerin shirye-shirye. GIIN yana ba da rahoton cewa kusan rabin masu zuba jari na tasiri suna amfani da ma'aunin IRIS da mafi yawan manajojin asusu, bankuna, da cibiyoyin kuɗin ci gaba da ke aiki a fannin.

Ƙa'idar tana da muhimmanci musamman inda take haɗuwa da [sakamako da fitarwa](../sakamako-da-fitarwa-bambancin-outcomes-da-outputs/): IRIS+ yana tura rahoto zuwa ma'aunin sakamako da tasiri da aka ayyana maimakon duk abin da tsarin sarrafa shari'ar mai karɓa ya yi rikodi, wanda shi ne daidai gibin da [kuɗin kowane sakamako](../kuɗin-kowane-sakamako/) da [kuɗin kowane mai amfana](../kuɗin-kowane-mai-cin-gajiya/) ke bayyanawa.

## Lissafi

Rahoton sakamakon tallafi tsari ne da hanya, ba dabara ba:

```
1. Funder selects a Core Metrics Set relevant to the grant's theme
   (e.g. IRIS+ "Financial Inclusion" or "Sustainable Agriculture")
2. Each metric has a fixed definition, unit, and calculation method
   published by GIIN — not invented per-funder
3. Grantee reports against the same metric definitions across all
   its funders using that standard, cutting duplicate reporting effort
4. Funder aggregates grantee-level metrics into portfolio-level reporting,
   comparable year over year and across grantees using the same metric
```

Ribar inganci ta haɗin gwiwa ce: tara N masu ba da kuɗi × M masu karɓa zuwa harshe ɗaya na gama gari yana mayar da alaƙar rahoto ta musamman N×M zuwa kusan taswirori N+M a kan ma'auni ɗaya.

## Misali aiki

**Mai karɓar tallafi mai masu ba da kuɗi uku, kafin ƙa'ida**: yana ruwaito "mutane da aka yi wa hidima" ga Mai Ba da Kuɗi 1 ta amfani da ma'anar ƙidayar kai, "masu amfana da aka isa" ga Mai Ba da Kuɗi 2 ta amfani da ma'anar gida, da "mutane da abin ya shafa" ga Mai Ba da Kuɗi 3 ta amfani da ma'anar sashen hidima (don haka mutum ɗaya da ya ziyarta sau biyu ana ƙidayarsa sau biyu). Rahotanni uku, lambobi uku, babu mai kwatantuwa, kuma babu mai kwatantuwa da lambobin wani mai karɓa ko da a cikin jerin shirye-shiryen mai ba da kuɗi ɗaya.

**Mai karɓa ɗaya a ƙarƙashin IRIS+**: yana ruwaito bisa ma'aunin mutane-da-aka-isa na IRIS+ da aka ayyana tare da ma'aunin sakamako da aka ayyana daga Rukunin Ma'aunin Tushe mai dacewa, yana amfani da hanyar lissafi da GIIN ya wallafa ga duka biyun. Dukan masu ba da kuɗi uku yanzu suna karɓar lamba ɗaya, da aka lissafa ta hanya ɗaya, kuma za su iya kwatanta kuɗin wannan mai karɓa a kowace naúrar da IRIS+ ya ayyana da sauran masu karɓa a jerin shirye-shiryensu ta amfani da ma'auni iri ɗaya — kwatankwacin, a ma'aunin kayayyakin rahoto, na samun [bayanan kuɗin naúrar](../rumbun-bayanan-kuɗin-rakaa/) na haɗin gwiwa.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Dandamali na sarrafa tallafi ya kamata su ɗauki masu gane ma'aunin IRIS+ a matsayin maɓallin waje (foreign key), ba rubutu na kyauta ba: adana lambar ma'aunin da aka wallafa tare da ƙimar da mai karɓa ya ruwaito (maimakon filin da aka ƙirƙira a gida mai suna "masu amfana") shi ne ke ba da damar tara bayanai tsakanin masu ba da kuɗi da jerin shirye-shirye daga baya ba tare da aikin tsabtace bayanai ba. Inda dandamali dole ya tallafa wa masu ba da kuɗi da ba su karɓi IRIS+ ba, ƙirar da ta dace ita ce barin ma'aunin gida a daidaita shi da mafi kusancin ma'anar IRIS+ maimakon tilasta kowane mai ba da kuɗi zuwa ma'aunin nan da nan — kwatantuwa tana inganta a hankali yayin da ƙarin hoton ke dacewa da masu gane na haɗin gwiwa. Duba batun 'yar'uwa [kuɗin kowane sakamako](../kuɗin-kowane-sakamako/) don abin da ya kamata a yi amfani da lambobin da aka ruwaito wajen lissafawa da zarar an tattara su.

## Tarkuna

- **Ɗaukar karɓar IRIS+ a matsayin kwatantuwa ta atomatik.** Masu karɓa biyu na iya ruwaito bisa ma'aunin IRIS+ ɗaya kuma har yanzu ba su kwatantu ba idan ingancin bayanansu ko zaton yanayin da ba a samu shiga ba (counterfactual) ya bambanta; ƙa'idar tana gyara ma'anoni, ba tsauraran aunawa ba.
- **Ma'aunin "daidai da IRIS" da mai ba da kuɗi ya ƙirƙira.** Ma'auni da kawai harshen IRIS+ ya yi wahayi gare shi amma ba ainihin ma'anar da aka wallafa ba yana sake gabatar da rarrabuwar da ƙa'idar ke wanzuwa don warwarewa.
- **Gajiyar rahoto daga zaɓi fiye da kima.** Buƙatar mai karɓa ya ruwaito bisa dukan Rukunin Ma'aunin Tushe lokacin da biyu ko uku kawai suna dacewa da yanke shawara yana sake ƙirƙirar matsalar nauyi a cikin kunshin ƙa'ida.
- **Rashin ma'aunin sakamako kwata-kwata.** IRIS+ ya ƙunshi ma'aunin fitarwa zalla da yawa (misali ƙidayar mutane da aka yi wa hidima); zaɓar waɗannan kawai, ba ma'aunin matakin sakamako ko ɗaya ba, yana haifar da rahoto mai siffar [kuɗin kowane mai amfana](../kuɗin-kowane-mai-cin-gajiya/) ƙarƙashin sunan rahoton sakamako.
