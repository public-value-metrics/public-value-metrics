# Ƙimar da Aka Samu Daidai da Kuɗi (VFM)

Ƙimar da aka samu daidai da kuɗi (value for money) ita ce gwajin hukuma na ɓangaren jama'a na Birtaniya don sanin ko kashe kuɗi yana cimma mafi kyawun daidaiton kuɗi da amfani da ake da shi. Green Book ta HM Treasury tana tsara ta ta "E" uku — tattali (economy), inganci (efficiency), da tasiri (effectiveness) — tare da adalci (equity) ana ƙara jayayya a kansa a matsayin na huɗu. Kowace shari'ar kasuwanci ta ɓangaren jama'a da ke tsira daga bincike dole ta amsa duka ukun a fili, ba kawai ta yi iƙirarin cewa kashe kuɗin "ya cancanta" ba.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

VFM ba ma'anar "mai arha" ba ce. Green Book (HM Treasury, bugun 2022) a bayyane take cewa sayen zaɓi mafi ƙarancin kuɗi (tattali) ba tare da tabbatar yana samar da sakamakon da aka nufa ba (tasiri) kuskure ne gama-gari kuma mai tsada — sayen da ke ceton 10% a kuɗin raka'a amma yana isar da tasiri da bai kai 40% ba ƙima ce mafi muni, ba mafi kyau ba. Tsarin E uku yana tilasta shari'ar kasuwanci ta raba hanyoyin kasawa guda uku daban-daban da gaske: biyan kuɗi da yawa don shigarwa, ɓarnatar shigarwa wajen sauya su zuwa abin da aka samar, da samar da abubuwan da ba su koma sakamakon da kowa ya so ba. Sarrafa kashe kuɗin gwamnatin Birtaniya — wuraren amincewa na Treasury, nazarin ƙimar da aka samu daidai da kuɗi na National Audit Office (NAO), da tantance jami'an lissafi na ma'aikata — an gina su a kewaye da wannan gwaji na sassa uku, don haka shari'ar kasuwancin injiniya da ke magana kan kuɗi kawai (tattali) za ta kasa a bincike ko da fasahar tana da kyau.

"E na huɗu", adalci, ana jayayya a kansa daidai saboda yana iya karo da sauran uku: hanya mafi inganci ta isar da sabis a ƙasa ba kasafai ta fi adalci ba, domin tattara isarwa inda ya fi arha a kai ga 'yan ƙasa sau da yawa yana nufin rashin hidima ga waɗanda suka fi wahalar isa. Sake dubawar Green Book na 2020 ya mayar da martani ga zargi (har da daga Treasury Select Committee na 2020 da IPPR North) cewa tsantsar rabon kuɗi-da-amfani yana fifita yankunan da suka riga suka bunƙasa a tsari, ta hanyar buƙatar tantancewa su magance tasirin rabon amfani a fili — duba [ɗora nauyi bisa rabon amfani](../auna-rabon-faida-bisa-matsayin-mutane/).

## Lissafi

VFM ba rabo ɗaya ba ne amma binciken sassa uku (ko huɗu) ne, ana yin sa bi da bi:

```
Economy:       Are inputs purchased at the lowest reasonable cost for the
               required quality?  (£ per unit of input)

Efficiency:    How well are inputs converted into outputs?
               (outputs / inputs, e.g. cases processed per caseworker-hour)

Effectiveness: Do the outputs actually produce the intended outcomes?
               (outcomes achieved / outcomes intended)

[Equity]:      Are costs and benefits distributed fairly across the
               population, or concentrated on those least in need?
```

Kasawar VFM na iya faruwa a kowane mataki daban: sayen da ya yi tattali tare da isarwa mara inganci; isar da abin da bai dace ba da inganci; sakamako mai tasiri da aka saya a kuɗi mai yawa. Duba [ma'aunin aikin ɓangaren jama'a (KPIs)](../manyan-alamomin-aiki-na-ɓangaren-jamaa/) don yadda waɗannan ke zama alamomin da za a iya aunawa, da [nazarin ingancin kuɗi a gwamnati](../nazarin-ingancin-kuɗi-a-gwamnati/) don hanyar kwatanta ta hukuma.

## Misali aiki

**Cibiyar kiran ƙaramar hukuma**: majalisa tana kwatanta zaɓuɓɓuka biyu don sabon tsarin sarrafa shari'a.

- *Zaɓi A*: lasisi £600,000 (mafi arha da ake da shi), amma ma'aikata har yanzu suna matsakaicin mintuna 22 ga kowace shari'a domin tsarin aiki yana buƙatar sake shigar da bayanai da hannu tsakanin tsare-tsare — inganci mara kyau.
- *Zaɓi B*: lasisi £900,000, tsarin aiki da aka haɗa, ma'aikata suna matsakaicin mintuna 9 ga kowace shari'a.

Tattali kaɗai yana fifita A (£300,000 mai arha). Amma a shari'o'i 40,000/shekara, A yana kashe 40,000 × 22/60 = awanni 14,667 na ma'aikata; B yana kashe 40,000 × 9/60 = awanni 6,000. A cikakken kuɗin ma'aikaci na £28/awa, A yana kashe £410,667/shekara a lokacin ma'aikata da £168,000/shekara na B — gibin inganci na £242,667/shekara wanda ya rufe bambancin tattali na £300,000 na farko cikin watanni 14. VFM tana fifita B da zarar an ƙirga inganci, ba A ba.

**Tallafin isarwa na ƙungiyar agaji**: mai ba da tallafi yana kwatanta tallafi na £50,000 da ya cimma sanya mutane 200 a aiki (£250/mutum — tattali mai kyau a zahiri) da tallafi na £120,000 da ya cimma sanya 350 waɗanda suka dawwama sama da watanni 12 idan aka kwatanta da na tallafin farko, rabi sun lalace cikin watanni 3. Tasiri — sakamako mai ɗorewa — yana juya jerin VFM na zahiri: ainihin kuɗin kowane sanya a aiki *mai ɗorewa* £250 ÷ 0.5 = £500 ne ga tallafin farko, idan aka kwatanta da £120,000/350 ≈ £343 ga na biyu.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

VFM tana ba ƙungiyoyin injiniya horo don tsara shari'o'in kasuwancin fasaha yadda ayyukan kuɗi da bincike za su karanta su da gaske:

- A bayyana tattali, inganci, da tasiri a matsayin layukan daban a shari'ar kasuwanci, ba lambar "ƙima" ɗaya da aka gauraya ba — mai bita da aka horar da Green Book zai nemi daidai wannan rarrabawa.
- A yi hankali da inganta kuɗin saye (tattali) da lalata haɗin kai da ingancin tsarin aiki, ceton ƙarya da ya fi yawa a IT na gwamnati (duba [jimillar kuɗin mallaka a IT na gwamnati](../jimillar-kuɗin-mallaka-a-it-na-gwamnati/) da [gina ko saye a gwamnati](../gina-ko-saya-a-gwamnati/)).
- Tasiri yana buƙatar bayanan sakamako, ba ƙirga abin da aka samar kaɗai ba — a haɗa ma'aunin isarwa da [sakamako da abin da aka samar](../sakamako-da-fitarwa-bambancin-outcomes-da-outputs/) da tantancewa ta gaske ta [nazarin yanayin da ba tare da shiri ba (counterfactual)](../nazarin-counterfactual/) maimakon ɗaukar cewa abin da aka samar yana nuna sakamako.
- Idan tsari yana hidima ba daidai ba tsakanin yankuna ko rukunoni, tambayar adalci ƙin yarda ne mai halacci na VFM, ba "abin da ake so kawai" daban ba — duba [haɗa kowa da kowa a dijital](../haɗa-kowa-da-dijital/).

## Tarkuna

- **Daidaita VFM da farashi mafi ƙasa.** Tattali kashi ɗaya bisa uku ne (ko huɗu) na gwajin; Green Book tana gargaɗi a bayyane game da ƙa'idodin saye na "mafi ƙarancin kuɗi" waɗanda ke watsi da inganci da tasiri.
- **Auna abin da aka samar a kira shi sakamako.** Adadin shari'o'in da aka sarrafa (inganci) ba daidai yake da shari'o'in da aka warware da kyau (tasiri) ba; duba [sakamako da abin da aka samar](../sakamako-da-fitarwa-bambancin-outcomes-da-outputs/).
- **Ɗaukar adalci a matsayin na zaɓi.** Tun sabunta Green Book na 2020, ana nufin a tantance tasirin rabon amfani tare da E uku na gargajiya, ba a ƙara shi daga baya ba; sake ƙara shi bayan an amince da shari'ar kasuwanci ya fi wahala fiye da haɗa shi daga farko.
- **Kwatanta zaɓuɓɓuka da yawa daban-daban ba tare da daidaitawa ba.** Kwatancen VFM na kowace raka'a tsakanin zaɓuɓɓuka masu hidima ga yawan jama'a daban-daban dole ya sarrafa girma, in ba haka ba kwatancen inganci ba shi da ma'ana.
