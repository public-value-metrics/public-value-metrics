# Samfurin Dabaru na Shiri (Logic Model)

Samfurin dabaru zane ne mai jere-jere wanda ke haɗa abubuwan da aka sa (inputs), ayyuka, fitarwa (outputs), sakamako (outcomes), da tasiri (impact) na wani shiri. Ana karanta shi daga hagu zuwa dama a matsayin sarkar lissafin alhaki: albarkatu su shiga, ayyuka su gudana, a samar da fitarwa, sakamako ya sauya ga masu cin moriya, sannan tasiri ya taru a fage mafi faɗi ko cikin lokaci mafi tsawo. Wannan shi ne tsarin da masu bayar da tallafi da masu binciken kuɗi ke tsammanin a iya bayar da rahoton shiri a kansa, kuma shi ne abokin hamayyar [taswirar sauyi (theory of change)](../kaidar-sauyi/) da ake zana ta daga ƙarshe zuwa baya, shi kuma yana duban gaba.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Littafin Magenta na Ma'aikatar Kuɗi ta Birtaniya (HM Treasury) ya ayyana samfurin dabaru a matsayin sashen da ake buƙata a tsarin kimanta shiri, kuma masu bayar da tallafi kamar National Lottery Community Fund suna gina fom ɗin neman tallafi da na bayar da rahoto a kan wannan sarkar ginshiƙai biyar. Amfaninsa shi ne tilasta wa shiri ya bayyana a zane ɗaya abin da zai kashe, abin da zai yi da shi, abin da zai samar, kuma — wannan shi ne mafi muhimmanci — abin da ya kamata ya sauya sakamakon haka, da cikakken bayani wanda sakin layi na rubutu kan ɓoye. Samfurin dabaru wanda ginshiƙin abubuwan da aka sa da na ayyuka cike suke amma na sakamako babu komai ko maras haske, ana gane shi nan take, kuma shi ya sa masu bayar da tallafi ke buƙatarsa.

## Lissafi

Samfurin dabaru sarka ce ta tsari, ba dabara ta lissafi ba:

```
Inputs          Activities        Outputs             Outcomes              Impact
(resources      (what is done     (direct, countable   (change for           (long-term,
 committed)      with them)        products)            beneficiaries)        population-level
                                                                               or systemic change)
```

Ya kamata kowane ginshiƙi ya fi wanda ya gabace shi cikakken bayani: abubuwan da aka sa su ne abin da kuke kashewa, ayyuka su ne abin da kuke yi, fitarwa su ne abin da aka kai ko da kuwa ba su da wani tasiri, sakamako shi ne abin da ya sauya sakamakon haka — bambancin da aka yi bayani dalla-dalla a [sakamako da fitarwa](../sakamako-da-fitarwa-bambancin-outcomes-da-outputs/) — sannan tasiri shi ne sauyi mai dorewa na dogon lokaci, wanda sau da yawa wani ɓangare ne kawai ake iya danganta shi ga shirin.

## Misali aiki

**Ƙaramar hukuma (hidimar shawara kan basussuka ta intanet)**:

- Abubuwan da aka sa: kasafin kuɗi na shekara £180,000, masu ba da shawara 4.0 FTE (ma'aikata na cikakken lokaci), tsarin sarrafa shari'o'i.
- Ayyuka: zaman wayar da kai, ganawa ɗaya-da-ɗaya na ba da shawara kan basussuka.
- Fitarwa: an gudanar da ganawa 900; an bayar da tsare-tsaren basussuka da na tallafi 750.
- Sakamako: cikin abokan ciniki da suka kai ga bin diddigi na watanni 6, kashi 60% (450 cikin 750) sun bayar da rahoton raguwar basussukan da suka tara, matsakaicin raguwa £1,200 ga kowane abokin ciniki — jimillar raguwar basussuka £540,000.
- Tasiri: raguwar da za a iya aunawa a buƙatun neman matsuguni daga abokan cinikin hidimar cikin shekaru biyu, wadda wani ɓangare ne kawai za a iya danganta ta ga wannan hidima baya ga wasu matakai (duba [nazarin yanayin kwatance (counterfactual)](../nazarin-counterfactual/)).

**Ƙungiyar agaji (haɗin gwiwar tura mutane zuwa bankin abinci)**:

- Abubuwan da aka sa: £45,000, mai gudanarwa 1.5 FTE, yarjejeniyar haɗin gwiwa da hukumomin tura mutane 12.
- Ayyuka: tantance waɗanda aka tura, tattara da rarraba fakitin abinci.
- Fitarwa: an rarraba fakitin abinci 5,000 ga gidaje 1,100.
- Sakamako: kashi 68% na gidajen da aka bincika (748 cikin 1,100) sun bayar da rahoton ingantuwar wadatar abinci a kiran bin diddigi na makonni 4.
- Tasiri: gudummawa wajen rage buƙatar hidimomin gaggawa a yankin, wanda alƙaluman yankin gaba ɗaya ne kawai ke tabbatarwa, kuma ba za a iya danganta shi ga wannan ƙungiyar agaji ita kaɗai ba.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Samfurin dabaru kusan samfurin bayanai ne na zahiri na tsarin sakamako: abubuwan da aka sa da ayyuka bayanai ne na aiki da kuka riga kuka mallaka (kashe kuɗi, ma'aikata, rajistar zama); fitarwa suna da sauƙin aunawa saboda ana ƙidaya su a wurin kai hidima; sakamako yana buƙatar tattara bayanan bin diddigi da aka tsara da gangan (bincike, haɗa bayanan gudanarwa) wanda ba zai wanzu ba sai wani ya gina shi; tasiri yawanci yana buƙatar bayanan da aka haɗa, na dogon lokaci, ko na matakin al'umma gaba ɗaya fiye da tsarin kowane shiri. Ya kamata injiniyoyin da ke gina kayan aikin bayar da rahoto su matsa wa masu bayar da kwangila su ayyana alamomin sakamako da tasiri a lokacin tsara aiki, maimakon su koma ga allon bayanai na fitarwa kawai saboda abin da bayanan mu'amala suka goyi baya ke nan. Duba [ribar zamantakewa a kan jari (social return on investment)](../ribar-zamantakewa-daga-zuba-jari/) don hanyar da ke ƙididdige darajar ginshiƙan sakamako da tasiri musamman, da kuma [cika alfanun shiri (benefits realization)](../cimma-faidodi/) don bin diddigin ko an cimma ginshiƙin tasiri da gaske.

## Tarkuna

- **Tsayawa a fitarwa.** Allon bayanai da ke bayar da rahoton ganawar da aka yi ko fakitin da aka rarraba kuma yana nuna cewa an samu alfanu, yana bayar da rahoton ayyuka ne, ba sakamako ba — duba [sakamako da fitarwa](../sakamako-da-fitarwa-bambancin-outcomes-da-outputs/).
- **Rashin bayyana alaƙar sababi tsakanin ginshiƙai.** Samfurin dabaru yana bayyana sarkar amma ba dalilin da ya sa ayyuka za su haifar da fitarwa wadda za ta haifar da sakamako ba; wannan hujja tana cikin [taswirar sauyi (theory of change)](../kaidar-sauyi/), kuma samfurin dabaru da babu ita a bayansa ba a gwada shi ba.
- **Ɗaukarsa a matsayin takardar neman tallafi ta sau ɗaya.** Samfuran dabaru da aka yi kawai don biyan buƙatar neman tallafi kuma ba a sabunta su ba, suna daina nuna abin da shirin ke yi a zahiri.
- **Ƙaruwar danganta sakamako a ginshiƙin tasiri.** Da'awar cewa sauyi na matakin al'umma gaba ɗaya ya samo asali daga shiri ɗaya kaɗai, ba tare da yanayin kwatance (counterfactual) ba, yana ƙara girman abin da shaida ta goyi baya.
