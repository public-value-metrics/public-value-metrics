# Ƙimar Buɗaɗɗen Bayanai (Open Data Value)

Ƙimar buɗaɗɗen bayanai matsala ce ta ƙiyasta darajar bayanan gwamnati da na jama'a idan ba su da farashi: ba a sayar da su, don haka babu layin kuɗin shiga, amma fitar da su (bayanan yanayi, jadawalin sufuri, iyakokin lambar gidan waya, rajistar kamfanoni) a bayyane yana haifar da ayyukan tattalin arziki da na zamantakewa daga baya. Ƙididdige shi da kyau yana da muhimmanci domin "kyauta ne a fitar da shi" da "ba shi da daraja" duka kuskure ne, kuma injiniyan software da ke yanke shawarar buɗe API ko tsarin bayanai yana buƙatar hujja mafi kyau fiye da ɗayansu.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Ƙiyasin sama-zuwa-ƙasa da aka fi ambata ya fito ne daga rahoton McKinsey Global Institute na 2013 "Open data: Unlocking innovation and performance with liquid information", wanda ya sanya yiwuwar ƙimar buɗaɗɗen bayanai ta shekara a fannoni bakwai — ilimi, sufuri, kayayyakin masu amfani, wutar lantarki, mai da iskar gas, kiwon lafiya, da kuɗin masu amfani — a dala tiriliyan $3 zuwa tiriliyan $5 a shekara a duniya, ta hanyoyi da suka haɗa da ƙarin bayyana gaskiya, daidaita wadata da buƙata da inganci, da ba da damar sabbin kayayyaki da hidimomi da aka gina a kan bayanan. Wannan adadi ƙiyasin yanayi ne, ba sakamakon da aka auna ba, kuma ana yawan kuskuren ambatonsa kamar kuɗin shiga ne da gwamnati za ta iya ɗauka kai tsaye, alhali ƙimar tana tarawa ne yawanci ga wasu — kasuwanci, masu bincike, 'yan ƙasa — masu amfani da bayanan, wanda shi ne ainihin manufar buɗe su maimakon sayar da su. Open Data Institute na Birtaniya, wanda Sir Tim Berners-Lee da Sir Nigel Shadbolt suka kafa tare a 2012, ya gina tun daga lokacin tarin nazarin shari'o'i dalla-dalla, daga ƙasa zuwa sama — fanni bayan fanni, tsarin bayanai bayan tsarin bayanai — wanda ya fi amfani sosai ga takardar shaidar kasuwanci ta gaske fiye da babban adadin McKinsey, domin yana nuna hanyar ƙirƙirar ƙima, ba girman jimillarta kaɗai ba.

## Lissafi

Buɗaɗɗen bayanai ba su da farashin kasuwa, don haka hanyoyin ƙididdige ƙima suna maye gurbinsa; hanyoyi uku suna maimaituwa, kuma babu ɗayan da ya isa shi kaɗai:

```
1. Cost-avoided / replacement-cost method:
   value ≈ what users would have paid to produce or licence the
   equivalent data themselves — a lower bound, ignores value created
   by uses the original producer never anticipated

2. Market-analogue / downstream-activity method:
   value ≈ revenue or savings generated by businesses/services built
   on the data (e.g. satnav apps built on open mapping and traffic
   data) — captures real economic activity but is hard to attribute
   cleanly to the data release itself (see additionality-and-deadweight)

3. Contingent/stated-preference method:
   value ≈ what users say they would pay, or the time they say it
   saves them — see stated-preference-valuation for the general
   method and its biases

None of these produces a figure as clean as a market price; credible
open data business cases triangulate across two or more, and are
explicit about which mechanism is doing the work.
```

## Misali aiki

**Misalin fitar da bayanan taswira/adireshi na ƙasa** (hanyar aiki bisa nazarin shari'o'i irin na ODI, adadin na misali ne na girman abin da irin waɗannan nazarce-nazarce yawanci ke samu):

```
Cost-avoided estimate:
  Businesses that would otherwise licence equivalent address-matching
  data commercially, at an estimated average licence cost of £4,000/
  year, across an estimated 15,000 SMEs now using the free open dataset
  = 15,000 × £4,000 = £60,000,000/year in avoided licensing cost alone

Downstream-activity estimate (more speculative, needs a counterfactual):
  New delivery-routing and logistics products built on the open data
  that would not exist, or would be materially worse, without it —
  requires a comparison against the counterfactual of the data staying
  closed or commercially licensed (counterfactual-analysis), because
  some of that activity would happen anyway on paid data at a higher
  price, which is deadweight in the "value created by opening it" sense

A defensible business case reports the cost-avoided figure as the
solid lower bound, and treats the downstream-activity figure as an
upper-bound scenario, not a fact.
```

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Ga injiniyoyi, tambayar ƙimar buɗaɗɗen bayanai ta aiki yawanci ta fi ƙunci fiye da manyan adadin ƙasa: shin buɗe wannan API ko tsarin bayanai na musamman (maimakon barin shi a bayan yarjejeniyar abokin haɗin gwiwa) yana ƙara sake amfani da shi isa ya tabbatar da kuɗin da ake ci gaba da kashewa wajen rubuta bayani, bayar da juzu'i, da tallafa masa a matsayin hanyar mu'amala ta jama'a? Wannan kuɗin kulawa gaske ne kuma shi ne abokin tattalin arzikin gina-sau-ɗaya-a-sake-amfani-da-yawa na [gwamnati a matsayin dandamali (government-as-a-platform)](../gwamnati-a-matsayin-dandamali/) — batutuwan biyu 'yan uwan juna ne na kusa, ɗaya game da lambar da ababen more rayuwa da aka raba, ɗayan game da bayanan da aka raba. Ya kamata a duba kowace da'awar ƙimar buɗaɗɗen bayanai a kan [ƙarin tasiri da nauyin mataccen sakamako (additionality-and-deadweight)](../ƙarin-tasiri-da-deadweight/) kafin ta shiga takardar shaidar kasuwanci: ayyukan da da sun faru ko ta yaya, a kan bayanan da aka ba da lasisi na kasuwanci, ba ƙima ba ce da *buɗewar* ta ƙirƙira.

## Tarkuna

- **Ambaton adadin $3–5 tiriliyan na McKinsey a matsayin na Birtaniya kaɗai ko kason wannan tsarin bayanai**: ƙiyasin yanayi ne na duniya, na fannoni bakwai, daga 2013 — amfani da shi a matsayin mai ninkawa daidai ga tsarin bayanai na ƙasa guda yana ɓata ma'anar adadin.
- **Rashin yanayin kwatance (counterfactual)**: ɗaukar yabo ga duk ayyukan tattalin arziki da aka gina a kan buɗaɗɗen bayanai, ba tare da tambayar nawa ne da sun faru ko ta yaya a kan bayanan da aka biya ko aka ba da lasisi a farashi mai tsada ba (duba [ƙarin tasiri da nauyin mataccen sakamako (additionality-and-deadweight)](../ƙarin-tasiri-da-deadweight/) da [nazarin yanayin kwatance (counterfactual-analysis)](../nazarin-counterfactual/)).
- **Rikita kuɗin samarwa da ƙimar da aka ƙirƙira**: tsarin bayanai da tattara shi ya yi tsada ba lallai ne ya zama mai daraja a fitar ba, kuma mai arha ba lallai ne ya zama ƙarancin daraja ba — ƙima tana bin amfani na ƙasa, ba kuɗin sama ba.
- **Watsi da kuɗin kulawa mai ci gaba na "buɗewa"**: wallafa ɗan ƙaramin CSV sau ɗaya ba iri ɗaya yake da alƙawarin tafiyar da API buɗaɗɗe mai bayani, juzu'i, da tallafi ba — rashin ba na biyun isasshen kuɗi bayan sanarwar ƙaddamarwa hanya ce ta gazawa da aka saba.
