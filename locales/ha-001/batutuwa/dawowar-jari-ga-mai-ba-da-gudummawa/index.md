# Dawowar Jari ga Mai Ba da Gudummawa

Dawowar jari ga mai ba da gudummawa (donor return on investment) ita ce abin da fam ɗin wani takamaiman mai ba da gudummawa ke saya a sakamako a zahiri — ba ma'aunin gudanarwar ƙungiyar agaji ba, ba kuma dawowar ƙungiyar agajin kan jimillar kasafinta ba. Tana sake tsara ROI daga mahangar ƙungiyar (yaya muke gudanar da aiki da inganci) zuwa mahangar mai ba da gudummawa (me gudummawata ta ƙarshe ke canzawa), kuma ana ɗaukar lambobin biyu a matsayin abu ɗaya akai-akai, ba daidai ba.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

"ROI" na ƙungiyar agaji da kanta, a inda ake amfani da kalmar kwata-kwata, yawanci yana bayyana wani abu kamar [kuɗin kowane mai amfana](../kuɗin-kowane-mai-cin-gajiya/) ko [ma'aunin kuɗin gudanarwar ƙungiyar agaji](../rabon-kuɗin-gudanarwa-na-ƙungiyar-agaji/) — ma'aunin ingancin ƙungiya. ROI na mai ba da gudummawa tambaya ce dabam gabaɗaya: idan aka yi la'akari da cewa wannan ƙungiyar agaji tuni tana da wasu kuɗaɗen shiga, me kuɗin *wannan* mai ba da gudummawa ke ƙarawa a ƙarshe? Idan ƙungiyar agaji za ta isar da shirin iri ɗaya da ko ba tare da wata gudummawar £10,000 ba — saboda tana da isassun ajiya, ko saboda wani mai ba da kuɗi zai cike gibin — ROI na mai ba da gudummawar wannan kyauta yana kusan sifili, komai kyawun ma'aunin kuɗin gudanarwa na ƙungiyar ko kuɗin kowane sakamako.

Wannan ita ce tambayar ƙarin tasiri (additionality) da ke ƙarƙashin tantance [ƙimar kuɗi](../ƙimar-da-aka-samu-daidai-da-kuɗi/) a kashe kuɗin jama'a na Burtaniya da [ƙarin tasiri da tasirin da da ma zai faru](../ƙarin-tasiri-da-deadweight/) a kimanta shirye-shirye: ƙimar da aka ƙirƙira tana da yabo ga mai ba da kuɗi ne kawai gwargwadon yadda ba za ta faru ba ko da ba shi ba. Manyan dandamali na masu ba da gudummawa da ƙungiyoyin bayarwa masu tasiri (Giving What We Can, GiveWell) suna gina shawarwarinsu a bayyane bisa wannan bambanci, ba tare da tambayar "wannan ƙungiya ce mai kyau?" ba sai "shin wannan ƙungiyar tana da sararin ƙarin kuɗi da ba a cika ba domin kyautata ta zama ƙari."

## Lissafi

```
Donor ROI ≠ Charity's operating efficiency

Donor ROI  ≈  (Outcome achieved with the gift) − (Outcome that would have
               occurred without it, i.e. the counterfactual)
             ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
                              Size of the gift

Key inputs:
  - Room for more funding (is the charity funding-constrained at the margin?)
  - Funging (would another donor have filled the gap?)
  - Marginal cost-effectiveness at the specific funding level (costs often
    rise as an intervention scales past its easiest-to-reach population)
```

Duba [ingancin kuɗi na bayarwa mai tasiri](../ingancin-kuɗi-na-bayarwa-mai-tasiri/) don yadda GiveWell ke aiwatar da tambayar "sararin ƙarin kuɗi" (room for more funding), da kuma [nazarin yanayin da ba a samu shiga ba](../nazarin-counterfactual/) don hanyar gabaɗaya.

## Misali aiki

Mai ba da gudummawa yana zaɓi tsakanin kyaututtuka biyu na £5,000:

- **Ƙungiyar agaji C**: tana da cikakken kuɗin shirinta na asali tare da ajiya ta £2 million da jerin masu ba da kuɗi masu jira; £5,000 na ƙarshe zai iya shiga ajiya ko aiki mai ƙarancin fifiko. Sakamakon da mai ba da gudummawa ya ƙara da ƙiyasi: kaɗan ne — kuɗin bai bayyana yana canza abin da ke faruwa ba.
- **Ƙungiyar agaji D**: ƙaramin shiri mai goyon bayan shaida wanda ya bayyana a bainar jama'a cewa zai juya mutane 200 baya a kwata mai zuwa idan ba tare da ƙarin £50,000 ba, kuma ya tara £42,000 daga ciki. £5,000 na ƙarshe yana da yiwuwar sosai ya ba da kuɗin ƙarin isarwa na gaske — misali, ƙarin mutane 20 da aka yi wa hidima, a kuɗin kowane mai amfana da ƙungiyar ta bayyana na £250.

Girman kyauta iri ɗaya, mai ba da gudummawa ɗaya, ROI na mai ba da gudummawa daban gabaɗaya — ba don Ƙungiyar C ta fi muni ba (tana iya samun kyakkyawar lambar kuɗin-kowane-sakamako gabaɗaya) sai don gibin kuɗinta na ƙarshe ya riga ya cika.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Dandamali na masu ba da gudummawa da kayan shawarwarin bayarwa galibi suna nuna ma'aunan ingancin matakin ƙungiya kawai (ma'aunin kuɗin gudanarwa, kuɗin kowane mai amfana) saboda waɗannan ne ƙungiyoyin agaji ke wallafawa a rahotannin shekara kuma su ne mafi sauƙin ja cikin teburin kwatance. Wakilcin ROI na mai ba da gudummawa yadda ya kamata yana buƙatar wani bayani dabam, mai wuyar samu: gibin kuɗin ƙungiyar agaji na yanzu da ta bayyana ko "sararin ƙarin kuɗi," wanda ke canzawa a duk shekara kuma da wuya ya zama bayanin tsari. Dandamali da ke son tallafa wa tunanin ROI na mai ba da gudummawa na gaske suna buƙatar ko dai ciyarwa kai tsaye daga bayyana gibin kuɗi (kamar yadda GiveWell ke kula da hannu ga ƙungiyoyin agajin da yake ba da shawara) ko bayyananniyar sanarwa cewa teburin kwatance yana nuna ingancin ƙungiya, ba ƙarin tasirin mai ba da gudummawa ba. Duba [ma'aunin kuɗin gudanarwar ƙungiyar agaji](../rabon-kuɗin-gudanarwa-na-ƙungiyar-agaji/) don ma'aunin da ake yawan rikita ROI na mai ba da gudummawa da shi, ba daidai ba.

## Tarkuna

- **Rikita ingancin ƙungiyar agaji da ƙarin tasirin mai ba da gudummawa.** Ƙungiyar agaji mai kyakkyawan gudanarwa da ƙarancin kuɗin gudanarwa na iya samun ROI na mai ba da gudummawa na ƙarshe kusan sifili idan ba ta da ƙarancin kuɗi.
- **Watsi da funging.** Idan babban mai ba da kuɗi na hukuma zai cike gibin ko ta yaya, kyautar mai ba da gudummawa ɗaya tana ƙauratar da kuɗin wannan mai ba da kuɗi maimakon ƙara sabon isarwa.
- **Zaton ingancin kuɗi mai daidaito a girma.** Ana yi wa masu amfana mafi arha-a-isa hidima da farko; kuɗin ƙarshe na kowane sakamako yakan ƙaru yayin da shiri ke faɗaɗa, don haka ROI na fam na gaba ba ɗaya yake da ROI na matsakaicin fam ɗin da aka riga aka kashe ba.
- **Rashin bayyana gibin kuɗi.** Ƙungiyar agaji ko dandamali da ba ta iya cewa me £X na gaba zai ba da kuɗi ba ba za ta iya goyon bayan da'awar ROI na mai ba da gudummawa ta gaske ba, sai ta matsakaicin kuɗi.
