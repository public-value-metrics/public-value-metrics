# Ingancin Kuɗi na Bayarwa Mai Tasiri (Effective Altruism)

Tunanin ingancin kuɗi na bayarwa mai tasiri (effective altruism, EA) yana jera shiga tsakani na agaji bisa adadin alherin — galibi ana bayyana shi a matsayin rayuka da aka ceta, ko lafiya da aka samu, a kowane dala da aka kashe — kuma yana tura kuɗi zuwa duk wane shiga tsakani da ke sayen alheri mafi yawa a ƙarshe. GiveWell shi ne mafi tasiri a wannan fanni: yana wallafa ƙiyasin kuɗin-kowane-rai-da-aka-ceta da kuɗin-kowane-sakamako bayyanannu kuma da aka sabunta ga ƙaramin jerin "manyan ƙungiyoyin agaji," kuma yana ba masu bayarwa shawarar su bayar ga wacce a yanzu take da sararin ƙarin kuɗi (room for more funding) a mafi kyawun ƙima.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

GiveWell ya bayyana ingancin kuɗi a matsayin ma'aunin farko a hanyarsa da aka wallafa: yana neman shiga tsakani masu goyon bayan shaida, yana ƙiyasta ingancin kuɗinsu a ma'auni ɗaya, kuma yana jerawa a tsakanin dalilai da ba su da alaƙa gabaɗaya — gidan sauro (bednets) kan zazzaɓin cizon sauro, ƙarin bitamin A, canja wurin kuɗi, biyan kuɗin ƙarfafa allurar rigakafi — a kan wannan axis ɗaya. Wannan kai tsaye shigo da tunanin salon QALY/DALY ne daga tattalin arzikin lafiya zuwa agaji: kamar yadda tsarin lafiya ke tambaya "nawa QALYs a kowane fam a ƙarshe," GiveWell yana tambaya "nawa rayuka, ko shekarun rayuwa, a kowane dala a ƙarshe," kuma yana ɗaukar dalilai a matsayin masu maye gurbin juna da zarar an mayar da su zuwa wannan ma'auni na gama gari. Duba [nazarin ingancin kuɗi a gwamnati](../nazarin-ingancin-kuɗi-a-gwamnati/) don dan uwan wannan tsarin tunani a fannin gwamnati.

Adadin GiveWell da aka fi ambata ya shafi Against Malaria Foundation (AMF), wanda ke rarraba gidan sauro da aka yi wa maganin kwari. A misalin GiveWell da aka wallafa (daga bayanan kuɗi na 2020), kusan $4,500 sun ba da kuɗin isassun gidan sauro don kauce wa mutuwa ɗaya, bayan yin la'akari da rashin cikakken amfani da gidan sauron, mace-macen tushe ba tare da gidan sauro ba, da gyaran funging — yiwuwar cewa AMF zai karɓi wani ɓangare na wannan kuɗi daga wasu masu bayarwa ko ta yaya. GiveWell ya bayyana a fili cewa wannan adadi yana motsawa a tsawon lokaci da wurare yayin da yaɗuwar cizon sauro, kuɗin gidan sauro, da gibin kuɗi ke canzawa, kuma ana sa ran kuɗin ceton rai gabaɗaya zai ƙaru a tsawon lokaci yayin da ake fara ɗaukar mafi arha damar; misalin hanya ne da aka warware, ba kafaffen farashi ba.

## Lissafi

```
Cost-effectiveness = Cost of intervention / Units of good produced
                    (e.g. $ per life saved, $ per DALY averted, $ per QALY)

GiveWell's chain for a bednet programme, illustratively:
  $ per net purchased and delivered
    ÷ share of nets actually used
    ÷ people protected per net
    × baseline annual mortality without nets
    × reduction in mortality attributable to net use (from RCT evidence)
    × years of protection per net
    ÷ adjustment for funging (money displacing other donors' funding)
  = $ per life saved (net of counterfactual funding effects)
```

Wannan jerin yana da muhimmanci domin kowane mataki wuri ne da ƙiyasin ingancin kuɗi ke yawan kuskure — duba kura-kurai a ƙasa — kuma domin yana bayyana a fili cewa "kuɗin kowane rai da aka ceta" ba ɗanyen farashi ne da aka lura ba; ƙiyasin ƙirar ne da aka gina daga abubuwan shigarwa da dama masu rashin tabbas daban-daban.

## Misali aiki

Shiga tsakani biyu na hasashe, duka masu goyon bayan shaida, suna gasa kan £100,000 na ƙarshe iri ɗaya:

- **Gidan sauro (irin AMF)**: kusan $4,500 a kowane rai da aka ceta bisa misalin GiveWell da aka wallafa daga bayanan 2020, wato kusan rayuka 20 da aka ceta a kowane £100,000 gwargwadon farashin musaya da shekarar da aka yi amfani da su.
- **Shirin maganin tsutsotsi (deworming)**: ba shi da wata fa'idar mace-mace da ta dace kwata-kwata, amma akwai ƙwaƙƙwaran shaida na ƙarin kuɗin shiga na dogon lokaci daga maganin tsutsotsi a ƙuruciya; GiveWell yana ƙimanta shi da fannin ƙarin kuɗin shiga, ba rayuka da aka ceta ba, wanda ke sa a yi wuya a kwatanta shi kai tsaye da gidan sauro ba tare da ma'auni na bai ɗaya ba. GiveWell yana amfani da bayyananniyar tsarin "nauyin ɗabi'a" (moral weights) don mayar da duka biyun zuwa ma'auni ɗaya na cikin gida don jerawa.

Horon hanyar EA shi ne tilasta wannan kwatance ya fito fili maimakon ba da kuɗi ga duka biyun saboda duka biyun "suna da daɗin ji." Duba [dawowar jari ta zamantakewa](../ribar-zamantakewa-daga-zuba-jari/) don kwatankwacin wannan tilastawa da kamfanonin zamantakewa na Burtaniya da masu ba da umarni na gida ke amfani da shi, wanda ke yin tambaya iri ɗaya — wace ce mafi kyawun dawowa a kowane fam — a salon ƙimar kuɗi maimakon salon rayuka/DALYs.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Injiniyoyin da ke gina dandamali na masu ba da gudummawa, kayan daidaita tallafi, ko allon tasiri don masu ba da kuɗi masu alaƙa da EA (Open Philanthropy, GiveWell kansa, dandamali na bayarwa mai tasiri kamar Giving What We Can) suna buƙatar wakiltar ƙiyasin ingancin kuɗi a matsayin kewayo tare da zato da aka bayyana, ba lamba ɗaya ba — ƙirar da ke ƙarƙashinsu tana da abubuwan shigarwa masu ninkawa da dama marasa tabbas, kuma rage wannan zuwa lamba ɗaya a allo yana bayyana ƙarfin gwiwar da GiveWell da kansa ya bayyana ba daidai ba. Ba kowane ƙiyasi lambar sigar bisa ranar wallafa; GiveWell yana sake duba lambobinsa, wani lokaci sosai, yayin da sabuwar shaidar RCT ko bayanan gibin kuɗi ke zuwa, kuma dandamali da ke adana tsohuwar lamba yana zama ba daidai ba cikin shiru.

## Tarkuna

- **Ɗaukar ƙiyasin ingancin kuɗi a matsayin kafaffen farashi.** Sakamakon ƙira ne mai abubuwan shigarwa masu ninkawa da dama marasa tabbas (ƙimar amfani, mace-macen tushe, gyaran funging); bayyana kwanan wata da sigar.
- **Watsi da funging/ƙauratarwa.** Ba da kuɗi ga ƙungiyar da za ta karɓi kuɗin daga wani mai bayarwa ko ta yaya yana sayen ƙarancin alherin da ba zai faru ba (counterfactual) fiye da yadda kanun labari ke nunawa — duba [ƙarin tasiri da tasirin da da ma zai faru](../ƙarin-tasiri-da-deadweight/) da [ƙauratarwa da danganta tasiri](../ƙauratarwa-da-danganta-tasiri/).
- **Kwatanta ma'aunai da ba su dace ba ba tare da juyawa ba.** "Rayuka da aka ceta" da "kuɗin shiga da aka samu" ba za a iya kwatanta su kai tsaye ba sai da bayyananniyar tsarin nauyin ɗabi'a; gabatar da su gefe-da-gefe kamar suna iri ɗaya kuskuren rukuni ne.
- **Hangen ramin rukunin dalili.** Jerawa a cikin rukunin dalili ɗaya kawai (misali ƙungiyoyin lafiyar duniya kaɗai) da kiran wanda ya ci "ƙungiyar agaji mafi ingancin kuɗi" yana wuce gona da iri kan da'awar; jerin GiveWell na tsakanin dalilai da gangan ya taƙaita (lafiya da jin daɗin duniya), ba na kowa-da-kowa ba.
