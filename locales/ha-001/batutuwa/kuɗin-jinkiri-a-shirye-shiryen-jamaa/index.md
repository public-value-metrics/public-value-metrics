# Kuɗin Jinkiri a Shirye-shiryen Jama'a (CoD)

Kuɗin Jinkiri (Cost of Delay) shi ne ƙimar jama'a da ake asara a kowace naúrar lokaci da shiri, hidima, ko sauyin tsari *ba a isar da shi ba tukuna*. Shi ne ma'aunin gada na jagora na wannan rukunin maudu'ai: yana mayar da "fara aiki ya jinkirta watanni shida" zuwa fam a kowane mako, ko zuwa WELLBY a kowane mako, don a iya jayayya game da jinkiri da kuɗi iri ɗaya da shawarar kasuwancin kanta.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Ƙa'idar Reinertsen — "idan za ka ƙididdige abu ɗaya kawai, ƙididdige Kuɗin Jinkiri" — tana shiga gwamnati kusan ba tare da sauyi ba, domin shirye-shiryen jama'a suna fuskantar ta ba kamar sauran ba: ana amincewa da shawarwarin kasuwanci bisa rafin fa'ida da aka hasashe, amma rafin yana fara gudana ne a lokacin fara aiki kawai, kuma kowane mako na jinkiri mako ne na ƙimar da aka rasa wanda babu wanda yake sanya wa farashi a rajistar haɗari. Binciken maimaitawa na National Audit Office kan yaɗa Universal Credit (duba rahotannin "Rolling Out Universal Credit," <https://www.nao.org.uk/>) yana misalta tsarin: an bi diddigin jinkirin jadawali kuma an ruwaito shi, amma kuɗin fam-a-kowane-mako na *rashin* isar da tsarin da aka sake fasalta ga rukunin masu da'awa na gaba an ɗan faɗe shi a matsayin lamba babba, duk da cewa shi ne lambar da ya kamata ta motsa fifiko da mika zuwa sama. Ba tare da lambar CoD ba, shirin da ya jinkirta yana kama da matsalar jadawali ga hukumar isarwa; da ita, matsalar lalacewar ƙima ce ga jami'in lissafi.

## Lissafi

```
CoD = fa'idar kowace naúrar lokaci da aka rasa yayin da ba a isar ba   (£/mako ko WELLBY/mako)

Jimillar asarar jinkiri = CoD × tsawon jinkiri

Rafukan fa'ida da za a jimlace ga shirye-shiryen jama'a:
  tanadi mai sakin kuɗi    (rage zamba/kuskure, kuɗin wucin gadi da aka kauce wa)
+ ƙarfi marar kuɗi da aka 'yanta  (awannin ma'aikacin shari'a/jami'i × cikakken kuɗi)
+ fa'idar walwala        (WELLBY × £13,000/WELLBY, jagorar ƙari ta walwala ta
                             HMT Green Book, farashin 2019)
```

Ga hidimomin da ke fuskantar ɗan ƙasa, a ƙididdige a walwala da kuɗi duka — duba [shekarun rayuwa da aka daidaita bisa walwala](../shekarun-rayuwa-da-aka-daidaita-da-jin-daɗi/) don naúrar tushe, da [kuɗin damar da aka rasa a kashe kuɗin gwamnati](../kuɗin-damar-da-aka-rasa-a-kashe-kuɗin-jamaa/) don abin da fam ɗin da aka jinkirta zai iya ba da kuɗi a madadinsa.

## Misali aiki

**Karamar hukuma**: sabunta tsarin fa'idar gidaje yana rage kuskuren biyan fiye da kima da £150/da'awa/shekara a duk da'awoyi masu rai 20,000.

```
Fa'idar shekara = 150 × 20,000 = £3,000,000/shekara
CoD = 3,000,000 / 52 ≈ £57,700/mako
Jinkirin aiwatarwa na watanni 12 yana ci 52 × 57,700 ≈ £3,000,000 na kuskuren da za a iya kaucewa.
```

**Hukumar gwamnatin tsakiya**: hidimar tantance fa'idar naƙasa, da aka isar da ita watanni shida (makonni 26) a ƙarshen abin da aka tsara, tana nufin masu da'awa 200,000/shekara suna jira matsakaicin makonni uku fiye don yanke shawara. Ana ƙirƙirar kowane ƙarin mako na rashin tabbacin kuɗi a matsayin tasirin −0.0018 WELLBY (maki na gamsuwar rayuwa):

```
Asarar WELLBY ga kowane mai da'awa = 3 × 0.0018 = 0.0054
Asarar WELLBY ta shekara = 200,000 × 0.0054 = 1,080 WELLBY/shekara
CoD_walwala = 1,080 / 52 ≈ 20.8 WELLBY/mako
CoD_kuɗi = 20.8 × £13,000 ≈ £270,000/mako na ƙimar walwala
```

Jinkirin makonni 26 saboda haka "yana ci" kusan 540 WELLBY — wanda ya kai kusan fam miliyan 7 a ƙimar walwala ta Green Book — yana sake tsara ranar fara aiki da aka rasa a matsayin lamarin jin daɗin ɗan ƙasa, ba bayanin ƙasa na gudanar da aiki ba.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

CoD shi ne ke sa [ma'aunin DORA](../maaunin-dora-don-ƙimar-jamaa/) da [ma'aunin gudana](../maaunin-kwarara-a-isar-da-aikin-gwamnati/) su zama masu fahimta a kuɗi: lokacin jagoranci a bututun × CoD kuɗi ne (ko walwala) da ake ƙonewa a layuka kafin ya taɓa kai wa ɗan ƙasa. A taƙaice:

- **Fifiko**: a jera rumbun aiki bisa CoD ÷ tsawon lokaci maimakon bisa girman matsayin masu ruwa da tsaki — takwaran injiniyancin manhaja na buƙatar Green Book na tantance zaɓuɓɓuka bisa ƙima, ba bisa wanda ke tambaya ba.
- **Sayayya**: zagayen sayayyar tsari na watanni 12–18 yana da CoD; sanya masa farashi yana canza hujjar gaggawa ga hanyoyi masu sauri, kuma yana shiga kai-tsaye cikin shawarwarin [gina ko saya](../gina-ko-saya-a-gwamnati/) inda lokacin isa ga ƙima shi ne mai yanke shawara.
- **Shari'ar fa'ida**: kowace lambar CoD da aka ambata a amincewa ya kamata ta sake bayyana a [cimma fa'idodi](../cimma-faidodi/) — idan kuɗin jinkirin na gaske ne, ya kamata a iya auna fa'idar da aka gaggauta bayan fara aiki.

## Tarkuna

- **Zaton CoD mai layi**: wasu hidimomin jama'a suna da ƙima mai siffar ranar ƙarshe (ranar bin doka — CoD yana tsalle zuwa matakin haɗarin tilastawa bayan ranar, kusan sifili kafin) maimakon ƙimar mako-mako mai santsi. A rarraba bayanin gaggawa kafin a ninka.
- **CoD kan abubuwan da ba wanda ke buƙata**: jinkiri yana da kuɗi ne kawai idan abin da ba a isar ba yana da ƙima; tsarin da babu wanda zai yi amfani da shi yana da CoD sifili ko da yaya ya yi jinkiri.
- **Ƙidaya jinkiri da rangwame sau biyu**: [ƙimar rangwamen zamantakewa](../kason-rangwame-na-zamantakewa/) ta riga ta sanya wa lokaci farashi a tsawon lokacin tantancewa na shekaru da yawa; CoD shi ne sigar aiki ta cikin tsawon lokaci don makonni da watanni. A yi amfani da CoD ga jinkirin jadawali, da sauyin NPV ga sake tsara matakai na shekaru da yawa.
