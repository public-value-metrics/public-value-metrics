# Ma'aunin Gamsuwar Ɗan Ƙasa

Ma'aunin gamsuwar ɗan ƙasa yana auna yadda mutane suke kimanta ƙwarewarsu kai-tsaye ta hidimar jama'a — dabam da amincewa da cibiyoyi gabaɗaya, kuma dabam da ko hidimar ta cimma kyakkyawan sakamako da gaske. Hidima na iya zama abin so amma mara tasiri, ko mai tasiri amma ba a so; gibin tsakanin biyun shi kansa bayani ne na bincike da ya kamata ƙungiyar isarwa ta kula da shi.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Ana auna gamsuwa a matakai biyu daban-daban waɗanda ake haɗa su kullum. A matakin hidima, Performance Platform na Birtaniya da aka yi ritaya yanzu da littafin jagorar sabis na GOV.UK na yau suna buƙatar binciken gamsuwa ga kowace hidima (yawanci ma'auni mai maki biyar daga "gamsu sosai" zuwa "ba gamsu ba sosai", ana gudanar da shi a lokacin ma'amala) a matsayin ɗaya daga cikin KPI huɗu na wajibi na hidima — duba [ma'aunin hidima da ma'aunin ma'amala](../ƙaidodin-hidima-da-maaunan-muamala/). A matakin cibiya, Civil Service People Survey na Birtaniya yana auna sa hannu da ƙwarewar ma'aikata a kowane sashen gwamnatin tsakiya a kowace shekara, kuma dabam shirin "Trust in Government" na OECD yana bincikar amincewar jama'a ga gwamnatin ƙasa a ƙasashe mambobi, yana bin diddigin tsarin faɗuwa da murmurewa na dogon lokaci wanda rikice-rikice suka tsara sosai (rikicin kuɗi na 2008 da annobar COVID-19 duka sun haifar da kaifi, motsi bayyananne a lambobin amincewa na OECD). Dalilin da ya sa injiniyoyin da ke gina hidimomi masu fuskantar ɗan ƙasa suke buƙatar riƙe gamsuwa da sakamako dabam shi ne sanannen hanyar gazawa a ƙirar hidima: fom na dijital mai kyakkyawar ƙira, mai sauƙin amfani don neman fa'ida na iya samun maki mai girma sosai na gamsuwa yayin da manufar da ke ƙasa — ƙa'idodin cancanta, tarin ayyukan da ba a sarrafa ba, adadin kyaututtuka — ba ta inganta halin mai neman ba. Gamsuwa tana auna ƙirar mu'amala; ba ta auna ƙimar da aka isar a bayanta.

## Lissafi

```
Gamsuwa ta ainihi = % masu gamsuwa (ko gamsuwa sosai) − % marasa gamsuwa (ko marasa gamsuwa sosai)
                    (an cire amsoshin tsaka-tsaki/marasa ra'ayi daga sharuɗɗan biyu, amma an ƙidaya
                    su a tushen amsoshi don lissafin kowane kashi)

Gibin gamsuwa-da-sakamako = maki na gamsuwa − maki na cimma sakamako
                    (duka an daidaita su 0–100; babban gibi mai kyau yana nuna hidimar
                    da "take da daɗi" amma ba ta isar da ainihin abu ba)

Ma'aunin amincewa (irin na OECD) = % na masu amsa binciken da suka amsa "e" ga
                    "kuna da kwarin gwiwa a [gwamnatin ƙasa]?"
                    ana bin diddiginsa a matsayin jerin lokaci, yawanci an raba shi bisa
                    shekaru, kuɗin shiga, da ilimi
```

## Misali aiki

**Hidimar e-billing ta harajin majalisa na karamar hukuma**: binciken gamsuwa a lokacin nasarar ma'amala yana nuna masu amsa 2,400: 1,650 gamsu/gamsu sosai, 250 ba gamsu/ba gamsu sosai ba, 500 tsaka-tsaki.

```
Gamsuwa ta ainihi = (1,650/2,400 × 100) − (250/2,400 × 100)
                  = 68.75% − 10.42%
                  = +58.3 gamsuwa ta ainihi
```

Wannan yana kama da ƙarfi shi kaɗai. Amma ana nuna binciken ne ga masu amfani da *suka kammala* ma'amalar cikin nasara kawai — son zuciya na auna da aka sani (duba tarkuna a ƙasa). Haɗa shi da ma'aunin yawan kammalawa daga [ma'aunin hidima da ma'aunin ma'amala](../ƙaidodin-hidima-da-maaunan-muamala/) yana nuna cewa kammalawa 71% ce kawai, ma'ana:

```
Ainihin gamsuwar jama'a ba a auna ta ba ga 29% da suka bar tafiyar —
mai yiwuwa su ne rukunin da suka fi rashin gamsuwa, domin barin shi kansa
babban alamar mara kyau ne da binciken bai taɓa kamawa ba.
```

**Misali na matakin ƙasa (tsarin jerin amincewa irin na OECD)**: amincewar gwamnatin ƙasa da aka ruwaito 42% a shekara ta 1, tana faɗuwa zuwa 34% a shekara ta 2 (shekarar rikici) kuma tana murmurewa zuwa 39% a shekara ta 3 — hanya ta musamman ta tsarin girgiza-da-murmurewa-a-ɓangare da OECD ta rubuta a ƙasashe mambobi bayan manyan rikice-rikice.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

A sanya binciken gamsuwa a kowane muhimmin wurin fita na tafiyar mai amfani, ba a nasarar kammalawa kaɗai ba — kuskuren injiniya mafi yawa a wannan fanni, wanda a ɓoye yake mayar da ma'aunin gamsuwa zuwa ma'aunin banza mai son zuciya na rayayyu kawai (survivorship bias). Duk inda zai yiwu, a haɗa maki na gamsuwa da ma'aunin kammalawa ko sakamako a allon sa ido ɗaya don kada ƙungiya ta yi murna da gamsuwa mai tashi yayin da kammalawa ke faɗuwa a ɓoye (duba [kuɗin kowace ma'amala](../kuɗin-kowace-maamala/) da [haɗa kowa da dijital](../haɗa-kowa-da-dijital/) don waɗanda ake cirewa daga samfurin gamsuwar dijital tun farko — masu amfani da ba na dijital ba da masu taimakon dijital ba su da wakilci a binciken cikin hidima). Bayanan gamsuwa da amincewa kuma suna shiga kai-tsaye cikin ƙafar halasci ta [triangle na dabara na Moore](../ƙimar-jamaa/), kuma suna cikin ra'ayoyin "abokin ciniki" da "halasci" na [katin maki na ƙimar jama'a](../katin-kimanta-ƙimar-jamaa/) — duba [ma'aunin amincewa da halasci](../maaunin-amana-da-halaccin-mulki/) don takwaran matakin cibiya na wannan ma'auni na matakin hidima.

## Tarkuna

- **Son zuciya na rayayyu a binciken lokacin kammalawa**: masu amfani da suka bar tafiya ba sa taɓa ganin binciken, don haka babban maki na gamsuwa a cikin hidima na iya wanzuwa tare da ƙarancin yawan kammalawa da babban rukunin da ba a gani na marasa gamsuwa waɗanda ba su kammala ba.
- **Ɗaukar gamsuwa a matsayin wakilin sakamako**: ingantaccen ƙirar mu'amala ga manufa mara kyau tana samun maki mai kyau a gamsuwa da mara kyau a sakamako — koyaushe a bayar da rahoton duka biyu, kada ɗaya ya tsaya maimakon ɗayan.
- **Ƙananan samfura marasa wakilci da aka ruwaito da daidaito na ƙarya**: maki na gamsuwa daga ƴan ɗaruruwan masu amsa da suka zaɓi kansu da aka ruwaito zuwa wuri ɗaya na ƙima yana nuna kwarin gwiwa da girman samfurin ba zai iya tallafawa ba.
- **Yin watsi da rarraba bisa ɗabi'un jama'a**: lambobin amincewa da gamsuwa na ƙasa da ba a raba su bisa shekaru, kuɗin shiga, naƙasa, ko samun dijital na iya ɓoye ƙwarewa mabambanta sosai tsakanin rukunoni — tsarin da bayanan Trust in Government na OECD kansu suke rarrabawa a fili.
