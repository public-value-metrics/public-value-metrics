# Metrik Nilai Publik

Pengantar lengkap babagan petungan, conto, lan nalar nilai publik, ditulis kanggo para insinyur piranti lunak sing nggawé solusi kanggo organisasi pamaréntah lan sektor sosial ing saindhenging donya. Saben berkas mbahas siji metrik utawa konsep: définisi, kenapa penting, petungan, conto petungan, gegayutan karo rekayasa piranti lunak, jebakan, lan sumber.

Anyar ing kéné? Wiwiti karo [nilai publik](locales/jv-001/topik/nilai-publik/), [value for money](locales/jv-001/topik/value-for-money-ngukur-apa-belanja-publik-menehi-imbangan-biaya-lan-manfaat-sing-paling-apik/), lan [teori owah-owahan](locales/jv-001/topik/teori-owah-owahan-peta-sebab-akibat-saka-tujuan-dawa-mundur-menyang-kegiatan/) — telung gagasan sing dadi dhasar kabèh liyané.

## Dhasar nilai publik

- [Nilai Publik (Public Value)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/public-value/) — Segitiga strategis Mark Moore: legitimasi, misi, lan kapasitas operasional minangka ujian regané nilai publik
- [Value for Money (VFM): Ngukur Apa Belanja Publik Menehi Imbangan Biaya lan Manfaat Sing Paling Apik](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/value-for-money/) — Economy, efficiency, effectiveness — lan E kaping papat sing dipasulayaké, equity
- [Biaya Kesempatan ing Belanja Publik (Opportunity Cost in Public Spending)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/opportunity-cost-in-public-spending/) — Saben pound sing dibelanjakaké departemen iku pound sing ora dibelanjakaké kanggo program paling apik sabanjuré
- [Tingkat Diskonto Sosial: Ngowahi Biaya lan Manfaat Mangsa Ngarep dadi Nilai Saiki](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/social-discount-rate/) — Tingkat diskonto Green Book sing saya mudhun, lan kenapa 3,5% dadi pitakonan politik sing urip
- [Pembobotan Distribusional](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/distributional-weighting/) — Kenapa sapoundhi luwih abot tumrap kulawarga miskin — lan kepriyé Green Book ngregani iku
- [Additionality lan Deadweight: Nyingkirake Asil sing Bakal Kedadéan Senajan Tanpa Program](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/additionality-and-deadweight/) — Apa iku bakal kedadéan senajan tanpa program? Pitakonan sing ngempesaké umumé klaim dampak
- [Pamindhahan lan Atribusi](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/displacement-and-attribution/) — Nalika kemenanganmu iku kerugian wong liya, lan nalika ora kabèh bisa kok klaim
- [Analisis Kontrafaktual: Ngira-ira Apa sing Bakal Kedadéan Tanpa Intervensi](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/counterfactual-analysis/) — Ora ana kontrafaktual, ora ana bukti — nyusun donya tanpa intervensi

## Appraisal ékonomi pamaréntah

- [Appraisal Green Book (Model Lima Kasus)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/green-book-appraisal/) — Model lima kasus HM Treasury: kasus strategis, ékonomi, komersial, keuangan, lan manajemen
- [Analisis Biaya-Manfaat Sosial (Social Cost-Benefit Analysis, SCBA)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/social-cost-benefit-analysis/) — Ngowahi kabèh dadi moneter, kalebu sing ora tau dirègani pasar
- [Analisis Efektivitas Biaya ing Pamaréntahan: Mbandhingaké Cara Nggayuh Outcome sing Padha](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cost-effectiveness-analysis-in-government/) — Biaya saben satuan outcome, nalika outcome ora bisa diowahi kanthi jujur dadi dhuwit
- [Analisis Keputusan Multikriteria (Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis, MCDA)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/multi-criteria-decision-analysis/) — Ngskor opsi mungsuh kritéria sing dibobot nalika metrik tunggal bakal nyingkepaké trade-off
- [Penilaian Kesejahteraan (Wellbeing Valuation): Ngregani Kabijakan Langsung ing Satuan Kepuasan Urip](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/wellbeing-valuation/) — WELLBY lan penyesuaian kesejahteraan: ngregani kabijakan ing satuan kepuasan urip
- [Penilaian Preferensi sing Dinyatakaké (Stated Preference Valuation): Nakokaké Wong Sepira Gelem Mbayar](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/stated-preference-valuation/) — Nakokaké wong sepira gelem mbayar — contingent valuation lan masalah kredibilitasé
- [Valuasi Preferensi sing Kasingkap (Revealed Preference Valuation)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/revealed-preference-valuation/) — Nyimpulaké nilai saka apa sing bener-bener ditindakaké wong, saka hedonic pricing nganti metode biaya lelungan
- [Shadow Pricing (Rega Bayangan): Ngregani Barang Sing Ora Duwé Rega Pasar](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/shadow-pricing/) — Ngregani apa sing ora duwé rega pasar: karbon, wektu, lan barang sing ora didagangaké

## Nilai sosial lan pangukuran dampak

- [Model Logika (Logic Model) kanggo Program Layanan Publik](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/logic-model/) — Input, kegiatan, output, outcome, dampak — balung akuntabilitas sawijining program
- [Outcome vs Output: Bedane Asil Langsung lan Owah-owahan Nyata](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/outcomes-vs-outputs/) — Kenapa "500 wong rawuh" dudu klaim sing padha karo "urip 500 wong dadi luwih apik"

- [Metode Evaluasi Dampak](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/impact-evaluation-methods/) — RCT, difference-in-differences, lan propensity score matching kanggo kabijakan sing ora bisa dilakokaké ing laboratorium
- [Evaluasi Dampak lan Evaluasi Proses](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/impact-evaluation-vs-process-evaluation/) — Apa iku kasil, lawan apa iku kedadéan miturut rancangan — rong pitakonan sing béda, rong metode sing béda

- [Produktivitas Layanan Publik (Public Service Productivity)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/public-service-productivity/) — Ngukur pertumbuhan output ing layanan tanpa rega pasar, saka metodologi ONS lan sakbanjuré
