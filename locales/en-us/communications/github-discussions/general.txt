Say hello and tell us how you use Public Value Metrics

Welcome to the Public Value Metrics Discussions. This is the place for introductions, opinions, and open conversation about the book.

I am a maintainer of the project. I wrote Public Value Metrics because I wanted an introduction to public value math, examples, and reasoning, written for software engineers building for governmental and social sector organizations worldwide to be easy to find and use. I would like to hear who is reading it and why.

**One question to start:** Which public value metric or concept would you like to see explained with a worked example?

A sentence or two is plenty. Replies to other people are welcome too.
