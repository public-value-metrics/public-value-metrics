# Blendit Value

Blendit value is Jed Emerson's proposition that aw organisational an investment activity creates value at aince alang three lines — economic, social, an environmental — an that thir arenae three separate returns tae be tradit aff against ane anither but a single, inseparable value proposition. There's nae sic thing, in Emerson's framin, as a purely financial return or a purely social ane; ilka pound spent, investit, or grantit produces some blend o aw three.

## Why it maitters

Emerson laid oot the idea in "The Blended Value Proposition: Integrating Social and Financial Returns" (California Management Review, 2003), scrievit straucht against the twentieth-century custom o sortin capital intae twa silos — philanthropy, that wis expectit tae produce social return an forgie financial return athegither, an investment, that wis expectit tae produce financial return an treat social or environmental effects as an externality. Emerson's argument wis that this sortin wis aye a fiction: a grant that funds a puirly run organization destroys value alang aw three lines, an a profitable business that fouls a river destroys value alang aw three lines an aw, whitiver its shareholders' return looks like on paper.

The idea runs alangside John Elkington's "triple bottom line" (people, planet, profit), coinit in 1994 an developit in his 1997 beuk "Cannibals with Forks," that pushed corporations tae report against social an environmental performance alangside financial performance. Blendit value pushed the same logic further intae capital allocation itsel, an it is the intellectual forebear o the impact investin field: the Global Impact Investing Network (GIIN), foundit in 2009, exists specifically tae big the infrastructure — includin the IRIS+ metrics catalogue, see [grant outcomes reporting](../grant-outcomes-reporting/) — that lets an investor actually meisure the blend raither than assert it.

## The maths

Blendit value is a framework, no a formula, an Emerson explicitly warnit against reducin it tae simple addition o three scores. The structure it proposes:

```
Ilka unit o capital deployit (grant, investment, contract, purchase) produces:
  - an economic effect      (financial return, cost saved, revenue generatit)
  - a social effect         (wellbein, capability, equity chynge for fowk)
  - an environmental effect (naitural capital protectit, degradit, or restorit)

Thir arenae separately optimizit an syne summit. A decision that maximizes
the economic line while destroyin the social line isnae "blendit value plus
a cost" — it is a net value-destroyin decision, an that's that.
```

In practice, organizations approximate the blend wi a scorecard: namit indicators against ilka line, reportit thegither, no nettit intae ae nummer. This is the same instinct ahint [social return on investment](../social-return-on-investment/) (that does attempt monetized nettin) an [natural capital accounting](../natural-capital-accoontin/) (that does the same for the environmental line) — baith are pairtial, single-line answers tae the question blendit value poses in fu.

## Wrocht example

An impact investor is choosin atween twa £500,000 loans:

- **Loan A**: tae a social enterprise runnin a job-trainin café for ex-offenders, at 2% interest (unner the 6% market rate for equivalent-risk lendin), expectit tae place 40 fowk/year intae sustained wark.
- **Loan B**: a market-rate loan at 6% tae a conventional retail business wi nae stated social or environmental objective.

A purely financial lens prefers B (6% > 2%). A blendit value lens asks the investor tae state aw three lines for baith:

| | Economic (annual) | Social (annual) | Environmental |
|---|---|---|---|
| Loan A | £10,000 interest | 40 fowk placed intae wark; ilka place-year cairries a plausible avoidit-reoffendin saving tae the state, drawn frae Ministry of Justice reoffendin cost analyses | Neutral |
| Loan B | £30,000 interest | Nane stated | Neutral |

Loan A's blendit return plainly dominates aince the social line is priced, even though its financial line alane loses tae Loan B by £20,000 a year. Blendit value disnae tell the investor tae ignore the £20,000 gap — it tells them no tae pretend it's the anely nummer that exists.

## Link tae saftware ingineerin

Reportin or portfolio-management software for foundations, impact funds, or local authority commissionin teams is aften biggit wi a financial ledger as the primary data model an social or environmental fields bolted on as free-text notes. Blendit value implies the opposite design: three first-class, equally structurit value streams attachit tae ilka transaction or grant record, ilk wi its ain unit, source, an confidence level, displayit thegither raither than nettit intae a single misleadinly precise score. See [social return on investment](../social-return-on-investment/) an [public value scorecard](../public-value-scorecard/) for twa structurit weys tae big that display athoot collapsin the blend.

## Pitfaws

- **Summin the three lines intae ae nummer.** Emerson's ain writin warns against this; a single blendit figure hides whilk line is actually daein the wark an invites cherry-pickin.
- **Blend-washin.** Assertin a strang social or environmental line wi nae namit indicator or meisurement method, tae justify a unner-market financial return that wad itherwise look like underperformance.
- **Ignorin negative blends.** A financially successfu programme can hae a negative social or environmental line; blendit value requires reportin bad news on onie line, no juist guid news on ane.
