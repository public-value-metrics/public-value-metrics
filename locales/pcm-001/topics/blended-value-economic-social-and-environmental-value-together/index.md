# Blended Value: Economic, Social and Environmental Value Together

Blended value na Jed Emerson proposition say all organisational and investment activity dey create value for three lines at di same time — economic, social, and environmental — and say dis three no be separate returns wey you go trade against each other, but one single value proposition wey no fit separate. For Emerson way of seeing am, nothing like purely financial return or purely social return dey; every pound wey dem spend, invest, or grant dey produce some blend of all three.

## Wetin make am matter

Emerson set out di idea for "The Blended Value Proposition: Integrating Social and Financial Returns" (California Management Review, 2003), wey dem write directly against di twentieth-century convention to sort capital into two silos — philanthropy, wey dem expect make e produce social return and forgive financial return completely, and investment, wey dem expect make e produce financial return and treat social or environmental effects as externality. Emerson argument be say dis sorting na always fiction: grant wey fund badly run organization destroy value for all three lines, and profitable business wey pollute river destroy value for all three lines too, no matter how di return of im shareholders look for paper.

Di idea dey run parallel to John Elkington "triple bottom line" (people, planet, profit), wey he coin for 1994 and develop for im 1997 book "Cannibals with Forks," wey push corporations to report against social and environmental performance beside financial performance. Blended value push di same logic further into capital allocation itself, and na di intellectual ancestor of di impact investing field: di Global Impact Investing Network (GIIN), wey dem found for 2009, exist specifically to build di infrastructure — including di IRIS+ metrics catalogue, see [grant outcomes reporting](../grant-outcomes-reporting-how-grantees-dey-report-standard-outcomes-to-funders/) — wey let investor really measure di blend instead of to just assert am.

## Di maths

Blended value na framework, no be formula, and Emerson clearly warn against to reduce am to simple addition of three scores. Di structure wey e propose:

```
Every unit of capital wey dem deploy (grant, investment, contract, purchase) produce:
  - an economic effect      (financial return, cost saved, revenue generated)
  - a social effect         (wellbeing, capability, equity change for people)
  - an environmental effect (natural capital protected, degraded, or restored)

Dem no optimize dem separately den add dem together. Decision wey maximize
di economic line while e dey destroy di social line no be "blended value plus
a cost" — na net value-destroying decision, full stop.
```

For practice, organizations dey approximate di blend with scorecard: named indicators against each line, wey dem report together, no be to net dem into one number. Na di same instinct dey behind [social return on investment](../social-return-on-investment-how-to-show-social-value-against-money-wey-dem-spend/) (wey try to net things after dem monetize dem) and [natural capital accounting](../natural-capital-accounting-how-to-put-value-on-di-environment/) (wey do di same for di environmental line) — both of dem na partial, single-line answers to di question wey blended value pose in full.

## Example wey dem work out

One impact investor dey choose between two £500,000 loans:

- **Loan A**: to one social enterprise wey dey run job-training café for ex-offenders, at 2% interest (below di 6% market rate for lending wey get equivalent risk), wey dem expect go place 40 people/year into sustained work.
- **Loan B**: market-rate loan at 6% to one conventional retail business wey no get any stated social or environmental objective.

Purely financial lens prefer B (6% > 2%). Blended value lens ask di investor to state all three lines for both:

| | Economic (annual) | Social (annual) | Environmental |
|---|---|---|---|
| Loan A | £10,000 interest | 40 people place into work; each place-year carry plausible avoided-reoffending saving to di state, wey dem draw from Ministry of Justice reoffending cost analyses | Neutral |
| Loan B | £30,000 interest | None stated | Neutral |

Loan A blended return plainly dominate once dem price di social line, even though im financial line alone lose to Loan B by £20,000 a year. Blended value no tell di investor to ignore di £20,000 gap — e tell dem make dem no pretend say na only number wey exist.

## How e connect to software engineering

Dem often build reporting or portfolio-management software for foundations, impact funds, or local authority commissioning teams with financial ledger as di primary data model and social or environmental fields bolted on as free-text notes. Blended value imply di opposite design: three first-class, equally structured value streams wey attach to every transaction or grant record, each one get im own unit, source, and confidence level, wey dem display together instead of to net dem into one misleadingly precise score. See [social return on investment](../social-return-on-investment-how-to-show-social-value-against-money-wey-dem-spend/) and [public value scorecard](../public-value-scorecard-how-to-report-performance-across-many-dimensions-without-to-hide-trade-offs/) for two structured ways to build dat display without to collapse di blend.

## Wahala dem wey you suppose avoid

- **To sum di three lines into one number.** Emerson own writing warn against dis; one single blended figure dey hide which line really dey do di work and e dey invite cherry-picking.
- **Blend-washing.** To assert strong social or environmental line without named indicator or measurement method, just to justify below-market financial return wey for otherwise look like underperformance.
- **To ignore negative blends.** Programme wey succeed financially fit get negative social or environmental line; blended value require make dem report bad news for any line, no be only good news for one.
