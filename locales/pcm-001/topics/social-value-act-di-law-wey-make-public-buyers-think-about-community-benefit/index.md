# Social Value Act: Di Law Wey Make Public Buyers Think About Community Benefit

Di Public Services (Social Value) Act 2012 na UK statutory duty wey require public authorities for England and Wales to consider how wetin dem wan procure fit improve di economic, social and environmental wellbeing of di relevant area, and to consider consulting on dis one, before dem start procurement process for public services contracts. E enter force for January 2013 as light-touch "have regard to" duty, and Procurement Policy Note (PPN) 06/20 for January 2021 strengthen am well well — e require say central government contracts must clearly evaluate social value, no be just consider am, with minimum weighting for di award criteria.

## Wetin make am matter

Before PPN 06/20, commissioner fit satisfy "considering" social value by just note say dem don think about am, without any requirement say e must affect di award decision — duty wey easy to discharge for paper and ignore for practice. PPN 06/20 close dat gap for central government procurement: e order say dem must score social value as part of tender evaluation, organized around five national priority themes — COVID-19 recovery, tackling economic inequality, fighting climate change, equal opportunity, and wellbeing — and dem commonly measure am with di National TOMs (Themes, Outcomes, Measures) framework wey Social Value Portal dey maintain. If you be software engineer wey dey build procurement, contract management or bid-support tools for public sector, dis na di legal basis wey your client must build against, no be optional nice-to-have.

## Di maths

Social value na topic wey get framework shape; im "maths" na di scoring structure wey most authorities dey use:

```
Total tender score = Price/cost weighting + Quality weighting + Social value weighting

PPN 06/20 (central government): social value weighting ≥ 10% of total score

Social value themes (PPN 06/20):
 1. COVID-19 recovery
 2. Tackling economic inequality
 3. Fighting climate change
 4. Equal opportunity
 5. Wellbeing
```

Bidders dey usually monetize their commitments against dis themes with [unit cost databases](../unit-cost-databases-library-of-ready-made-money-values-for-social-outcomes/), and di same monetization logic wey dem use for [social return on investment](../social-return-on-investment-how-to-show-social-value-against-money-wey-dem-spend/) dey apply: commitment suppose get evidence, e suppose attributable to di contract, and dem no suppose double-count am against other funding.

## Example wey dem work out

**Local authority IT contract**: dem score £2 million, 3-year contract as 60% quality, 30% price, 10% social value. Bidder A commit to 2 apprenticeships, £150,000 of local subcontracting spend, and 200 hours of pro bono digital-skills training for local school, wey dem monetize with proxies from unit cost database at combined £90,000 of additional social value. Bidder B commit to smaller package wey dem monetize at £40,000. If di authority score social value proportionally against di strongest bid, Bidder A go receive full 10 points; Bidder B go receive 10 × (£40,000 ÷ £90,000) = 4.4 points — 5.6-point gap wey fit decide di contract even when quality and price near each other.

**Voluntary sector bidder**: small VCSE (voluntary, community and social enterprise) wey dey bid for grounds maintenance contract against commercial competitor no fit compete for unit price alone, but e use Global Value Exchange proxies to monetize im existing community employment and volunteering commitments, so dat e make social value case wey get evidence and worth to score beside price and quality.

## How e connect to software engineering

To win bid with monetized social value commitments create obligation to show evidence say you deliver against dem through contract management — tools wey dey log apprenticeship starts, local spend and training hours against di specific commitments wey dem score for tender, and wey dey feed contract review meetings instead of make dem forget dem once dem sign di contract. G-Cloud and Digital Marketplace listings dey more and more require social value statements for di point of listing. See [social return on investment](../social-return-on-investment-how-to-show-social-value-against-money-wey-dem-spend/) for di valuation method wey dey behind di commitments, [unit cost databases](../unit-cost-databases-library-of-ready-made-money-values-for-social-outcomes/) for di proxies wey bidders dey draw from, and [outcomes vs outputs](../outcomes-and-outputs-di-difference-between-wetin-you-do-and-wetin-e-change/) to make sure say di commitments wey dem deliver na outcomes, no be just activity counts.

## Wahala dem wey you suppose avoid

- **Social-washing bids.** Vague commitments ("we support di local community") wey no fit measure or hold dem to during contract management dey score well but dem no deliver anything wey dem fit verify.
- **To treat social value as tie-breaker.** PPN 06/20 require say dem must evaluate social value clearly inside di award criteria, no be to use am informally to break tie between bids wey equal.
- **No follow-through for contract management.** Commitments wey dem score for tender dey often never get tracking during delivery — see [benefits realization](../benefits-realization-to-show-say-di-promised-benefits-really-happen/).
- **Measurement frameworks wey no consistent across contracts.** If you use different proxy sources for similar commitments for different contracts, portfolio-level comparison no go mean anything, and na why common frameworks like National TOMs and shared unit cost databases dey exist.
